## 1. Explain Like I'm 12 (Core Idea)
Write the idea in the simplest way possible.  
Avoid jargon unless you immediately explain it.  
Imagine you're teaching a smart child.

---

## 2. Identify Gaps in Understanding
- Write questions you *cannot* fully answer yet.
- Write confusing steps, definitions, exceptions.
- Include anything that doesn't feel intuitive.

---

## 3. Fill the Gaps (Refine the Explanation)
Rewrite the idea more clearly **after reviewing** the weak areas above.  
Add small examples or analogies if needed.

---

## 4. Explain Again, Even Simpler
This should be the cleanest, shortest version.  
If you can explain it here without hesitation → you truly understand it.

---
