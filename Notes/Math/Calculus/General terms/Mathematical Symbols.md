## Definition
A mathematical symbol is one or more figures that is used to represent a mathematical object, an action on math objects, a relation between math objects, or for structuring the other symbols in a mathematical expression.

## Logical Symbols
### Implication
#### Definition
$T_1$ is implying $T_2$ ,if the correctness of $T_2$ stems from the correctness of $T_1$. We will say that claim $T_1 \Rightarrow T_2$ 
#### Example
$x>1$ is implying $x^2>1$ and $x^3>1$ so denote:
$x>1 \Rightarrow x^2>1$
$x>1 \Rightarrow x^3>1$

### If and only if
#### Definition
$T_1$ and $T_2$ are the same if $T_1$ implies $T_2$ and the other way around.
