## Sign 
$$
\begin{array}{l}
^c /' \\
A^c
\end{array}
$$
## Definition
Given a set, the complement to that set is composed of every non [[Element Of]] the set.
## Latex
^c /'
## Example
$$
\begin{array}{l}A = \{1,2,3\}, B=\{2\} \subset A\\
B^c=\{1,3\}
\end{array}
$$
