## Sign 
$$
\Rightarrow 
$$

## Definition

Implication means that if claim $T_2$ correctness is coming from the correctness of claim $T_1$.
## Latex

\Rightarrow

## Example

$x>1$ is implying $x^2>1$ and $x^3>1$ so denote:
$x>1 \Rightarrow x^2>1$
$x>1 \Rightarrow x^3>1$
