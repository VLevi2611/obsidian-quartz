## Definition

The action of applying the [[Linear Transformation]] $A$ to a vector $x$. The resulting [[Matrix]] is a [[Linear Combination]] of the columns of $A$, where the components of $x$ are the [[Scalar]]s.

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
a_{11} & a_{12} \\
a_{21} & a_{22} 
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
\mathbf{x} \\
\mathbf{y}
\end{pmatrix}=
\begin{pmatrix}
a_{11}\mathbf{x}+a_{12}\mathbf{y} \\
a_{21}\mathbf{x}+a_{22}\mathbf{y}
\end{pmatrix}
$$
