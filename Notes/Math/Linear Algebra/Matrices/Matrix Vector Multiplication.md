## Definition

The action of applying the [[Linear Transformation]] $A$ to a vector $x$. The result is a [[Linear Combination]] of the columns of $A$, where the components of $x$ are the [[Scalar]]s.

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{11} & a_{12} \\
a_{21} $ 
\end{bmatrix}$$
