## Definition

The action of applying the [[Linear Transformation]] $A$ to a vector $x$. The resulting [[Matrix]] is a [[Linear Combination]] of the columns of $A$, where the components of $x$ are the [[Scalar]]s.

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{11} & a_{12} \\
a_{21} & a_{22} 
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
x \\
y
\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{11}x+a_{12}y \\
a_{21}x+a_{22}y
\end{bmatrix}
$$
