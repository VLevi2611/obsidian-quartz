## Definition

A transpose $\mathbf{A}^T$ is a [[Matrix]] operation that flips a matrix over 
its diagonal (rows and columns switch).

$$\mathbf{A}=\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4
\end{pmatrix}
$$
$$\mathbf{A}^T=\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 3 \\
2 & 4
\end{pmatrix}$$
