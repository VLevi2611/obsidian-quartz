## Definition

The determinant of a [[Matrix]] $\det A$ measures the factor by with the area (2D) or volume (3D) is scaled by the [[Linear Transformation]].
If the determinant is 0, the space is compressed into a lower dimension.

--- 

### In $2*2$
$$A = \begin{bmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{bmatrix}$$
$$\det A= \begin{vmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{vmatrix} = ad-bc$$
