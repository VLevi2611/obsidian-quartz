## [[Dot Product]]

### Question 1

whats the dot product $\mathbf{a \cdot b}$
#### Given

$$\mathbf{a}=(9,5,-4,2),\mathbf{b}=(-3,-2,7,-1)$$
#### Solution

To solve a dot product, multiply the corresponding components and sum them.
$$\mathbf{a \cdot b}=\sum_{i=1}^{4}a_ib_i$$
