## Definition

A dot product is an operation between two vectors. 
Multiply each corresponding component, then summing the results.
The result of scalar multiplication is a [[Scalar]].
$$
\begin{array}{l}
x=(1,2),y=(5,3) \\
x \cdot y=(1*5+2*3) \\
x \cdot y=11
\end{array}$$
