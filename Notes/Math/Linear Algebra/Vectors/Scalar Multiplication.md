## Definition

To multiply a vector by a [[Scalar]], we multiply each component with the scalar.
The geometric interpretation of scalar multiplying is changing the size of the vector but keeping his direction.
$$
\begin{array}{l}
X=(1,2,0) \\
6X=(1*6,2*6,)
\end{array}$$
