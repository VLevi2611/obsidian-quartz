## Definition

Two vectors are called orthogonal if their [[Scalar Multiplication]] = 0.
Geometrically, that means that they are perpendicular.
$$\begin{array}{l}
\mathbf{x}=(1,2),\mathbf{y}=(-2,1) \\
\mathbf{x \cdot y} = 0 \\
\mathbf{x \perp y}
\end{array}$$
