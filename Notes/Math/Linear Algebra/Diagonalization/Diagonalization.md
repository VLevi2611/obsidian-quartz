## Overview

Diagonalization is the process of using a [[Linear Transformation]] on a [[Matrix]] $\mathbf{A}$ into a [[Diagonal Matrix]] by the form $\Lambda$, by relating it to a [[Basis]] composed of its [[Eigenvector]]s.
