## Overview

A field is a [[Set]] with [[Addition]] $+$ and [[Multiplication]] $\times$.
An [[Element Of]] a field is called [[Scalar]].
The field must have all of the following 11 properties:

- [[Closure Of Addition]] $$a+b \in F$$
- [[Associativity Of Addition]] $$(a + b) + c = a + (b + c)$$
- [[Commutativity Of Addition]] $$a + b = b + a$$
- [[Additive Identity]] $$a + 0 = 0 + a = a$$
- [[Additive Inverse]] $$a + (-a) = 0$$
- [[Closure of Multiplication]] $$ a \times b \in F$$
- [[Associativity Of Multiplication]] $$(a \times$$
- [[Commutativity Of Multiplication]]
- [[Multiplicative Identity]]
- [[Multiplicative Inverse]]
- [[Distributivity]]
