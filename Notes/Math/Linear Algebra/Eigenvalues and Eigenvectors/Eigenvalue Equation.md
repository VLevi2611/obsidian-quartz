## Definition

The Eigenvalue equation describes how a [[Linear Transformation]] $\mathbf{A}$ acts on a [[Eigenvector]] $\mathbf{v}$, only by scaling it with an [[Eigenvalue]] $\lambda$, without changing the direction.
$$\mathbf{Av}= \lambda \mathbf{v}$$
