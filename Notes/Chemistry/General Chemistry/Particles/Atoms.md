## Definition
Every chemical element is built from tiny undestroyable particles called atoms. Atoms cannot be created or destroyed during a chemical process. 
Atoms contain some combination of [[Protons]], [[Neutrons]] and [[Electrons]]. In the center of the atom there is a [[Nucleus]], there are the [[Protons]] and [[Neutrons]]. Around the [[Nucleus]], [[Electrons]] are "spinning".
