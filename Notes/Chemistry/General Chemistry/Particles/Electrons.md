## Definition
The electron is a subatomic particle whose electric charge is negative.
## Properties

| Symbol  | Mass                        | Electric charge |
| ------- | --------------------------- | --------------- |
| $$e^-$$ | $$9.1 \times 10^{-28} (g)$$ | $$-1e$$         |
