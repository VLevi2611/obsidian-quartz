## Overview 

[[Chemical Reaction|Chemical Reactions]] are processes in which one or more starting [[Substance|Substances]], the [[Reagent|Reagents]], form one or more new substances, the [[Product|Products]]. A chemical reaction can be represented symbolically in a [[Chemical Equation]], with [[Chemical Formula|Formulas]]of reagents on the left and formulas of products on the right. Reagents and products are separated by an arrow. The equation must be balanced. An equation is balanced by placing [[Stoichiometric Coefficients]] before formulas to signify that the total number of each kind of [[Atom]] is the same on each side of the equation. Reagents and products can be [[Solid|Solids]], [[Liquid|Liquids]] [[Gas|Gases]] or [[Aqueous Solution]].

[[Stoichiometry]] comprises quantitative relationships based on [[Atomic Mass|Atomic]] and [[Molecular Mass]], chemical formulas, balanced chemical equations, and related matters. Stoichiometric calculations usually require [[Molar Mass|Molar Masses]], [[Density|Densities]] and percent [[Composition]], along with other factors.

The [[Molality]] of a [[Solution]] is the amount of [[Solute]] (in [[Mole|Moles]]) per liter of solution. Molarity can be treated as a conversion factor between solution [[Volume]] and amount of solute.
