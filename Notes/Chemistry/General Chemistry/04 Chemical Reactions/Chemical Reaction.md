## Definition

A **chemical reaction** is a process in which one set of [[Substance|Substances]], called [[Reagent]] is converted to a new set of substances called [[Product]]. A chemical reaction is the process by which a chemical change occurs. We need evidence before we can say that a reaction has occurred. Some of the types of physical evidence to look for:
- a color change
- formation of a [[Solid]] within a clear solution
- evolution of a [[Gas]]
- evolution or absorption of [[Heat]]
