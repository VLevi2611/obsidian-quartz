## Definition 

The **Boltzmann constant, $k_B$** is related to the [[Gas Constant]] and [[Avogadro's Number]].
$$k_B = \frac{R}{N_A}$$
## Variables
| Symbol |        Meaning         |       Units        |
| :----: | :--------------------: | :----------------: |
| $k_B$  | [[Boltzmann Constant]] |  $(\frac{J}{K})$   |
|  $R$   |    [[Gas Constant]]    | $(\frac{J}{Kmol})$ |
| $N_A$  | [[Avogadro's Number]]  | $(\frac{1}{mol})$  |
