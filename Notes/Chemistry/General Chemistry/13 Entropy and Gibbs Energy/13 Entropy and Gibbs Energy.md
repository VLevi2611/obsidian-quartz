## Overview 

[[Entropy]], $S$, is a thermodynamic [[Property]] that is related to the way in which the [[Energy]] of a [[System]] is distributed around the available [[Energy Level|Energy Levels]]. A [[Microstate]] is a specific microscopic configuration describing how the particles of a system are distributed among the available energy levels.
