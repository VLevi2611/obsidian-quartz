## Definition 

The **gas constant** $R$ is a constant in the [[Ideal Gas Equation]]. Using the [[SI Units]] gives:
$$R = \frac{PV}{nT} = \frac{101,325 Pa \times 22.4140 \times 10^{-2} m^3}{1 mol \times 273.15K} = 8.3145 \frac{Pa \, m^3}{mol \, K}$$
