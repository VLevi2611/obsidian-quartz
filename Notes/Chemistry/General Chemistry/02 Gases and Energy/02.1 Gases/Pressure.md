## Definition

**Pressure** is defines as a [[Force]] per unit area.
$$P(Pa) = \frac{F(N)}{A(m^2)}$$
Because it is difficult to measure the total force of a gas, it is measured indirectly, by comparing it with a [[Liquid]] pressure. Consider a liquid with [[Density]] $d$, contained in a cylinder with cross sectional area $A$ filled to a height $h$.

$$P = \frac{F}{A} = \frac{W}{A} = \frac{gm}{A}$$
The [[Mass]] of a liquid is the product of its [[Volume]]  and density:
$$m = Vd$$
And the volume of a cylinder is the product of its volume and height:
$$V = hA$$
Substituting:
$$P = \frac{gm}{A} = \frac{gVd}{A} = \frac{ghAd}{A} = ghd$$
