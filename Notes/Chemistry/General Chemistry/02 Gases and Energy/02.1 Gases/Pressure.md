## Definition

**Pressure** is defines as a [[Force]] per unit area.
$$P(Pa) = \frac{F(N)}{A(m^2)}$$
## Variables

| Symbol | Meaning      | Units   |
| :----: | ------------ | ------- |
|  $P$   | [[Pressure]] | $(Pa)$  |
|  $F$   | [[Force]]    | $(N)$   |
|   A    | Area         | $(m^2)$ |
