## Definition

**Standard conditions of temperature and pressure** or **STP** is the standard for comparing different [[Gas|Gases]]. The standard [[Temperature]] for gases is taken to be $0^\circ\mathrm{C} = 273.15 \mathrm{K}$, and the standard [[Pressure]] is $1 bar = 100 kPa = 10^5 Pa$.
