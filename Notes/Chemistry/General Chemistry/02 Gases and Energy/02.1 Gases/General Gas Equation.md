## Definition

The **general gas equation** is the general case of the [[Ideal Gas Equation]] when a gas has two different sets of conditions. The ideal gas equation is applied twice: to the initial condition and the final condition.
$$P_i V_i = n_i R T_i , \, \, P_f V_f = n_f R T_f$$
