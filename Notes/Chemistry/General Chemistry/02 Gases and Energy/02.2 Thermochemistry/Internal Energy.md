## Definition

The **internal energy**, denoted as $U$ is the total [[Energy]], both [[Kinetic Energy]] and [[Potential Energy]] in a [[System]], including the kinetic energy of [[Molecule|Molecules]], molecular rotations and vibrations,, energy stored in chemical bonds,, inter molecular attractions and the energy associated with [[Electron|Electrons]] in [[Atom|Atoms]]. 
A system contains only internal energy, and does not contain energy in the form of [[Heat]] or [[Work]].
