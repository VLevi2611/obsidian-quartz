## Definition

An **exothermic reaction** is a [[Chemical Reaction]] that [[Product|Produces]] a [[Temperature]] increase in an [[Isolated System]] or, in a nonisolated system, gives off [[Heat]] to the [[Surroundings]]. For an exothermic reaction, the [[Heat of Reaction]] is negative ($q_{rxn} < 0$).
