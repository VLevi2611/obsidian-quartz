## Definition

Imagine a [[System]] with constant [[Volume]], because of that no [[Work]] is done. $$W = -P_{ext} \Delta V = 0$$If the [[Heat]] transferred for this constant volume process is denoted as $q_V$, than by the [[First Law of Thermodynamics]]: 
$$\Delta U = q_V$$
Now, imagine a system with constant [[Pressure]], if the heat transferred for this constant pressure process is denoted as $q_P$:
$$\begin{array}{l}
\Delta U = q_P -P_{ext} \Delta V \\
U_f - U_i = q_P 
\end{array}$$
