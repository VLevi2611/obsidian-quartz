## Definition

**Pressure volume work**, or $P - V$ [[Work]] is the [[Energy]] transferred when a [[System]] (usually [[Gas]]) expands or compresses against an external [[Pressure]]. 
$$W = - \frac{Mg}{A} \Delta h A = - P_{ext} \Delta V$$
