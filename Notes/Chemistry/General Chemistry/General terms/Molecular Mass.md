## Definition 

The molecular mass of a [[Molecule]] is the [[Mass]] in [[Atomic Weight]], Denotes as $M$.
The molecular mass is calculated by summing all of its [[Atoms]] mass.
For example: $$
\begin{array}{l}M(H_2O) = 2 \cdot Aw(H) + Aw(O)$$
