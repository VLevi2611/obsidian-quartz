## Definition

Combustion is a [[Chemical Reaction]] between a [[Molecular Compound]] and an oxidant that releases a lot of heat. 
One of the [[Chemical Products]] of the combustion is water.
If the [[Reagents]] is an [[Organic Compound]] creates water and carbon dioxide.
$$\mathrm{C_{x}H_{y}O_{z} + O_2 \rightarrow x\, CO_2 + y/2 \, H_2O}$$
