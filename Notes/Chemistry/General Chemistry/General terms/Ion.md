## Definition
An ion is an atom with a different number of [[Electrons]] and [[Protons]]. 
Ions have a charge.
$$^{16}_8O^{2-}$$
A positive ion is called [[Cation]] and a negative one is called [[Anion]].

## Notes
Notate the charge with the value of the charge on the left and the sign on the right.
