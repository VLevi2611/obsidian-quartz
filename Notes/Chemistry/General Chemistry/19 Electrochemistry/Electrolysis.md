## Definition

The process in which a [[Nonspontaneous Process]] is driven by the application of [[Electrical Energy]] to split [[Compound|Compounds]] into [[Element|Elements]]. It involves an [[Electrolytes|Electrolyte]], a [[Cathode]] (negative [[Electrode]]) attracting [[Cation|Cations]] and an [[Anode]] attracting [[Anion|Anions]].
