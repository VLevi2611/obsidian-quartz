## Definition

The **standard hydrogen electrode** involves the [[Equilibrium]] established on the surface of an inert [[Metal]] (such as platinum) between [[Hydronium Ion]] $\ce{H3O+}$ from a [[Solution]] in which they are at unit activity and $\ce{H2}$ [[Molecule|Molecules]] from the gaseous state at a [[Pressure]] of 1 bar. The equilibrium reaction produces a particular [[Potential]] on the metal surface, but this potential is arbitrarily taken to be 0.
