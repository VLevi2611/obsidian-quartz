## Definition

A **standard electrode potential, $E^{\circ}$**, measures the tendency for a [[Reduction]] process to occur at an [[Electrode]]. In all cases, the [[Ion|Ionic Species]] are present in [[Aqueous Solution]] at unit activity, and [[Gas|Gases]] are at 1 bar [[Pressure]].
