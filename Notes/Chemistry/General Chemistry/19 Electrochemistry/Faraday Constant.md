## Definition

When a [[Chemical Reaction|Reaction]] occurs in a [[Voltaic Cell]], the cell does [[Work]]. Think of this as the work of a moving electric [[Charge|Charges]]. The total work done is the product of 3 terms:
1. [[Cell Voltage]], $E_{cell}$.
2. $n$, the number of [[Mole|Moles]] of [[Electron|Electrons]] transferred between the [[Electrode|Electrodes]].
3. The electric charge per mole of electrons called the **Faraday Constant, $F = 96485 \frac{C}{mol}$**.
$$W_{elec} = nFE_{cell}$$
