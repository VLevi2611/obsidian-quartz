## Definition

A **salt bridge** is a device in [[Electrochemical Cell|Electrochemical Cells]] that connect 2 [[Half-Cell|Half-Cells]], maintaining electrical neutrality without mixing the [[Solution|Solutions]]. It consists of an [[Electrolytes|Electrolyte]] containing inert [[Ion|Ions]] that migrate into the half-cells to balance [[Charge]] buildup, allowing continuous [[Electron]] flow and preventing the cell potential from dropping to 0.
