## Definition

The [[Work]] that can be derived from a process is equal to the negative [[Gibbs Energy Change]]. For an electrochemical [[Chemical Reaction|Reaction]], we have $n = z \xi$ , where $z$ is the electron number, the [[Atomic Number]] + [[Absolute Value]] of negative [[Charge]]. $\xi$ is the extent of reaction in [[Mole|Moles]]. Substituting, we get:
$$W_{elec} = - \Delta G = z \xi F E_{cell}$$
The quantity $\Delta G / \xi$ is the Gibbs energy change per mole of reaction, represented as $\Delta _r G$.
$$\Delta _r G = -z F E_{cell}$$
From the [[Gibbs Energy of Reaction]], [[Standard Gibbs Energy of Reaction]], and the [[Reaction Quotient]], [[Temperature]] and the [[Gas Constant]] we get: $$\Delta _r G = \Delta _r G^{\circ} + RT \ln Q$$
Substituting, we get:
$$-z F E_{cell} = -z F E_{cell}^{\circ} + R T \ln Q$$
Dividing through by $-z F$ gives the **Nernst equation**:
$$E_{cell} = E_{cell}^{\circ} - \frac{RT}{zF} \ln Q$$
