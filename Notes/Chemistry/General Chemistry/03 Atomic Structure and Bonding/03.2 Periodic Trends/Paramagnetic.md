## Definition

A **paramagnetic** [[Atom]] or [[Ion]] has unpaired [[Electron|Electrons]], and the individual magnetic effects do not cancel out. The unpaired electrons possess a magnetic [[Moment]] that causes the atom or ion to be attracted to an external [[Magnetic Fields|Magnetic Field]]. The more unpaired electrons present, the stronger is this attraction.
