## Definition

In 1888 Heinrich Hertz discovered that when light strikes the surface of certain metals, [[Electron|Electrons]] are ejected. This phenomenon is called the **photoelectric effect** and the electrons emitted are called photoelectrons. The electron emission only occurs when the [[Frequency]] of the incident light is greater than a threshold value $\nu _0$. When this condition is met:
- The number of electrons emitted depends on the intensity of the incident of light.
- The [[Kinetic Energy]] of the emitted electrons depends on the frequency on the light.
Einstein proposed that electromagnetic radiation has particle like qualities, and they are called [[Photon|Photons]].
