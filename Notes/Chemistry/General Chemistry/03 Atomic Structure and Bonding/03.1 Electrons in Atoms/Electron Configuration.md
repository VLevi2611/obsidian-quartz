## Definition

The **electron configuration** of an [[Atom]] is the designation of how [[Electron|Electrons]] are distributed among the [[Atomic Orbital|Orbitals]] in [[Principal Electronic Shell|Principal shells]] and [[Subshell|Subshells]].

Rules for assigning electrons to orbitals:
1. Electrons occupy orbitals in a way that minimizes the [[Energy]] of the atom. Also known as **Pauli exclusion principle**. $$1s,2s,2p,3s,3p,4s,3d,4p,5s,4f,5d,6p,7s,5f,6d,7p$$
2. Only two electrons may occupy the same orbital, and these electrons must have two opposite [[Electron Spin|Spins]].
3. When orbitals of identical energy, are available, electrons initially occupy these orbitals singly and with parallel spins. Also known as **Hund's Rule**.
To write electron configurations we'll use the **aufbau process**.
