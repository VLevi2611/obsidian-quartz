## Definition

An **atomic orbital** is a function describing the location and wave-like behavior of an [[Electron]] in an [[Atom]]. Each orbital in an atom is characterized by a set of values of 3 [[Quantum]] numbers:
1. The principal quantum number, $n$, which may have only a positive, non zero integral value. $$n = 1,2,3,...$$
2. The orbital [[Angular Momentum]] quantum number, $\ell$, which may be zero or a positive integer but not larger than $n$. $$\ell = 0,1,2,...,n-1$$
