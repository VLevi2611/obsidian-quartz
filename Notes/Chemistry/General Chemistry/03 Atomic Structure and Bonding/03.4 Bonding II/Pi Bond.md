## Definition

A **pi bond** is a type of [[Covalent Bond]] formed by the side to side overlap of 2 [[Atomic Orbital|Atomic Orbitals]] on adjacent [[Atom|Atoms]]. These bonds feature electron density concentrated in 2 lobes, one above and one below the internuclear axis, and are weaker than [[Sigma Bond|Sigma Bonds]].
