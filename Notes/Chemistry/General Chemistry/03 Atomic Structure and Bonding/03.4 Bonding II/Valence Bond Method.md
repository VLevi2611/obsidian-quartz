## Definition

A description of [[Covalent Bond]] formation in terms of [[Atomic Orbital]] overlap is called the **valence bond method**. The valence bon method gives a localized [[Electron]] model of bonding: Core electrons and [[Lone Pair]] [[Valence Electron|Valence Electrons]] retain the same orbital location as in the separated [[Atom|Atoms]], but the bonding electrons do not. Instead, they are described by an electron probability density that includes the region of orbital overlap and both [[Nucleus|Nuclei]].
