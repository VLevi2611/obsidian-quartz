## Definition

A **terminal atom** is [[Chemical Bond|Bonded]] to just one other [[Atom]] in a [[Skeletal Structure]]. Hydrogen atoms are always terminal atoms, because an $\ce{H}$ atom can accommodate only 2 [[Valance Electron|Valance Electrons]], it can form only one [[Chemical Bond]] to another atom.
