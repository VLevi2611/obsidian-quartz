## Definition

The starting point in writing a [[Lewis Structure]] is to designate the **skeletal structure**, all the [[Atom|Atoms]] in the structure arranged in the order in which they are [[Chemical Bond|Bonded]] to one another. In a skeletal structure with more than 2 atoms, we need to distinguish between [[Central Atom|Central]] and [[Terminal Atom|Terminal Atoms]].
