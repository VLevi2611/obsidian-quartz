## Definition

**Electronegativity**, $\Delta EN$, describes an [[Atom|Atom's]] ability to compete for [[Electron|Electrons]] with other atoms to which it is bonded. Electronegativity is related to [[Ionization Energy]] and [[Electron Affinity]].
$$EN_A \propto E_i(A)-E_{ea}(A)$$
An [[Element]] with a high ionization energy and electron affinity that is large and negative, will have a large electronegativity.
