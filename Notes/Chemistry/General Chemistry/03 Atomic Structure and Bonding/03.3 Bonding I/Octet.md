## Definition

The **octet rule** is a chemical rule of thumb that reflects the theory that main group [[Element|Elements]] tend to create a [[Chemical Bond]] in such a way that each [[Atom]] has 8 [[Electron|Electrons]] as [[Valance Electron|Valance Electrons]], giving it the same [[Electron Configuration]] as a [[Noble Gas]].
