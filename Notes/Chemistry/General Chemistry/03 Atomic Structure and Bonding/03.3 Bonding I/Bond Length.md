## Definition

The **bond length** is the distance between the center of 2 [[Atom|Atoms]] joined by a [[Covalent Bond]]. A [[Double Covalent Bond]] is shorter than a [[Single Covalent Bond]], and [[Triple Covalent Bond]] is the shortest. The length of the covalent bond between 2 atoms can be approximated as the sum of the [[Covalent Radius|Covalent Radii]] of the two atoms.
