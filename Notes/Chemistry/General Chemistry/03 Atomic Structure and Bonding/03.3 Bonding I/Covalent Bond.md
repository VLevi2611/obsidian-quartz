## Definition

A **covalent bond** is a type of [[Chemical Bond]] in which [[Atom|Atoms]] share pairs of [[Valance Electron|Valance Electrons]], typically between [[Nonmetal|Nonmetals]]. These bonds can be 
- [[Single Covalent Bond]]
- [[Double Covalent Bond]]
- [[Triple Covalent Bond]]
