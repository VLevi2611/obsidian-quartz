## Definition

There are a few [[Lewis Structure]] that stand out by having 10 or 12 [[Valence Electron|Valence Electrons]] around the [[Central Atom]], creating an **expanded valence shell**. [[Molecule|Molecules]] with expanded valence shells typically involve [[Nonmetal]] [[Atom|Atoms]] of the third period and beyond that are in a [[Chemical Bond]] with highly [[Electronegativity]]
