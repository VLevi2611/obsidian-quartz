## Definition

The [[Absolute Value]] of the difference in [[Electronegativity]] values of the bonded [[Atom|Atoms]] is called the **electronegativity difference**, $\Delta EN$. If $\Delta EN$ for two atoms is very small, the bond between them is a [[Covalent Bond]]. If $\Delta EN$ is large, the bond is an [[Ionic Bond]]. For intermediate values of $\Delta EN$ the bond is described as [[Polar Covalent Bond]].
