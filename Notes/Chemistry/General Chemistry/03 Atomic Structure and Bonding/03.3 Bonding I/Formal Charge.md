## Definition

**Formal charge ($\ce{FC}$)** are apparent charges on certain [[Atom|Atoms]] in a [[Lewis Structure]] that arise when atoms have not contributed equal numbers of [[Electron|Electrons]] to the [[Covalent Bond]] sharing them. 
The formal charge on an atom in a Lewis structure is the number of [[Valence Electron]] in the free atom minus the number of electrons assigned to that atom in the Lewis structure:
- Count [[Lone Pair]] electrons as belonging to the atom on which they are found.
- Divide [[Bond Pair]] electrons equally between the [[Chemical Bond|Bonded]] atoms.
