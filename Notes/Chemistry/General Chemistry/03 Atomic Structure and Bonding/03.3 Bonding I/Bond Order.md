## Definition

The **bond order** describes whether a [[Covalent Bond]] is [[Single Covalent Bond]] (bond order = 1), [[Double Covalent Bond]] (bond order = 2), or [[Triple Covalent Bond]] (bond order = 3). The more [[Electron|Electrons]] present, the more tightly the [[Atom|Atoms]] are held together.
