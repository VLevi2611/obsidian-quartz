## Definition

A **chemical bond** is a lasting electrostatic attraction that holds [[Atom|Atoms]] or [[Ion|Ions]] together, allowing them to form stable [[Compound|Compounds]] by sharing or transferring [[Valance Electron|Valance Electrons]]. There are a few types of bonds: [[Covalent Bond]], [[Ionic Bond]], [[Metallic Bond]].
