## Definition

Metals are a group of [[Element]]s with similar [[Property]]s. Except for mercury, a [[Liquid]], 
metals are [[Solid]]s at room temp. They are generally malleable (capable of being flattened into thin sheets), ductile (capable of being drawn into fine wires), good conductors of heat and electricity, and have a shiny appearance.
