## Definition

Like [[Liquid|Liquids]], [[Solid|Solids]] can also give off [[Vapor|Vapors]], although because of the stronger intermolecular forces present, solids are generally not as volatile as liquid at a given [[Temperature]]. The direct passage of [[Molecule|Molecules]] from the solid to the vapor state is called **sublimation**. 
When sublimation and [[Deposition]] occur at equal rates, a [[Dynamic Equilibrium]] exists between a solid and its vapor. The vapor exerts characteristic [[Pressure]] called the **sublimation pressure**.
