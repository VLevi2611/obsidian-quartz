## Definition

In a crystalline [[Solid]], there is a regular, ordered arrangement of particles. That may be [[Atom|Atoms]], [[Ion|Ions]] or [[Molecule|Molecules]]. As a crystalline solid is [[Heat|Heated]], its particles vibrate more vigorously. Eventually a [[Temperature]] is reached at which these vibrations disrupt the ordered structure. The particles can slip past one another and the solid loses its definite shape and is converted to a [[Liquid]]. This process is called **melting**.
