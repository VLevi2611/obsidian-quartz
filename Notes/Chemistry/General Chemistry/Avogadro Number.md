## Symbol
$$N_A$$
## Definition
The number of constituent particles (usually atoms or molecules) in one mole of a substance.

## Value
$$
N_A = 6.02214076 \times 10^{23}
$$

**Units:** mol$^{-1}$

## Notes
- Defined exactly since 2019 as part of the SI redefinition.
- Links macroscopic quantities (moles) to microscopic particles.
