## Overview 

The [[Equilibrium Constant]] for the [[Equilibrium]] between a [[Solid]] [[Ionic Compound|Ionic]] [[Solute]] and its [[Ion|Ions]] in a [[Saturated Solution]] is expressed through the [[Solubility Product Constant]], $K_{sp}$.

A solute's [[Molality]] in a saturated [[Aqueous Solution]] is known as its **molar solubility**. Molar solubility and $K_{sp}$ are related to each other in a way that makes it possible to calculate one when the other is known.

The [[Solubility]] of a slightly soluble ionic solute is greatly reduced in an aqueous solution containing ion in common with the solubility equilibrium- a common ion.
