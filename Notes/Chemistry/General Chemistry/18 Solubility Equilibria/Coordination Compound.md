## Definition

**Coordination compounds** are [[Substance|Substances]] containing [[Complex Ion|Complex Ions]]. Theses complexes from through [[Lewis Base|Lewis Acid-Base Reaction]] and often exhibit distinct vibrant colors, specific magnetic [[Property|Properties]] ([[Paramagnetic]] or [[Diamagnetic]]) and diverse [[Electron-Group Geometry|Geometries]]. The coordination number is the number of [[Proton Donor|Donor Atoms]] directly [[Chemical Bond|Bonded]] to the [[Central Atom|Central]] [[Metal]] (commonly 4 or 6).
