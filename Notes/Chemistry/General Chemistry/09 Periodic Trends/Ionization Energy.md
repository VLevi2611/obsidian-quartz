## Definition

The **ionization energy, $E_i$** is the quantity of [[Energy]] an [[Atom]] must absorb to be able to expel an [[Electron]]. With relatively few exceptions, Ionization energies increase from left to right across a period and decrease from top to bottom within a group. Ionization energies decrease as [[Atomic Radius]] increase.
