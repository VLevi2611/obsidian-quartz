## Definition

**Functional groups** are specific clusters of [[Atom|Atoms]] within [[Molecule|Molecules]] that dictate their chemical behavior, reactivity, and [[Property|Properties]], regardless of the rest of the molecule's structure. Key examples are:
- alcohols $(\ce{OH-})$
- carbonyls $(\ce{C=O})$
- carboxylic [[Acid|Acids]] $(\ce{COOH-})$
- amines $(\ce{NH2-})$
