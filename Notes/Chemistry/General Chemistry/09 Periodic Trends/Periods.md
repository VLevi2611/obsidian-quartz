## Definition

A **period** on the [[Periodic Table]] is a row of [[Element|Elements]]. All elements in a row have the same number of [[Principal Electronic Shell|Electron Shells]]. Each next element in a period has one or more [[Proton]] and is less [[Metal|Metallic]] than its predecessor.
