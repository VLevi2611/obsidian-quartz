## Definition

**Electron-group geometry** describes the spatial arrangement of all [[Electron]] groups ([[Bond Pair|Bond Pairs]] and [[Lone Pair|Lone Pairs]]) around a [[Central Atom]] to minimize repulsion. Based on [[VSEPR Theory]], these geometries determine the basic shape of a [[Molecule]].
