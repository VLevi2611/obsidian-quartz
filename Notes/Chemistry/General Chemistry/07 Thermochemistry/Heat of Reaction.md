## Definition

A **heat of reaction**, $q_{rxn}$ , is the quantity of [[Heat]] exchanged between a [[System]] and its [[Surroundings]] when a [[Chemical Reaction]] occurs within the system at constant [[Temperature]]. If a reaction occurs in an [[Isolated System]], the reaction [[Product|Produces]] a change in the [[Thermal Energy]] of the system. The temperature either increases or decreases.
