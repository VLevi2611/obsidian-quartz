## Definition

The **enthalpy of reaction** is the [[Heat]] absorbed or released during a [[Chemical Reaction]] at constant [[Pressure]]. It represents the difference between the total [[Enthalpy]] of [[Product|Products]] and [[Reagent|Reagents]]
$$\Delta H_{rxn} = H_{products} - H_{Reagents}$$
The enthalpy of reaction changes sign when a a process is [[Reversible Process|Reversed]].
