## Definition

The **standard enthalpy of reaction, $\Delta _r H^{\circ}$**, is the [[Heat]] absorbed or released during a [[Chemical Reaction]] when [[Reagent|Reagents]] in their [[Standard State|Standard States]] convert to [[Product|Products]] in their standard states, based on molar amounts in a balanced [[Chemical Equation]]
