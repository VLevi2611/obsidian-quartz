## Definition

A **bonding molecular orbital** is a region of high [[Electron]] probability created by the constructive, in-phase overlap of [[Atomic Orbital|Atomic Orbitals]]. These orbitals are lower in [[Energy]] than the original atomic orbitals, stabilizing the [[Molecule]] by concentrating electron density between [[Nucleus|Nuclei]], resulting in attraction rather than repulsion.
