## Definition

**Chemical formulas** represent [[Molecular Compound|Molecular Compounds]] with the [[Element|Elements]] present and the relative number of [[Atom|Atoms]] of each element.
In the formula for water, the constituent elements are denoted by their symbols. The relative number of atoms are indicated by subscripts.
