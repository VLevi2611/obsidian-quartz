## Definition

A **molecular compound** is made up of [[Molecule|Molecules]], which typically consists of a small number of [[Nonmetal]] [[Atom|Atoms]] held together by [[Covalent Bond|Covalent Bonds]]. Molecular [[Compound|Compounds]] are represented by [[Chemical Formula|Chemical Formulas]].
