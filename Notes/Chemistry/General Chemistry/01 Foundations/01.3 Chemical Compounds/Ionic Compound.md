## Definition

An **ionic compound** is made up of positive and negative [[Ion|Ions]] joined together by electrostatic forces of attraction. The [[Atom|Atoms]] of metallic [[Element|Elements]] tend to lose one or more [[Electron|Electrons]] to the nonmetal atoms when they combine with them. It makes the compound into [[Cation]] and [[Anion]].
