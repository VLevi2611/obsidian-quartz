## Definition

The number of [[toolbox file/Notes/Chemistry/General Chemistry/01 Foundations/01.2 Atomic Theory/Proton]]s in a given [[Atom]] is called the **atomic number, Z**. The number of [[Electron]]s on the atom is also equal to **Z**, because the atom is electrically neutral.
