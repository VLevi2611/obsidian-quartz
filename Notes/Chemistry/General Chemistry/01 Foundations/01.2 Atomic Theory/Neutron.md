## Definition

Neutron is a subatomic particle, that has no electric charge, and a [[Mass]] slightly greater that the [[toolbox file/Notes/Chemistry/General Chemistry/01 Foundations/01.2 Atomic Theory/Proton]]. The Neutrons are found, together with a similar number of protons in the [[Nucleus]] of an [[Atom]].

| Electric Charge (C) | Electric Charge (Atomic) |       [[Mass]] (g)       |
| :-----------------: | :----------------------: | :----------------------: |
|         $0$         |           $0$            | $1.6749 \times 10^{-24}$ |
