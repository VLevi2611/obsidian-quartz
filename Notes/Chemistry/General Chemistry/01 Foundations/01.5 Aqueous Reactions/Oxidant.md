## Definition

In a [[Oxidation Reduction]] the [[Substance]] that makes it possible for some other substance to be [[Oxidation|Oxidized]] is called the **oxidizing agent** or **oxidant**. An oxidant contains an [[Element]] whose [[Oxidation State]] decreases in a redox reaction. The oxidant gains [[Electron|Electrons]]. In general, a substance with an element in one of its highest possible oxidation state is an oxidant.
