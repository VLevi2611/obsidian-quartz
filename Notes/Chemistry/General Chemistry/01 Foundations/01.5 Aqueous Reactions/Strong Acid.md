## Definition

A **strong acid** has a strong tendency for producing $\ce{H+}$ [[Ion|Ions]] in [[Aqueous Solution]]. A strong acid is a [[Molecular Compound]] that is almost completely [[Ionization|Ionized]] into $\ce{H+}$ (aq) and accompanying [[Anion|Anions]] when in aqueous solution.

Hydrogen chloride $\ce{HCl}$, and nitric [[Acid]] $\ce{HNO3}$ are examples of strong acids.
