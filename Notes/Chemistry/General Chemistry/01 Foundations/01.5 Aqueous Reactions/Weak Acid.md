## Definition

A **weak acid** is a [[Molecular Compound]] that has a weak tendency for [[Product|Producing]] $\ce{H+}$ [[Ion|Ions]]. A weak acid is incompletely [[Ionization|Ionized]] in [[Aqueous Solution]]. Most [[Acid|Acids]] are weak acids.
