## Definition

A strong base is a [[Substance]] that goes through a complete [[Dissociation]] in an [[Aqueous Solution]].
Here is a list of Strong bases:
- $\ce{LiOH}$
- $\ce{NaOH}$
- $\ce{KOH}$
- $\ce{RbOH}$
- $\ce{CsOH}$
- $\ce{Mg(OH)2$
