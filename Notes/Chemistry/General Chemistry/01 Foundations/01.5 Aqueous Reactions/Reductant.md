## Definition

In the [[Oxidation Reduction]] reaction, the [[Substance]] that causes some other substance to be [[Reduction|Reduced]] is called the **reducing agent** or **reductant**. The reductant contains an [[Element]] whose [[Oxidation State]] increases in a redox reaction. The reductant loses [[Electron|Electrons]]. If the substance with the element is in one of its lowest possible oxidation states, the substance is a reductant.
