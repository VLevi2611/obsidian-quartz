## Definition

A **base** is a [[Substance]] that [[Product|Produces]] hydroxide [[Ion|Ions]] $(\ce{OH-})$ IN [[Aqueous Solution]]. There are [[Strong Base|Strong Bases]] and [[Weak Base|Weak Bases]]. Bases usually are [[Proton Acceptor|Proton Acceptors]].
