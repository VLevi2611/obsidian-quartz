## Definition

Some [[Metal]] [[Salt|Salts]], are quite [[Solubility|Soluble]] in water. Precipitation reactions occur when [[Cation|Cations]] and [[Anion|Anions]] combine to [[Product|Produce]] an insoluble Ionic [[Solid]] called a **precipitate**.
