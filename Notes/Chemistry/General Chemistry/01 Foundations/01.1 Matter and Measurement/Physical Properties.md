## Definition

A **physical property** is one that a sample of [[Matter]] displays without changing its [[Composition]]. 
Color, Malleableness, brittleness etc. are examples of physical [[Property|Properties]].

A **physical change** is where some of the physical properties of a sample change, but its [[Composition]] remains. Water [[Freezing]] for example.
