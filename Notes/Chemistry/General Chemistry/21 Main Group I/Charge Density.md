## Definition

The **charge density, $\rho$**, can be defined as [[Charge]] per unit [[Volume]]. For a [[Metal]] [[Cation]], $\ce{M^z+}$, with [[Ionic Radius]] $r$, the charge density is calculated by using this formula:
$$\rho = \frac{ze}{V} = \frac{1.6 \times 10^{-19} C}{4/3 \pi r^3} z$$
