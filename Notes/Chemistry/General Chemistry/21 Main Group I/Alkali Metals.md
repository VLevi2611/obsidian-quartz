## Definition

The [[Groups|Group]] 1 [[Element|Elements]], the **alkali metals**, are relatively abundant. Some of their [[Compound|Compounds]] have been known and used since prehistoric times. Yet these elements were not isolated in pure form until about 200 years ago. The compounds if the alkali metals are difficult to decompose by ordinary chemical means, so discovery of the elements had to await new scientific developments. Sodium $\ce{Na}$ and potassium $\ce{K}$ were discovered through [[Electrolysis]]. Cesium $\ce{Cs}$ and rubidium $\ce{Rb}$ were identified as new elements through their [[Atomic Spectra]]. Francium $\ce{Fr}$ was isolated in the radioactive decay products of actinium $\ce{Ac}$.
Because most alkali metal compounds are [[Water]] [[Solubility|Soluble]], a number of Lithium $\ce{Li}$, Sodium $\ce{Na}$, and potassium $\ce{K}$ compounds, including chlorides, carbonates and sulfates can be obtained from natural brines.
