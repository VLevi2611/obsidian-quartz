## Definition

The **Heisenberg uncertainty principle** implies that for a very precise measurement of position, $x$, many values of [[04 Momentum|Momentum]], $p$, are possible. One way to rationalize this result is to conceive of a highly localized particle as a superposition of many [[Matter]] [[Wave|Waves]] of different de Broglie [[Wavelength|Wave]]
