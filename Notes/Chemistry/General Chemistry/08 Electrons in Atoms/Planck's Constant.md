## Definition 

Max Planck figured out that [[Energy]] is discontinuous, and comes in small packets, **Plank's constant** $h$, with the value:
$$h = 6.626 \times 10^{-34} Js$$
The [[Energy]] of a quantum of [[Electromagnetic Radiation]] is proportional to the [[Frequency]] of the radiation, and that is Planck's equation:
$$E = h \nu$$
