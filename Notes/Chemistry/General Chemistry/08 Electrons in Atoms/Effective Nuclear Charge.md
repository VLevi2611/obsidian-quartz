## Definition

The **effective nuclear charge**, $Z_{\text{eff}}$ , is the net positive [[Charge]] experienced by an [[Electron]] in a multielectron [[Atom]], always lower than the actual nuclear charge $Z$, due to the shielding effect of inner-shell electrons. It determines [[09 Periodic Trends|Periodic Trends]] such as [[Atomic Radius]], [[Ionization Energy]] and [[Electronegativity]].
