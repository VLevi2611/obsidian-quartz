Wave-particle duality is ==the quantum mechanical principle that every particle or quantum entity (like photons or electrons) behaves as both a wave and a particle depending on the experimental setup==. Light propagates as a wave but interacts as a particle (photoelectric effect). Conversely, matter behaves as particles but can exhibit wave-like diffraction patterns.
