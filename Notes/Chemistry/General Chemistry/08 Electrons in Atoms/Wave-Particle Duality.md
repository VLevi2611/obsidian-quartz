## Definition

**Wave-particle duality** is the principle that every particle or quantum entity (like [[Photon|Photons]] or [[Electron|Electrons]]) behaves as both a [[Wave]] and a particle depending on the experimental setup. Light propagates as a wave but interacts as a particle ([[Photoelectric Effect]]). Conversely, [[Matter]] behaves as particles but can exhibit wave-like [[Diffraction]] patterns.
