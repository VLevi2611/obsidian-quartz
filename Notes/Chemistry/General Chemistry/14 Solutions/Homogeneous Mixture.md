## Definition

A **homogeneous mixture** is a type of [[Mixture]] that combines 2 or more [[Substance|Substances]] that is uniform throughout, possessing the same [[Composition]] and [[Property|Properties]] regardless of where the sample is taken. All [[Solution|Solutions]] are homogeneous because the dissolved [[Solute]] is present throughout the solution.
