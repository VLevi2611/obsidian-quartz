## Overview 

In a [[Solution]], the [[Solvent]], usually the component present in greatest amounts, determines the [[State of Matter]] in which the solution exists. A [[Solute]] is a solution component dissolved in the solvent. [[Dilute Solution]] contain relatively small amounts of solute and [[Concentrated Solution]], large amounts.

Any description of the [[Composition]] of a solution must indicate the quantities of solute and solvent present. Solution [[Concentration|Concentrations]] expressed as [[Mass]] percent, [[Volume]] percent and mass/volume percent all haev practical importance
