## Definition

If the [[Van Der Waals Forces]] are of the same type or of equal strength, the [[Solute]] and [[Solvent]] [[Molecule|Molecules]] mix randomly. A homogeneous [[Mixture]] or [[Solution]] appears. Because [[Property|Properties]] of solution of this type can be predicted from the properties of the pure components, they are called **ideal solutions**. There is no overall [[Enthalpy]] change in the formation of an ideal solution, $\Delta _{soln} H = 0$.
