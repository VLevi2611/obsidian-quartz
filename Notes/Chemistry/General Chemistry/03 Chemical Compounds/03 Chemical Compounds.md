## Overview 

The 2 main classes of chemical [[Compound|Compounds]] are [[Molecular Compound|Molecular Compounds]] and [[Ionic Compound|Ionic Compounds]]. The fundamental unit of a molecular compound is a [[Molecule]] and that of an ionic compound is a **formula unit**. A formula unit is the smallest collection of positively [[Charge|Charged]] [[Ion|Ions]], called [[Cation|Cations]]
