## Overview 

A [[Gas]] is described in terms of its [[Pressure]], [[Temperature]], [[Volume]] and amount. Gas pressure is most readily measured by comparing it with the pressure exerted by a [[Liquid]] column, usually mercury. The pressure exerted in a **barometer** and called the **barometric pressure** is equal to the prevailing pressure of the atmosphere. Other gas pressures can be measured with a **manometer**. Pressure can be expressed in a variety of units, including the [[SI Units]]**pascal ($Pa$)0**
