## 1. Explain Like I'm 12 (Core Idea)

Atoms bond because they want to reach a more stable, lower-energy state.  
They can **give**, **take**, or **share** electrons:

- **Ionic bonds** → give/take
- **Covalent bonds** → share
- **Metallic bonds** → share freely

Bond type affects melting point, shape, strength, and conductivity.

---

## 2. Identify Gaps in Understanding

- Why do ionic solids conduct when melted?
- Why do molecules have shapes?
- What causes polarity?

---

## 3. Fill the Gaps (Refine the Explanation)

Molecular shape comes from electron repulsion (VSEPR). Polarity comes from unequal sharing (electronegativity). Ionic compounds conduct because ions are mobile when melted.

---

## 4. Explain Again, Even Simpler

Bonding = atoms lowering energy by sharing or transferring electrons.
