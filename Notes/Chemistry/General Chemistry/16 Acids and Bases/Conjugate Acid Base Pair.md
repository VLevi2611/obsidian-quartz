## Definition

A **conjugate acid base pair** consists of two [[Substance|Substances]] that differ only by one [[Proton]] $(\ce{H+})$, as defined by the [[Bronsted Lowry theory]]. An [[Acid]] becomes its [[Conjugate Base]] after donating a proton. A [[Base]] becomes its [[Conjugate Acid]] after accepting a proton.
