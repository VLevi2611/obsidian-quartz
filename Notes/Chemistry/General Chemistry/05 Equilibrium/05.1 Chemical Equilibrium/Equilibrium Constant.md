## Definition

The **equilibrium constant, $K$,** is a numerical value defining the ratio of [[Product]] [[Concentration]] to [[Reagent]] concentration for a [[Reversible Process]] at [[Equilibrium]], with each concentration raised to the power of its [[Stoichiometric Coefficients|Stoichiometric Coefficient]]. It indicates whether products $(K > 1)$ or reagents $(K < 1)$ are favored at a given [[Temperature]].
For the general [[Chemical Equation]] $aA + bB \ce{\leftrightharpoons} cC + dD$, the equilibrium constant is expresses as
$$K = \frac{[C]^c [D]^d}{[A]^a [B]^b}$$

When individual equations are combined, their equilibrium constants are [[Multiplication|Multiplied]] to obtain the equilibrium constant for the [[Overall Reaction]].
