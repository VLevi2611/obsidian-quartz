## Definition

The **equilibrium constant, $K$,** is a numerical value defining the ratio of [[Product]] [[Concentration]] to [[Reagent]] concentration for a [[Reversible Process]] at [[Equilibrium]], with each concentration raised to the power of its [[Stoichiometric Coefficients|Stoichiometric Coefficient]].
