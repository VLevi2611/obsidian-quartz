## Definition

**Titration** is a laboratory method used to determine the unknown [[Concentration]] of a target [[Solution]] ([[Analyte]]) by adding a [[Reagent]] of known concentration [[Titrant]] from a burette. The process continues until the [[Chemical Reaction|Reaction]] reaches completion, indicated by a color change ([[End Point]]).
