## Definition

A **buffer solution** is a type of [[Solution]] that resists significant changes in [[pH]] upon the addition of small amounts of [[Strong Acid]] or [[Strong Base]], maintaining a stable, relatively constant pH. They are [[Composition|Composed]] of a [[Weak Acid]] and its [[Conjugate Base]], or a [[Weak Base]] and its [[Conjugate Acid]].
