## Definition

The **ion product of water, $K_w$** , is the [[Equilibrium Constant]] for the [[Self Ionization]] of water into hydronium $(\ce{H3O+})$ and hydroxide $(\ce{OH-})$ [[Ion|Ions]], expressed as 
$$K_w = [\ce{H+}] [\ce{OH-}]$$
At room [[Temperature]], this constant is $1.0 \times 10^{14} mol^2 dm^{-6}$ , where the [[Concentration]] of both $\ce{H+}, \ce{OH-}$ in pure water is $1.0 \times 10^{-7} M$.
$$-\log K_w = pK_W= pH + pOH$$
