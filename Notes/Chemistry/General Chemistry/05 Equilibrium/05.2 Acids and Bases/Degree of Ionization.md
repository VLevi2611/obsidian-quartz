## Definition

The **degree of ionization, $\alpha$**, is the proportion of neutral [[Molecule|Molecules]] that [[Dissociation|Dissociate]] into [[Ion|Ions]] in a [[Solution]] or [[Gas]], calculated as the ratio of [[Ionization|Ionized]] molecules to the total molecules initially taken. It indicates the strength of [[Electrolytes]]
