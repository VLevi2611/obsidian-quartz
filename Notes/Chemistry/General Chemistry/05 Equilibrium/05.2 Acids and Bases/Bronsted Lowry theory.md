## Definition

The **Bronsted Lowry theory** defines [[Acid|Acids]] as [[Proton Donor|Proton Donors]] and [[Base|Bases]] as [[Proton Acceptor|Proton Acceptors]]. This theory is fundamental for understanding acid base [[Chemical Reaction|Reactions]], specifically focusing on the transfer of [[Proton|Protons]] between species.
