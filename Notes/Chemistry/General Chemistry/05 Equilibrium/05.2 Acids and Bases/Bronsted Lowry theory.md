## Definition

The **Bronsted Lowry theory** defines [[Acid|Acids]] as [[Proton Donor|Proton Donors]] and [[Base|Bases]] as [[Proton Acceptor|Proton Acceptors]]. This theory is fundamental for understanding acid base [[Chemical Reaction|Reactions]], specifically focusing on the transfer of [[Proton|Protons]] between species.
- An acid contains at least one [[Ionization|Ionizable]] $\ce{H}$ [[Atom]], and a base contains an atom with a [[Lone Pair]] of [[Electron|Electrons]] onto which a proton can bind. 
- For a [[Conjugate Acid Base Pair]], the [[Molecular Formula]] for the acid and base differ by a single proton.
- When added to water, acids [[Protonation|Protonate]] water [[Molecule|Molecules]] to form hydronium $(\ce{H3O+})$ [[Ion|Ions]] and bases [[Deprotonation|Deprotonate]] water molecules to form hydroxide $(\ce{OH-})$ ions.
