## Definition

**Acid base reaction** is a [[Chemical Reaction]] where an [[Acid]] and a [[Base]] react to neutralize each other, typically [[Product|Producing]] [[Water]] and [[Salt]]. The reaction involves a transfer of [[Proton|Protons]] from the acid to the base  ([[Bronsted Lowry theory]]), or exchange of [[Ion|Ions]], resulting in a [[pH]] change toward neutrality.

- For an acid-base reaction, [[Equilibrium]] favors the formation of the [[Weak Acid|Weaker Acid]] and the [[Weak Base|Weaker Base]].
- If the acid or base in an acid-base reaction is [[Strong Acid]] or [[Strong Base]], they react essentially to completion.
