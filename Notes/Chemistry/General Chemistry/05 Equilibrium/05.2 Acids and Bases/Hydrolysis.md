## Definition

The [[Chemical Reaction]] between an [[Ion]] and water is called a **hydrolysis**. In it water is used to break down [[Chemical Bond|Chemical Bonds]] within a [[Molecule]], splitting it into smaller components. It involves adding a hydrogen [[Ion]] $(\ce{H+})$ to one fragment and a hydroxyl group $(\ce{OH-})$ to another.
