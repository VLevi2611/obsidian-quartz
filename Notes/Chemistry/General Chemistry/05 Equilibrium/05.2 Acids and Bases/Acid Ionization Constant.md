## Definition

The **acid ionization constant, $K_a$** , is a quantitative measure if an [[Acid]] strength in [[Solution]], representing the [[Equilibrium Constant]] for its [[Dissociation]] into [[Ion|Ions]]. A higher $K_a$ indicates greater [[Ionization]] and a [[Strong Acid|Stronger Acid]].
$$K_a = \frac{[\ce{H+}] [\ce{A-}]}{[\ce{HA}]}$$
