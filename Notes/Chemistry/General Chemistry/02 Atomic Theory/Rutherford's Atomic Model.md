## Definition

**The Rutherford atomic model**, proposed by Ernest Rutherford in 1911, established that the [[Atom]] has a tiny, [[Density|Dense]], positively [[Charge|Charged]] [[Nucleus]] containing most of its [[Mass]], surrounded by orbiting [[Electron|Electrons]]. The remainder of the atom is mostly empty space.
The magnitude of the positively charge is different for different atoms and is approximately half of the [[Atomic Mass]] of the [[Element]]. The atom as a whole is electrically neutral.
