## Definition

The Rutherford atomic model, proposed by Ernest Rutherford in 1911, established that the [[Atom]] has a tiny, dense, positively charged [[Nucleus]] containing most of its [[Mass]], surrounded by orbiting [[Electron]]s. The remainder of the atom is mostly empty space.
The magnitude of the positively charge is different for different atoms and is approximately half of the [[Atomic Weight]] of the [[Element]]. The atom as a whole is electrically neutral.
