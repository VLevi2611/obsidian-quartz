## Definition
Protons are subatomic particles with a positive charge.

## Properties

| Symbol  | Mass                         | Electric charge |
| ------- | ---------------------------- | --------------- |
| $$p^+$$ | $$1.67 \times 10^{-24} (g)$$ | $$+1e$$         |

## Notes
Protons are much larger than [[Electrons]]
