## Definition

One of the goals in a chemical kinetics study is to derive an equation that can be used to predict the relationship between the [[Rate of Reaction]] and the [[Concentration|Concentrations]] of [[Reagent|Reagents]], The **rate equation**. 
Consider the hypothetical [[Chemical Reaction]]
$$aA + bB + ... \rightarrow gG + hH + ...$$ Where $a,b,...$ are the [[Stoichiometric Coefficients]] in the [[Chemical Equation]]. The rate law for this reaction can be expressed in the following general form.
$$\text{rate of reaction} = k [A]^m [B]^n ...$$
The terms $[A],[B],...$ represent the reagents [[Molarity|Molarities]]. The required exponents, $m,n,...$ are generally small, positive [[Whole Number|Whole Numbers]], although in some cases they may be 0, fractional or negative.
