## Definition

The **active site** is a specialized, 3D crevice on an [[Enzyme]] surface, where [[Substrate]] [[Molecule|Molecules]] bind via weak, noncovalent interaction ([[Hydrogen Bond]], hydrophobic interactions) and undergo [[Chemical Reaction]]. This region consists of specific [[Amino Acids|Amino Acid]] residues that form 2 distinct and overlapping regions: the [[Binding Site]], which docks the substrate, and the [[Catalytic Site]] which acts upon it.
