## Definition

The **activated complex** is the species that formed in the [[Transition State]]. The activated complex, formed through [[Collision|Collisions]], either [[Dissociation|Dissociates]] back to the original [[Reagent|Reagents]] or forms [[Product]] [[Molecule|Molecules]].
