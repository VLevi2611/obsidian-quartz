## Definition

The **activated complex** is the species that formed in the [[Transition State]]. The activated complex, formed through [[Collision|Collisions]], either [[Dissociation|Dissociates]] back to the original [[Reagent|Reagents]] or forms [[Product]] [[Molecule|Molecules]]. We can represent an activated complex for reaction in this way.
$$\begin{array}{c}
\ce{N#N}
\end{array}$$
