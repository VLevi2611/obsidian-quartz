## Definition

The **order of reaction** is a number which quantifies the degree to which the [[Rate of Reaction]] depends on [[Concentration|Concentrations]] of the [[Reagent|Reagents]].

- 0 order in the reagent: There is no effect on the [[Initial Rate of Reaction]].
