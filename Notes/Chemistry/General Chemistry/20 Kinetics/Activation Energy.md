## Definition

For a [[Chemical Reaction]] to occur following a [[Collision]] between [[Molecule|Collision]], there must be a redistribution of [[Energy]] that puts enough energy into certain [[Chemical Bond|Bonds]] to break them. We would not expect 2 slow-moving molecules to bring enough [[Kinetic Energy]] into their collision to permit bond breakage. We would expect 2 fast-moving molecules to do so, or one extremely fast molecule colliding with a slow one. The **activation energy** of a reaction is the minimum kinetic energy that molecules must bring to the collision for a chemical reaction to occur.
