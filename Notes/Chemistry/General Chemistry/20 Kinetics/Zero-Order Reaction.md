## Definition

An overall **zero-order reaction** has a [[Rate Equation]] in which the sum of all the exponents, $m,n, ...$ is equal to 0. As an example, lets take a [[Chemical Reaction|Reaction]] in which a single [[Reagent]] $A$ decomposes to [[Product|Products]].
$$A \rightarrow \text{products}$$
If the reaction has a [[Order of Reaction]] of 0, the rate law is
$$\text{rate of reaction} = k [A]^0 = k = \text{constant}$$
