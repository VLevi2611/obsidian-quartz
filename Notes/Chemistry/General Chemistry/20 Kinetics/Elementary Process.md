## Definition

Each step in a [[Reaction Mechanism]] is called an **elementary process**, which describes any molecular event that significantly alters a [[Molecule|Molecule's]] [[Energy]] or [[Molecular Geometry]] or produces a new molecule. It can be either [[Unimolecular process]] or [[Bimolecular process]].
