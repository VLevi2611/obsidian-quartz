## Definition

Many [[Chemical Reaction|Reactions]] can be [[Catalyst|Catalyzed]] by allowing them to occur on the appropriate [[Solid]] surface. Essential [[Reaction Intermediate|Reaction Intermediates]] are found on the surface. This type of catalyst is called **heterogeneous catalyst** because it is present in a different [[State of Matter]] than are the [[Reagent|Reagents]] and [[Product|Products]]. A key feature of heterogeneous catalysis is that reagents from a [[Gas|Gaseous]] or [[Solution]] phase are adsorbed, or attached to the surface of the catalyst. Not all surface [[Atom|Atoms]] are equally effective for catalyst, those that are effective are called [[Active Site|Active Sites]].
