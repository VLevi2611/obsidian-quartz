## Definition

The **half-life** if a [[Chemical Reaction|Reaction]] is the [[Time]] required for one-half of a [[Reagent]] to be consumed. It is the time during which the amount of reagent or its [[Concentration]] decreases to half its [[Initial Rate of Reaction|Initial Value]]. That is, at $t = t_{\frac{1}{2}}, [A]_t = \frac{1}{2} [A]_0$ . At this time, the [[Integrated Rate Law]] takes the form
$$\ln \frac{[A]}{}$$
