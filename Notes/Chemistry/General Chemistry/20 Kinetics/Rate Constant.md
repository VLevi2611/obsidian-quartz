## Definition

The proportionality constant $k$ in the [[Rate Equation]]
$$\text{rate of reaction} = k [A]^m [B]^n ...$$
is called the **rate constant** of the [[Chemical Reaction|Reaction]]. Its value depends on the specific reaction, the presence of a [[Catalyst]] (if any) and the [[Temperature]]. The larger the value of $k$, the **faster** a reaction goes.
