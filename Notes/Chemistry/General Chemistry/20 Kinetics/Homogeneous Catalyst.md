## Definition

A **homogeneous catalyst** is a [[Substance]] that exist in the same [[State of Matter]] (typically [[Liquid]] or [[Gas]]) as the [[Reagent|Reagents]] in a [[Chemical Reaction]]. It accelerate the [[Rate of Reaction]] by creating a lower [[Activation Energy]], usually involving transition metals  that change [[Oxidation State|Oxidation States]]. The reagents and [[Product|Products]] are all present throughout the [[Solution]], or [[Homogeneous Mixture]].
