## Definition

**Entropy, $S$**, is a thermodynamic [[Property]] that is related to the way in which the [[Energy]] of a [[System]] is distributed among the available [[Energy Level|Energy Levels]].
Boltzmann associated the number of energy levels in the system with the number of ways of arranging the particles ([[Atom|Atoms]], [[Ion|Ions]] or [[Molecule|Molecules]]) in these energy levels. He derived the relationship
$$S = k_B \, ln(W)$$
