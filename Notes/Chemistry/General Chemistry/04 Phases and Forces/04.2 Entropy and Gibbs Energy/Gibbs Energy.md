## Definition

Imagine a hypothetical process that occurs at constant [[Temperature]] $T$ and constant [[Pressure]] $P$, with [[Work]] limited to [[Pressure Volume Work]]. We will assume that the surroundings also have temperature $T$. Because the pressure is constant we have $\Delta H_{sys} = q_p$.
