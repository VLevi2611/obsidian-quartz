## Definition

The **Gibbs energy change, $\Delta G$**, for a process at constant [[Temperature]] $T$ is
$$\Delta G = \Delta H - T \Delta S$$
All the terms refer to changes for the [[System]]. All reference to the surroundings has been eliminated.
$$\Delta G = -T \Delta S_{univ}$$
By noticing that $\Delta G$ is negative when $\Delta S_{univ}$ is positive, we have the final criterion for spontaneous change based on [[Property|Properties]] of only the system itself.
- If $\Delta G < 0$, the process is a [[Spontaneous Process]].
- If $\Delta G > 0$, the process is a [[Nonspontaneous Process]].
- If $\Delta G = 0$, the process is a [[Reversible Process]] and the system has reached [[Equilibrium]].
