## Definition

A **saturated solution** is a [[Mixture]] containing the maximum [[Concentration]] of a dissolved [[Solute]] in a [[Solvent]] at a specific [[Temperature]] and [[Pressure]]. At this point, no additional solute will dissolve, and any excess added will remain undissolved or settle at the bottom. It represents a state of [[Dynamic Equilibrium]] between dissolved and undissolved solute.
