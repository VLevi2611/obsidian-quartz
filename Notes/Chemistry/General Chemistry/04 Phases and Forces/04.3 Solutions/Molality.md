## Definition

A concentration unit that is independent of [[Temperature]] but also proportional to [[Mole]] fraction in [[Dilute Solution|Dilute Solutions]] is **molality** ($m$), the number of moles of [[Solute]] per kilogram of [[Solvent]] (not of [[Solution]]). A solution in which 1.00 moles of urea, $\ce{CO(NH2)}$,  is dissolved in 1.00 kg of [[Water]] is described as a 1.00 molal solution and designated as $1.00 m$ $\ce{CO(NH2)2}$. Molality is defines as
$$\text{molality}(m) = \frac{\text{amount of solute (in moles)}}{\text{amount of solvent (in kilograms)}}$$
