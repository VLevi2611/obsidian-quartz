## Definition

Earlier we saw that the speed and [[Kinetic Energy]] of [[Molecule|Molecules]] vary over a wide range at any given [[Temperature]]. We also learned that molecules having kinetic energies above the average value are able to overcome intermolecular forces of attraction and escape from the surface of the [[Liquid]] into the gaseous state. This passage of molecules from the surface of a liquid into the vapor state is called **evaporation**. Evaporation occurs more readily with
- Increase temperature
