## Definition

Like [[Liquid|Liquids]], [[Solid|Solids]] can also give off [[Vapor|Vapors]], although because of the stronger intermolecular forces present, solids are generally not as volatile as liquid at a given [[Temperature]]. The direct passage of [[Molecule|Molecules]] from the solid to the vapor state is called **sub**
