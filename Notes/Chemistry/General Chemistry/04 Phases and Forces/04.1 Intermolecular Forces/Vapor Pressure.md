## Definition

 The [[Pressure]] exerted by a [[Vapor]] in a [[Dynamic Equilibrium]] with its [[Liquid]] is called the **vapor pressure**. Liquids with high vapor pressure at room [[Temperature]] are said to be **volatile**, and those with very low vapor pressure are called **nonvolatile**. The weaker the intermolecular forces, the more volatile the liquid.
