## Definition

**States of matter** are distinct forms- [[Solid]], [[Liquid]] [[Gas]] and [[Plasma]], in which [[Matter]] exists based on molecular arrangement and [[Energy Level|Energy Levels]] . Solids have fixed [[Volume]] , liquids have fixed volume but flow, gases fill their container, and plasmas are ionized, high-[[Energy]] fluids. Phase changes occur with [[Temperature]]/[[Pressure]] changes.
