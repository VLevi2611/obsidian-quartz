## Definition

**States of matter** are distinct forms- [[Solid]], [[Liquid]] [[Gas]] and [[Plasma]], in which [[Matter]] exists based on molecular arrangement and [[Energy]] levels. Solids have fixed [[Volume]] , liquids have fixed volume but [[flow]] , gases fill their container, and plasmas are ionized, high-energy fluids. Phase changes occur with temperature/pressure changes.
