## Definition

**Surface tension**, ($\gamma$) is the [[Energy]], or [[Work]] required to increase the [[Surface Area]] of a [[Liquid]]. It has the units of energy per unit area, Joules per square meter ($Jm^{-2}$). As the [[Temperature]] increases, intermolecular forces become less effective. Less work is required to extend the surface of a liquid.
