## Definition

The **critical point** is the point at which the following conditions are reached and the [[Liquid]] and [[Vapor]] become indistinguishable.
- The [[Density]] of the liquid decreases, that of the vapor increases and the two become equal.
- The [[Surface Tension]] of the liquid approaches zero. The interface between the liquid and vapor becomes less distinct and eventually disappears.
The [[Temperature]] at the critical point is the **critical temperature, $T_c$** and the [[Pressure]] is the **critical pressure, $P_c$**. The critical point is the highest point on a [[Vapor Pressure]] curve and represents the highest temperature at which the liquid can exist. 
A [[Gas]] can be liquified only at temperatures below its critical temperature.
