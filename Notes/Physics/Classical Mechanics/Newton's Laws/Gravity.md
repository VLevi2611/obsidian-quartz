## Definition
Gravitation Force is the effect of a field that is generated by an object with mass. Each planet has its [[Gravitational Constant]] $g$. 
Earth's [[Gravitational Constant]] is $g = 9.8$
## #formula 

[[Gravity Force Formula]]
$$
\begin{array}{l}
F_g = m \, g
\end{array}
$$
## Units

Newtons $$N$$
