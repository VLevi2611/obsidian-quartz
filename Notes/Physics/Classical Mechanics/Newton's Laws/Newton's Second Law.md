## Description

Newton's second law relates the [[Force]] acting on a body to its [[Mass]] and [[Acceleration]].

## #formula 

$$ \mathbf{F} = m \mathbf{a} $$
## Variables
| Symbol         | Meaning          | Units               |
| :--------------: | :----------------: | :-------------------: |
| $\mathbf{F}$ | [[Force]]        | $(N)$             |
| $m$          | [[Mass]]         | $(Kg)$            |
| $\mathbf{a}$ | [[Acceleration]] | $(\frac{m}{s^2})$ |

## Example Calculation

A 5 kg object accelerates at 3 m/s²:  
$$ \mathbf{F} = 5*3 = 15 (N) $$

## Notes

- Force and acceleration are vectors in the same direction.
- Valid only for non-relativistic motion.
