## Definition

Kinetic energy $(E_K)$ is the energy that an object has from its motion.
The more [[Velocity]] / [[Mass]] an object has, the more kinetic energy it has.

## #formula 

$$E_K=\frac{1}{2}mv^2$$

## Units

Joules $$(J)$$
