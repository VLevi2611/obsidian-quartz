## Definition

Elastic potential energy $U_{sp}$ is a type of [[Potential Energy]] that stems from the deformation of an object in space. 
## #formula 

$$U_{sp}= \frac{1}{2}K\mathbf{x}^2$$
## Variables
| Symbol         | Meaning          | Units             |
| -------------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
