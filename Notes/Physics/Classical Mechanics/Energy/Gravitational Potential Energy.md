## Definition

Gravitational potential energy $U_g$ is a type of [[Potential Energy]] that stems from the position of an. object in space. 
## #formula 

$$U_g=m\mathbf{g}h$$
## ## Variables
| Symbol         | Meaning                    | Units               |
| -------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| $$m$$          | [[Mass]]                   | $$(Kg)$$            |
| $$\mathbf{g}$$ | [[Gravitational Constant]] | $$(\frac{m}{s^2})$$ |
| $$h$$          | Height                     | $$(m)$$             |
