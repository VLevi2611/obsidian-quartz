## Definition

Gravitational constant $\mathbf{g}$ is the [[Acceleration]] that gives the strength of the gravitational field induced by a [[Mass]].

## Value
$$\mathbf{g} = 9.8 (\frac{m}{s^2})$$
## #formula 

[[Gravity Force Formula]]
$$\mathbf{F}_g = m\mathbf{g}$$
