## Definition
Acceleration is the rate of velocity change. It is a vector with the same direction as the force.

## #formula 

[[Newton's Second Law]]
$$F = m \, a $$
[[Motion Equations]]
$$
\begin{array}{l}
a = \frac{dv}{dt} \\
x(t) = x(0) + v(0)t + a \frac{t^2}{2} \\
v(t)^2 = v(0)^2 + 2ax(t)
\end{array}
$$
## Units
$$\frac{m}{s^2}$$
