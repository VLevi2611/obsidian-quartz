## Overview

The motion equation are he differential relations between
## #formula 

$$
\begin{array}{l}
\mathbf{v} = \frac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt} \\
\mathbf{a} = \frac{d\mathbf{v}}{dt} \\
\mathbf{x}(t) = \mathbf{x}(0) + \mathbf{v}(0)t + \mathbf{a} \frac{t^2}{2} \\
\mathbf{v}(t)^2 = \mathbf{v}(0)^2 + 2\mathbf{ax}(t)
\end{array}
$$

## Description
The motion equations are the differential relations between distance, speed and acceleration.

## Example Calculation
An object starts moving from rest for 2 seconds. It crosses in the first second 5 metes in steady motion. in the second second it crosses 10 meters in steady motion.
$$
\begin{array}{l}
v_1 = \frac{dx_1}{dt} = \frac{5}{1} = 5 (\frac{m}{s}) \\
v_2 = \frac{dx_2}{dt} = \frac{10}{1} = 10 (\frac{m}{s}) \\
a = \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{10-5}{1} = 5 (\frac{m}{s^2})
\end{array}
$$

## Notes
Positive velocity: going forwards
Negative velocity: going backwards
Positive acceleration: speeding up
Negative acceleration: slowing down
