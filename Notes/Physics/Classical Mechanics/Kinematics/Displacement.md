## Definition
Displacement is a vector whose length is the shortest distance between 2 points.

## #formula 

[[Motion Equations]]
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\mathbf{v} = \frac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt} \\
\mathbf{x}(t) = \mathbf{x}(0) + \mathbf{v}(0)t + \mathbf{a} \frac{t^2}{2} \\
\mathbf{v}(t)^2 = \mathbf{v}(0)^2 + 2\mathbf{ax}(t)
\end{array}
$$
