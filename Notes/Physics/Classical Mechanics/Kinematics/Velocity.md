## Definition
Velocity is a vector that measures speed in a certain direction.

## Core Equation(s)
$$
\begin{array}{l}
v = \frac{dx}{dt} \\
a = \frac{dv}{dt}
\end{array}
$$
## Units
Velocity (v): (m/s)
