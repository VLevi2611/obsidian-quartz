## Overview
Kinematics is the study of motion in objects.

## Key Equations
[[Motion Equations]]
$$
\begin{array}{l}
v = \frac{dx}{dt} \\
a = \frac{dv}{dt} \\
x(t) = x(0) + v(0)t + a \frac{t^2}{2} \\
v(t)^2 = v(0)^2 + 2ax(t)
\end{array}
$$
