## Definition

The coefficient of restitution $(e)$ is a scalar between 0-1 measuring how much [[Inelastic Collision]] of a [[Collision]] it is.
Coefficient of 0 means [[Perfectly Inelastic Collision]] and 1 means [[Elastic Collision]].
$$e=\frac{|Relative \ velocity \ of \ seperation \ after \ collision|}{|Relative \ velocity \ of \ approach \ before \ collision|}$$
$$e=\frac{|\mathbf{v}_{f1}-\mathbf{v}_{f2}|}{|\mathbf{v}_{i1}-\mathbf{v}_{i2}|}$$
