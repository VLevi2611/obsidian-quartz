## Definition

Impulse $\mathbf{J}$ is the change of [[Momentum]] of an object. Impulse is conserved in an [[Isolated System]].
$$\mathbf{J} = \mathbf{p}_f-\mathbf{p}_i$$
$$\mathbf{J}=\int_{t_i}^{t_f} \mathbf{F}dt$$
## Variables

| Symbol | Meaning             | Units     |
| ------ | ------------------- | --------- |
| $$J$$  | [[Impulse]]         | $$(N s)$$ |
