## Definition

Conservation of momentum states that the total [[Momentum]] in an [[Isolated System]] remains constant.

## #formula 
$$\sum_{j=1}^{n} m_{intial,j}v_{initial,j}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}m_{final,j}v_{final,j}$$

## Variables

| Symbol | Meaning      | Units             |
| ------ | ------------ | ----------------- |
| $$m$$  | [[Mass]]     | $$(Kg)$$          |
| $$v$$  | [[Velocity]] | $$(\frac{m}{s})$$ |
