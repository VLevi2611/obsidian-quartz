## Definition

The impulse momentum theorem states that the [[Impulse]] applied to an object is equal to its change of momentum

## #formula 

$$\mathbf{J}= \int_{t_i}^{t_f} \mathbf{F}dt = m(\mathbf{v}_f-\mathbf{v}_i)$$
