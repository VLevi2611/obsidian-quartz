## Equation
$$ F = m \, a $$

## Variables
| Symbol | Meaning          | Units |
| ------ | ---------------- | ----- |
| F      | [[Force]]        | N     |
| m      | [[Mass]]         | kg    |
| a      | [[Acceleration]] | m/s^2 |

## Description
Relates the net force acting on a body to its mass and acceleration.  
Foundation of classical dynamics and motion analysis.

## Example Calculation
A 5 kg object accelerates at 3 m/s²:  
$$ F = 5 \times 3 = 15 [N] $$

## Notes
- Force and acceleration are vectors in the same direction.
- Valid only for non-relativistic motion.
