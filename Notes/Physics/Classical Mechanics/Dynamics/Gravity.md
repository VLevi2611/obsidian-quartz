## Definition
Gravitation Force is the effect of a field that is generated by an object with mass. Each planet has its gravitational coefficient g. In earth its 9.8.
## Core Equation(s)
$$
\begin{array}{l}
F_g = m \, g
\end{array}
$$
## Units

Gravitational force$$F_g: Newton (N)$$
