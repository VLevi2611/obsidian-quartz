## Definition
Force is a vector that causes an object to accelerate.

## Key Equations
[[Newton's Second Law]]
$$ F = ma $$
[[Gravity Force Formula]]
$$
\begin{array}{l}
F_g = m \, g
\end{array}
$$
## Units
Force ($F$): Newton ($N)/ (\frac{kg\,m} {s^2}$)

## Notes
- Direction of force determines direction of acceleration.
- Net force determines changes in motion.
