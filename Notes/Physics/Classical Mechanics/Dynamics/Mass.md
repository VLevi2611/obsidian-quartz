## Definition
Mass is a scalar quantity that measures the amount of matter in an object and represents its resistance to acceleration when a force is applied.

## Key Equations
$$ F = ma $$
## Units
- Mass ($m$): kilogram ($kg$)

## Notes
- Do not confuse mass with weight.
