# Action Potential

**Subject:** Physiology  
**Tags:** #concept #biology #physiology #nervous-system #action-potential

---

## Definition  
An **action potential** is a rapid, temporary change in a neuron’s membrane potential that enables the transmission of electrical impulses along the axon.

---

## Key Phases  
1. **Resting State** — Neuron at –70 mV; Na⁺ and K⁺ channels closed.  
2. **Depolarization** — Na⁺ channels open, Na⁺ flows into the cell → potential rises to +30 mV.  
3. **Repolarization** — K⁺ channels open, K⁺ leaves the cell → potential returns negative.  
4. **Hyperpolarization** — Overshoot below resting potential before stabilizing again.  

---

## Main Formula  
**Membrane potential (Goldman equation):**
$$
E_m = \frac{RT}{F} \ln\left(\frac{P_K[K^+]_{out} + P_{Na}[Na^+]_{out} + P_{Cl}[Cl^-]_{in}}{P_K[K^+]_{in} + P_{Na}[Na^+]_{in} + P_{Cl}[Cl^-]_{out}}\right)
$$
