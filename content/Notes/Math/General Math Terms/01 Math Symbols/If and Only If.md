## Sign 
$$
\Leftrightarrow 
$$
## Definition

If and only if means that if claim $T_2$ and claim $T_1$ are the same if $T_1$ implies $T_2$ and the other way around. 
## Latex

\Leftrightarrow 

## Example

$x>1$ is the same as $x^3>1$ so denote:
$x^3>1 \Leftrightarrow x>1$
