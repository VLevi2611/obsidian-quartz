## Definition

Distributivity means that the [[Multiplication]] of an [[Element Of]] a [[Set]] with the [[Addition]] of two elements in parentheses is the same as multiplying each element in the parentheses with the element outside the parentheses and summing them. $$a \times (b + c) = a \times b + a \times c$$
