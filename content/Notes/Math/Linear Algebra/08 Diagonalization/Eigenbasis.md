## Definition

The eigenbasis is a [[Set]] of [[Basis]] [[Vector]]s that are also [[Eigenvector]]s of a [[Matrix]] $\mathbf{A}$.
If such basis exists, the transformation matrix $\mathbf{A}$ is equivalent to the [[Diagonal Matrix]] $\Lambda$ in this coordinate system.
