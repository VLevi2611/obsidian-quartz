## Definition

The dimension is the size of the space, defined by the number of [[Vector]]s contained in any [[Basis]] for that space.

The standard basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$ is:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\left\{ 
\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 
\end{pmatrix}, 
\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 
\end{pmatrix}, 
\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 1 
\end{pmatrix} 
\right\}
\end{array}
$$ The dimension is:
$$\dim _\mathbb{R} (\mathbb{R}^3)=3$$
