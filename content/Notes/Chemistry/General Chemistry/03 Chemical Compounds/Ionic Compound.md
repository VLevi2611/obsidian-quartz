## Definition

An **ionic compound** is made up of positive and negative [[Ion|Ions]] joined together by electrostatic forces of attraction. The [[Atom|Atoms]] of [[Metal]] [[Element|Elements]] tend to lose one or more [[Electron|Electrons]] to the [[Nonmetal]] atoms when they combine with them. It makes the compound into [[Cation]] and [[Anion]].
