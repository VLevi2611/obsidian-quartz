## Definition

In **combustion**, a weighed sample of a [[Compound]] is burned in a stream of oxygen [[Gas]]. The [[Water]] [[Vapor]] and baron dioxide gas produces in the combustion are absorbed by appropriate [[Substance|Substances]].

$$\ce{C_xH_yO_z} + \ce{O_2} \rightarrow x \ce{CO_2} + \frac{y}{2} \ce{H_2O}$$
After combustion, all the carbon [[Atom|Atoms]] in the sample are found in the $\ce{CO_2}$. All the hydrogen atoms are in the $\ce{H_2O}$.
