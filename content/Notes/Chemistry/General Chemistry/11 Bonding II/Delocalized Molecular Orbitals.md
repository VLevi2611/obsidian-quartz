## Definition

**Delocalized molecular orbitals** are [[Quantum]] [[Wave Function|Wavefunctions]] that extend over several [[Atom|Atoms]] or the entire [[Molecule]], rather than being confined to a single [[Chemical Bond|Bond]] or atom. They arise from the [[Linear Combination]] of [[Atomic Orbital|Atomic Orbitals]] in [[Conjugate Acid Base Pair|Conjugated Systems]] and are essential for describing aromatic compounds, [[Resonance]], and bonding in [[Metal|Metals]].
