## Definition

The **molecular orbit theory** describes [[Chemical Bond]] by assuming [[Atomic Orbital|Atomic Orbitals]] combine to form new delocalized molecular orbitals spread across the entire [[Molecule]]. Each molecular orbital can be represented as a [[Linear Combination]] of atomic orbitals. The total number of molecular orbitals is equal to the total number of atomic orbitals. They can be classified as bonding antibonding or nonbonding. The [[Electron Configuration]] for the molecule is obtained by placing the [[Electron|Electrons]] into the molecular orbitals in a particular order.
