## Definition

**Molecular geometry** is the 3-dimensional arrangement of [[Atom|Atoms]] within a [[Molecule]], determined by [[Bond Length|Bond Lengths]], [[Bond Angle|Bond Angles]] and [[Lone Pair]] repulsion. Based on the [[VSEPR Theory]], [[Electron]] pairs surrounding a [[Central Atom]] arrange themselves to minimize repulsion, directly impacting a [[Substance]] reactivity, [[Polar Molecule|Polarity]] and [[Physical Properties]].

Key molecular shapes:
- **Linear**: 2 electron domains, 180 angle.
- **Trigonal Planar**: 3 electron domains, 120 angle.
- **Tetrahedral**: 4 electron domains, 109.5 angle.
- **Trigonal Bipyramidal**: 5 electron domains, 90 and 120 angles.
- **Octahedral**: 6 electron domains, 90 angle.

Lone pairs occupy more space than [[Bond Pair|Bond Pairs]], forcing [[Chemical Bond|Bonds]] closer together and reducing bond angles.
