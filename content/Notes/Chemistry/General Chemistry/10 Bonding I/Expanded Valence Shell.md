## Definition

There are a few [[Lewis Structure]] that stand out by having 10 or 12 [[Valence Electron|Valence Electrons]] around the [[Central Atom]], creating an **expanded valence shell**. [[Molecule|Molecules]] with expanded valence shells typically involve [[Nonmetal]] [[Atom|Atoms]] of the third period and beyond that are in a [[Chemical Bond]] with highly [[Electronegativity|Electronegativity]] atoms. 
By assuming that after the $3s$ and $3p$ [[Subshell|Subshells]] of the [[Central Atom]] fill to capacity (8 electrons), extra electrons go into the empty $3d$ subshell.
