## Definition

The situation in which 2 or more plausible [[Lewis Structure|Lewis Structures]] contribute to the correct structure is called **resonance**. The true structure is a resonance hybrid of plausible contributing structures. Acceptable contributing structures to a resonance hybrid must all have equal [[Skeletal Structure]], they can differ only in how [[Electron|Electrons]] are distributed withing the structure. The two contributing structures are joined by a double headed arrow. We can decide which resonance structure likely contributes most to the hybrid by applying the general rules for [[Formal Charge]].
