## Definition

An **average bond energy** is the average of [[Bond Dissociation Energy|Bond Dissociation Energies]] for a number of different species. [[Double Covalent Bond]] have higher bond energies than [[Single Covalent Bond]] between the same [[Atom|Atoms]] but they are not twice as large. [[Triple Covalent Bond]] are the strongest.
