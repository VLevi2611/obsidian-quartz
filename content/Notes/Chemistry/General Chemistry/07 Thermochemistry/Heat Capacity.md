## Definition

The quantity of [[Heat]] required to change the [[Temperature]] of a [[System]] by one degree is called the **heat capacity**. Represented by the symbol $C$.
$$C = \frac{q}{\Delta T}$$
Heat capacity depends on whether the system is heated at constant [[Pressure]] or at constant [[Volume]]. To distinguish between them, the symbols $C_p$ and $C_V$ are used.
