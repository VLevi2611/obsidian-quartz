## Definition

The **first law of thermodynamics** is a relationship between [[Heat]], [[Work]] and the changes in [[Internal Energy]] in a [[System]].
$$\Delta U = q + W$$
An [[Isolated System]] is unable to exchange heat or work with its [[Surroundings]], so that the energy of an isolated system is constant.

Any energy **entering** the system is positive. If heat is absorbed by the system, $q > 0$. If work is done on the system, $W > 0$.
Any energy **leaving** the system is negative. If heat is given off by the system, $q < 0$. If work is done by the system, $W < 0$.
