## Definition

A **standard cell potential, $E_{cell}^{\circ}$** , is the [[Potential]] difference, or [[Voltage]] of a [[Voltaic Cell]] formed from 2 standard [[Electrode|Electrodes]]. The difference is always taken from the [[Cathode]] to the [[Anode]].
$$E_{cell}^{\circ} = E_{cathode}^{\circ} - E_{anode}^{\circ}$$
