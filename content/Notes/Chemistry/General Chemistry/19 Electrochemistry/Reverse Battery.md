## Definition

The [[Electrolytes|Electrolyte]] in a **reverse battery** is [[Isolated System|Isolated]] from the rest of the [[Battery]] to reduce self discharge or the chemical degradation typically found in other batteries. The battery is designed as a long term storage battery, which can be used to deliver high power over a relatively short [[Time]].
