## Definition

A **battery** is a device that stores [[Chemical Energy]] for later release as [[Electricity]] ([[Matter]] possessing [[Charge]]). Some batteries consist of a single [[Voltaic Cell]] with 2 [[Electrode|Electrodes]] and the appropriate [[Electrolytes]]. An example is a flashlight cell. Other batteries consist of 2 or more voltaic cells joined in series- plus to minus, to increase the total [[Voltage]]. An example is an automobile battery. 
There are 3 types of voltaic cells and the batteries based on them are:
- [[Primary Cell]]
- [[Secondary Cell]]
- [[Reverse Battery]]
- [[Fuel Cell]]
