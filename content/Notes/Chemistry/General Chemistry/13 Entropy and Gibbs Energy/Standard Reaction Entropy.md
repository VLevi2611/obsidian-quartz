## Definition

The **standard reaction entropy, $\Delta _r S ^\circ$,** of a [[Chemical Reaction]] can be calculated using the [[Standard Molar Entropy]] for each [[Product]] and [[Reagent]].
$$aA + bB + ... \rightarrow cC + dD + ...$$
When the uppercase letters represent different [[Substance|Substances]] and the lowercase letters represent the [[Stoichiometric Coefficients]] required to balance the [[Chemical Equation]]. $\Delta_r S ^\circ$ for the reaction is obtained using the following equation.
$$\Delta _r S ^\circ = [c S_C ^\circ + d S_D ^\circ + ...] - [a S_A ^\circ + b S_B ^\circ + ...]$$
