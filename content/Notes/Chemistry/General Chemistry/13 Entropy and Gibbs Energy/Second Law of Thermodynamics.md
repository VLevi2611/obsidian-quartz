## Definition

All [[Spontaneous Process|Spontaneous Processes]] produce an  increase in the [[Entropy]] of the universe.
$$\Delta S_{univ} = \Delta S_{sys} + \Delta S_{surr} > 0$$
- If $\Delta S_{univ} > 0$, the process is spontaneous.
- If $\Delta S_{univ} < 0$, the process is a [[Nonspontaneous Process]].
- If $\Delta S_{univ} = 0$, the process is a [[Reversible Process]].
