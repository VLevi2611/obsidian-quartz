## Definition

We have seen 2 ways to obtain [[Product]] from a [[Nonspontaneous Process|Nonspontaneous Reaction]]: 
1. Change the [[Chemical Reaction]] conditions to ones that make the reaction [[Spontaneous Process|Spontaneous]]. (mostly by changing the [[Temperature]])
2. Combine a pair of reactions, one with a positive [[Gibbs Energy Change]] and one with a negative $\Delta _r G$, to obtain a spontaneous overall reaction.
Such paired reactions are called **coupled reactions**.
