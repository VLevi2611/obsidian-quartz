## Definition

**Energy levels** are specific, discrete, and [[Quantum|Quantized]] amounts of [[Energy]] that bound [[System|Systems]], such as [[Electron|Electrons]] in an [[Atom]], are allowed to possess. Electrons occupy fixed [[Principal Electronic Shell|Shells]] at increasing distances from the [[Nucleus]], with higher levels holding more energy.
