## Definition

The **electron spin** is a [[Quantum]] number, $m_s$, may have a value of $+ \frac{1}{2}$, also denoted by $\uparrow$, or $- \frac{1}{2}$, also denoted by $\downarrow$. The value of $m_s$ does not depend on any of the other three quantum numbers.
Few key details:
1. An [[Electron]], because of its spin, generates a magnetic field.
2. A pair of electrons with opposing spins has no net magnetic field.
3. A silver [[Atom]] in its lowest [[Energy]] state has only one unpaired electron. The direction of the net magnetic field depends on the spin of the unpaired electron.
4. In a beam of a large number of silver atoms there is an equal chance that the unpaired electron will have a positive or negative spin.
