## Definition

The **atomic spectra** are the unique [[Set|Sets]] of light [[Frequency|Frequencies]] emitted or absorbed by [[Element|Elements]] and acting as elemental fingerprints due to the [[Quantum|Quantized]] [[Electron]] [[Energy Level|Energy Levels]]. When electrons drop to lower energy levels, they emit light (emission spectrum), and when they absorb energy, they move to higher levels (absorption spectrum).
