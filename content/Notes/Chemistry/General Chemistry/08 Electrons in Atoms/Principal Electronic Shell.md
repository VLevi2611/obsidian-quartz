## Definition

All [[Atomic Orbital|Atomic Orbitals]] with the same value of $n$ are in the same **principal electronic shell**. Principals electronic shells are numbered according to the value of $n$. The value of $n$ related to the [[Energy]] and most probable distance of an [[Electron]] from the [[Nucleus]].
