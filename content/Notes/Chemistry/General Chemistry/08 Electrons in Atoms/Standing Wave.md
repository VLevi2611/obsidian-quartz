## Definition

A **standing wave** is a [[Wave]] pattern that appears to stay in one place, formed by the interference of 2 waves of the same [[Frequency]] and [[Amplitude]] traveling in opposite directions. They consist of fixed points of no [[Displacement]], known as [[Node|Nodes]] and areas of maximum [[Amplitude]], known as [[Antinode|Antinodes]].
