## Definition

A **gas** is a state of matter with no fixed [[Volume]] or shape. It is a compressible form of fluid, unlike a [[Liquid]]. A pure gas consists of individual [[Atom|Atoms]] ([[Noble Gas]]) or [[Molecule|Molecules]] (for example $\ce{O2}$). Gases diffuse into one another. 4 [[Property|Properties]] determine the physical behavior of a gas:
1. the amount of the gas (in [[Mole|Moles]])
2. the volume of the gas
3. the [[Temperature]] of the gas
4. the [[Pressure]] of the gas
