## Definition

[[Atom|Atoms]] that have the same [[Atomic Number]] but different [[Mass Number]] are called **Isotopes**.
Of all the $\ce{Ne}$ atoms on earth, 90.51% are $\ce{^{20}_{10}Ne}$. The percentages of $\ce{^{21}_{10}Ne}$ and $\ce{^{22}_{10}Ne}$ are 0.27% and 9.22%, respectively.
