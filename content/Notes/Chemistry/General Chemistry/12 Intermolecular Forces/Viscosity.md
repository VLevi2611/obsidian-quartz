## Definition

**Viscosity** is a [[Property]] partly related to [[Van Der Waals Forces]], a [[Liquid]] resistance to flow. The stronger the intermolecular forces of attraction, the greater the viscosity. When a liquid flows, one portion of the liquid moves with respect to neighboring portions. [[Cohesive Force|Cohesive Forces]] within the liquid create an inter friction, which reduces the rate of flow. For liquids, viscosity decreases with increasing [[Temperature]], but for [[Gas|Gases]], the viscosity increases with increasing temperature.
