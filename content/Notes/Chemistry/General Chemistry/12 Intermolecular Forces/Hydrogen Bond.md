## Definition

The **hydrogen bond** is a specialized, relatively strong [[Dipole Dipole Interactions|Dipole Dipole Attraction]] between a hydrogen [[Atom]] [[Covalent Bond|Covalently Bonded]] to a highly [[Electronegativity|Electronegative]] atom  ($\ce{N}, \ce{O}, \ce{F}$)
