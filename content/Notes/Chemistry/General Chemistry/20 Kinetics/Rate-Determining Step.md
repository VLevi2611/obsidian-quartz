## Definition

The **rate-determining step** is the slowest [[Elementary Process]] in a multi-step [[Chemical Reaction]] mechanism. It acts as a bottleneck, meaning the [[Overall Reaction]] [[Rate of Reaction|Rate]] is entirely dependent on the speed of this single step. It has the [[Transition State]] of highest [[Activation Energy]] among all the steps in the [[Reaction Mechanism]].
