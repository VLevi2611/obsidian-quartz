## Definition

**Amphiprotic** species are chemical [[Substance|Substances]] that can both [[Proton Donor|Donate]] and [[Proton Acceptor|Accept]] a [[Proton]], allowing them to act as either a [[Bronsted Lowry theory|Bronsted Lowry]] [[Acid]] or [[Base]] depending on the [[Chemical Reaction|Reaction]]. [[Water]] is an example of an amphiprotic [[Molecule]].
