## Definition

A **conjugate acid** is a chemical species formed when a [[Bronsted Lowry theory|Bronsted Lowry]] [[Base]] [[Proton Acceptor|Accepts]] a [[Proton]]. It is a [[Product]] of a base gaining a hydrogen [[Ion]], effectively acting as an [[Acid]] in the reverse [[Chemical Reaction|Reaction]] by being capable of [[Proton Donor|Donating]] that proton back.
