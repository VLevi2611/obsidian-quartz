## Definition

The **degree of ionization, $\alpha$**, is the proportion of neutral [[Molecule|Molecules]] that [[Dissociation|Dissociate]] into [[Ion|Ions]] in a [[Solution]] or [[Gas]], calculated as the ratio of [[Ionization|Ionized]] molecules to the total molecules initially taken. It indicates the strength of [[Electrolytes]], with [[Strong Acid|Strong Acids]] and [[Strong Base|Strong Bases]] having a high $\alpha$ and [[Weak Acid|Weak Acids]] and [[Weak Base|Weak Bases]] having a low $\alpha$.
$$\alpha = \frac{\text{Number of ionized molecules}}{\text{Total number of molecules taken}}$$
