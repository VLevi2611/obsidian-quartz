## Definition

The **ion product $Q_{sp}$**, is the product of the [[Molarity|Molar]] [[Concentration]] of [[Ion|Ions]] in a [[Solution]] at any given moment, calculated similarly to the [[Equilibrium Constant]]. It is used to determine if a [[Precipitate]] will form $Q > K$, if the solution is [[Saturated Solution|Saturated]] $Q = K$, or [[Unsaturated Solution|Unsaturated]] $Q < K$
