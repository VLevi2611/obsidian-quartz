## Definition

**Fractional precipitation** is a technique in which 2 or more [[Ion|Ions]] is [[Solution]], each capable of being [[Precipitate|Precipitated]] by the same [[Reagent]], are separated by the proper use of that reagent: One ion is precipitated while the other(s) remains in solution. The primary condition for a successful fractional precipitation is that there be a significant difference in the [[Solubility]] of the [[Substance|Substances]] being separated. The key to the technique is the slow addition of a [[Concentration|Concentrated]] solution of the precipitating reagent to the solution from which precipitation is to occur, as from a burette.
