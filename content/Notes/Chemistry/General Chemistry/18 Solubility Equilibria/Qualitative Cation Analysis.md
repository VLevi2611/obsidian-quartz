## Definition

An analysis that aims at identifying the [[Cation|Cations]] present in a [[Mixture]] is called **qualitative cation analysis**. It provides us with many examples of [[Precipitate|Precipitations]], [[Equilibrium|Equilibria]], [[17 Acid-Base Equilibria|Acid-Base Equilibria]] and [[Oxidation Reduction]] [[Chemical Reaction|Reactions]].
