## Definition

The **ionic radius** is based on the distance between the [[Nucleus|Nuclei]] of [[Ion|Ions]] joined by an [[Ionic Bond]]. 
The nucleus draws the [[Electron|Electrons]] in closer, so [[Cation|Cations]] are smaller than the atoms from which they are formed, and [[Anion|Anions]] are bigger.
