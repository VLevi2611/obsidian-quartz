## Definition

If the [[Van Der Waals Forces]] between unlike [[Molecule|Molecules]] are more attractive than those between like molecules, a [[Solution]] forms. The [[Property|Properties]] of such solutions cannot be predicted, however, they are called **nonideal solutions**. Interactions between [[Solute]] and [[Solvent]] molecules release more [[Heat]] that the heat absorbed to separate the solvent and solute molecules. The solution process is an [[Exothermic Reaction]], $\Delta _{soln} H < 0$.
