## Definition

A [[System]] at **equilibrium** is in a state of balance. macroscopically, the [[Composition]] of the system is unchanging whereas, at the microscopic level, change continues. The overall composition of the system does not change because, at equilibrium, a balance has been achieved between 2 opposing processes. The conversion of small amounts of [[Reagent|Reagents]] into [[Product|Products]] is perfectly offset by the conversion of small amounts of products into reagents. This balance maintains the lowest possible value for the [[Gibbs Energy]] of the system and the maximum possible value for the combined [[Entropy]] of the system and its surroundings. Unless disturbed by an external influence, the system remains **indefinitely in this equilibrium state**.
