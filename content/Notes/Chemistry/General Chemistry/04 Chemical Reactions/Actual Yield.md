## Definition

The quantity of [[Product]] that is actually produced in a [[Chemical Reaction]] is called the **actual yield**.
In many reactions the actual yield almost exactly equals to the [[Theoretical Yield]], and the reactions are said to be quantitative. In other reaction the actual yield is less than the theoretical yield. the reduced yield may occur for a few reasons:
1. The product of a reaction rarely appears in a pure form, and some product may be lost during the purification steps.
2. In many cases the [[Reagent|Reagents]] may participate in reactions other than the one of interest, these are called **side reactions**.
3. If a reverse reaction occurs some of the expected product may react to reform the reagents.
