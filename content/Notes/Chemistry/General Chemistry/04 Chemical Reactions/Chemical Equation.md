## Definition

A **chemical equation** is a way of representing a [[Chemical Reaction]]. In a chemical equation formulas for the [[Reagent|Reagents]] are written on the let side and formulas for the [[Product|Products]] are written on the right. The two sides of the equation are joined by an arrow ($\rightarrow$) . We say that the reagents **yield** the products.

In a **balanced equation**, the total number of [[Atom|Atoms]] of each [[Element]] present is the same on both sides.

==An equation can be balanced only by adjusting the [[Stoichiometric Coefficients]] of the formulas.==
