## Definition

The term **stoichiometry** means to measure the [[Element|Elements]], but from a practical standpoint, using [[Stoichiometric Coefficients]]. It includes all the quantitative relationships involving [[Atomic Mass]], [[Chemical Formula]] and [[Chemical Equation]].
The coefficients in the chemical equation allow us to make statements such as:
- 2 [[Mole|Moles]] of $\ce{H2O}$ are [[Product|Produced]] for every 2 moles of $\ce{H2}$ [[Reagent]] consumed.
- 2 moles of $\ce{H2O}$ are produced for every 1 mole of $\ce{O2}$ reagent consumed.
- 2 moles of $\ce{H2}$ are consumed for every 2 moles of $\ce{O2}$ reagent consumed.
