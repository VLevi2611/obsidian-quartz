## Definition

Displacement $\mathbf{x}$ is a [[Vector]] whose [[Length]] is the shortest distance between 2 points ([[Absolute Value]]).
Displacement is the integral of [[Velocity]].
$$\mathbf{x}=\int\mathbf{v}dt$$
## Units

meters $$(m)$$
