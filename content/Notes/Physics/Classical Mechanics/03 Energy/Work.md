## Definition

Work $W$ is the transfer of [[Energy]] to or from an object via the application of a [[Force]] along a [[Displacement]].
Work is the [[Dot Product]] of force and displacement. Therefore, it is a [[Scalar]].

$$W = \mathbf{F \cdot x}$$

## Units

Joules $$(J)$$
