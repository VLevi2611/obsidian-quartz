## 🔗 Cross-Disciplinary Connections (Biomedical Engineering)
- **Biomechanics:** Connects [[Joint]], [[Bone]], and [[Muscle]] dynamics to *Classical Mechanics* (Torque, Forces).
- **Bio-electricity:** Connects [[Nerve]] and [[Cardiovascular System]] to *Electrical Circuits* and *Action Potentials*.
- **Fluid Dynamics:** Connects [[Blood Vessels]] and [[Artery]] flow to *Fluid Mechanics* (Poiseuille's Law, Shear Stress).
