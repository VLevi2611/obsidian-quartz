## Definition

The **pubic symphysis** is a secondary [[Solid Joint|Solid]] [[Joint]] in the [[Pubis]]. It is in front of and below the [[Urinary Bladder]]. In males, the suspensory [[Ligament]] of the [[Penis]] attaches to the pubic symphysis, and in females, to the suspensory ligament of the [[Clitoris]].
