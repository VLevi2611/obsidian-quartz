The **pubic symphysis** (pl.: **symphyses**) is a secondary [cartilaginous joint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartilaginous_joint "Cartilaginous joint")between the left and right [superior rami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superior_pubic_ramus "Superior pubic ramus") of the pubis of the [hip bones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pubis_\(bone\) "Pubis (bone)"). It is in front of and below the [urinary bladder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urinary_bladder "Urinary bladder"). In males, the [suspensory ligament of the penis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suspensory_ligament_of_the_penis "Suspensory ligament of the penis")attaches to the pubic symphysis. In females, the pubic symphysis is attached to the [suspensory ligament of the clitoris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suspensory_ligament_of_the_clitoris "Suspensory ligament of the clitoris"). In most adults, it can be moved roughly 2 mm and with 1 degree rotation. This increases for women at the time of childbirth.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pubic_symphysis#cite_note-1)
