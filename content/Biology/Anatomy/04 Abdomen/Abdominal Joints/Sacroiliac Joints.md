## Definition

The **sacroiliac joint** is the [[Joint]] between the [[Sacrum]] and the [[Ilium]] [[Bone|Bones]] of the [[Pelvis]], which are connected by strong [[Ligament|Ligaments]]. The sacrum supports the [[Spinal Column]] and is supported by an ilium on each side. There are 2 sacroiliac joints in the body, one on each side.
