## Definition

The **sacroiliac joint** is the [[Joint]] between the [[Sacrum]] and the [[Ilium]] [[Bone|Bones]] of the [[Pelvis]], which are connected by strong [[Ligament|Ligaments]]. The sacrum supports the [[Spinal Column]] and is supported by an ilium on each side

The **sacroiliac joint** or **SI joint** (**SIJ**) is the [joint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint "Joint") between the [sacrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacrum "Sacrum") and the [ilium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilium_\(bone\) "Ilium (bone)") bones of the [pelvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pelvis "Pelvis"), which are connected by strong [ligaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ligaments "Ligaments"). In humans, the sacrum supports the [spine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertebral_column "Vertebral column") and is supported in turn by an ilium on each side. The joint is strong, supporting the entire weight of the upper body. It is a [synovial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synovial_joint "Synovial joint")[plane joint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_joint "Plane joint") with irregular elevations and depressions that produce interlocking of the two bones.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacroiliac_joint#cite_note-pmid13478452-1) The human body has two **sacroiliac** joints, one on the left and one on the right, that often match each other but are highly variable from person to person.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacroiliac_joint#cite_note-pmid13478452-1)
