## Definition

The **sacrococcygeal joint** is a [[Joint]] formed between the oval surface at the apex of the [[Sacrum]] and the base of the [[Coccyx]]. It is a slightly [[Movement|Moveable]] joint, which is frequently, partially or completely, obliterated in old age.
