## Definition

The **facet joints** are a set of [[Synovial Joint|Sy]]

The **facet joints** (also **zygapophysial joints**, **zygapophyseal**, **apophyseal**, or **Z-joints**) are a set of [synovial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synovial_joint "Synovial joint"), [plane joints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_joint "Plane joint") between the [articular processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Articular_processes "Articular processes") of two adjacent [vertebrae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertebra "Vertebra"). There are two facet joints in each [spinal motion segment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_spinal_unit "Functional spinal unit")and each facet joint is innervated by the [recurrent meningeal nerves.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meningeal_branches_of_spinal_nerve "Meningeal branches of spinal nerve")
