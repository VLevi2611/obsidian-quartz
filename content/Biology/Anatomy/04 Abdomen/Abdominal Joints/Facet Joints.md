## Definition

The **facet joints** are a set of [[Synovial Joint|Synovial]] [[Joint|Joints]] between the articular processes of 2 adjacent [[Vertebra|Vertebrae]]. They guide [[Spinal Column|Spinal]] [[Movement]], and bear weight.
