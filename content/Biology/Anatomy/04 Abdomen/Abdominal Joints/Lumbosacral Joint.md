## Definition

The **lumbosacral joint** is a [[Joint]] between the last [[Lumbar Vertebrae|Lumbar Vertebra]] and the base of the [[Sacrum]]. It includes an [[Intervertebral Discs|Intervertebral Disc]] and 2 [[Facet Joints]] that help transfer upper body weight to the [[Pelvis]].
