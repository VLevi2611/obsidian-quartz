## Definition

The **transversus abdominis** is the deepest layer of front and side [[Muscle|Muscles]] in the abdomen, wraps horizontally around the  [[Ribs]] and[[Trunk]]. It protects and supports the lower back and [[Pelvis]] and compresses internal [[Organ|Organs]] and breathing.
