## Definition

The **rectus abdominis** is a pair of segmented [[Muscle|Muscles]] on the anterior aspect of the abdomen. The paired muscle is separated at the midline by a band of [[Tissue|Connective Tissue]] called the [[Linea Alba]], and the connective tissue defining each lateral margin of the rectus abdominis is called the **linea semilunaris**. The muscle extends from the [[Pubis]] inferiorly, to the [[Xiphoid Process]] and the 5-7 [[Ribs]] superiorly. It is contained in the [[Rectus Sheath]].

It is responsible for [[Movement|Flexing]] the [[Spinal Column]]. The [[Rib Cage]] is brought up to where the [[Pelvis]] is or the pelvis is brought toward the rib cage. It assists with respiration, helps in keeping internal [[Organ|Organs]] intact and in creating intra-abdominal [[Pressure]].
