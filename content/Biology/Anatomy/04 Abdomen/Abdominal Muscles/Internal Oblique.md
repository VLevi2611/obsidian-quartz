## Definition

The **internal oblique** is a deep [[Muscle]] on the side of the abdomen that sits under the [[External Oblique]] and above the [[Transversus Abdominis]]. It originates from the [[Hip Bone]] and inserts into the lower [[Ribs]] (10-12) and the [[Linea Alba]]. It helps [[Movement|Rotate]] the [[Spinal Column]] to the same side as the working muscle, bends the [[Trunk]] forward when both sides work together, or to the side when only one side works. It also compresses the abdomen [[Organ|Organs]] to help push air out during heavy breathing, and supports core stability.
