## Definition

The **linea alba** is a fibrous midline structure of the anterior abdominal wall, situated between the [[Rectus Abdominis]]. It attaches to the [[Xiphoid Process]] superiorly, and to the [[Pubis]] inferiorly. It stabilizes the anterior abdominal wall.
