## Definition

The **external oblique** is the largest and outermost [[Muscle]] on the side of the abdomen. It attaches to the outer surface of the lower [[Ribs]] (5-12), and inserts into the [[Hip Bone]] and the [[Linea Alba]]. It help turning the [[Chest]] to the opposite side when used on just one side. It helps tilt the body sideways and [[Movement|Flexes]] the [[Trunk]] forward and squeezes the belly to help with breathing out and pushing.
