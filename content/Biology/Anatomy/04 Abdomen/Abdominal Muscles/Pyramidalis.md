## Definition

The **pyramidalis** is a small triangular [[Muscle]], anterior to the [[Rectus Abdominis]], and contained in the [[Rectus Sheath]]. It is a part of the anterior abdominal wall. I





The **pyramidalis muscle** is a small triangular [muscle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle "Muscle"), anterior to the [rectus abdominis muscle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectus_abdominis_muscle "Rectus abdominis muscle"), and contained in the [rectus sheath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectus_sheath "Rectus sheath").

## Structure

The pyramidalis muscle is part of the anterior abdominal wall.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramidalis_muscle#cite_note-1) Inferiorly, the pyramidalis muscle attaches to the [pelvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pelvis "Pelvis") in two places: the [pubic symphysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pubic_symphysis "Pubic symphysis") and [pubic crest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pubic_crest "Pubic crest"), arising by tendinous fibers from the anterior part of the [pubis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pubis_\(bone\) "Pubis (bone)") and the [anterior pubic ligament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pubic_symphysis "Pubic symphysis").

Superiorly, the fleshy portion of the pyramidalis muscle passes upward, diminishing in size as it ascends, and ends by a pointed extremity which is inserted into the [linea alba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linea_alba_\(abdomen\) "Linea alba (abdomen)"), midway between the [umbilicus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navel "Navel") and [pubis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pubis_\(bone\) "Pubis (bone)").
