## Definition

The **pyramidalis** is a small triangular [[Muscle]], anterior to the [[Rectus Abdominis]], and contained in the [[Rectus Sheath]]. It is a part of the anterior abdominal wall. Anteriorly, it attaches to the [[Pelvis]] and superiorly, inserted into the [[Linea Alba]].
