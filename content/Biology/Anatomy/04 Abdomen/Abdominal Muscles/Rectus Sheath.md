## Definition

The **rectus sheath** is a strong fibrous sleeve in the front of the abdomen that encloses the [[Rectus Abdominis]] and [[Pyramidalis]]. It protects these core [[Muscle|Muscles]] and holds vital [[Blood Vessels]] and [[Nerve|Nerves]] in place. It originates from the [[External Oblique]], [[Internal Oblique]] and [[Transversus Abdominis]], then join at the [[Linea Alba]].
