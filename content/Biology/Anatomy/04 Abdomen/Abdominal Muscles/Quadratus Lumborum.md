## Definition

The **quadratus lumborum** is a paired [[Muscle]] of the posterior abdominal wall. It originates from the wings of the [[Ilium]], inserts on the upper 4 [[Lumbar Vertebrae]] and on the 12th [[Ribs|Rib]]. It flexes the [[Spinal Column]] and elevates the [[Pelvis]].
