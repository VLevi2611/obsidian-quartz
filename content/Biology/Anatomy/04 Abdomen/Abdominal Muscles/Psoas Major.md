## Definition

The **psoas major** is a long [[Muscle]] located in the lateral lumbar region between the [[Spinal Column]] and the brim of the [[Pelvis]]. It joins the [[Iliacus]] to form the [[Iliopsoas]]. It is divided into a superficial and deep part.
