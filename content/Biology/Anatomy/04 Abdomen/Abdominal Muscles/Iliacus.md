## Definition

The **iliacus** is a flat, triangular [[Muscle]] that forms the lateral portion of the [[Iliopsoas]], providing [[Movement|Flexion]] of the [[Thigh]] and the [[02 Lower Limb|Lower Limb]]. It inserts into the [[Femur]].
