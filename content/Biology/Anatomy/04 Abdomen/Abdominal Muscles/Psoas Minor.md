## Definition

The **psoas minor** is a long, slender [[Muscle]], located anterior to the [[Psoas Major]]. It originates from the [[Lumbar Vertebrae|Lumbar Vertebra]] L1 and the [[Thoracic Vertebrae|Thoracic Vertebra]] T12 and inserts into the [[Ilium]]. It is a weak [[Movement|Flexor]] of the [[Spinal Column]].
