## Overview 

The [[Bone|Bony]] framework of the abdomen is primarily concentrated in the posterior and inferior regions, providing a musculoskeletal axis of support for the [[Trunk]] and protecting vital internal [[Organ|Organs]]. While the anterior and lateral walls are predominantly [[Muscle|Muscular]], they are anchored to specific bony landmarks of the [[Spinal Column]], [[Rib Cage]], and [[Pelvis]].

---

### Lumbar Spine (L1-L5)

The 5 [[Lumbar Vertebrae]] form the central skeletal support for the posterior abdominal wall.

- **Characteristics:** They are distinguished by their **massive size**, which is necessary to bear the weight of the upper body.
- **Structure:** Each lumbar vertebra features a large cylindrical **body (corpus)**, short stocky pedicles, and long slender transverse processes.
- **Curvature:** The lumbar region has a **secondary curvature** (forward convexity) that helps balance the [[Center Of Mass]] over the vertebral column for an upright stance.

### [[Pelvic Girdle]] (Superior Parts)

The upper regions of the pelvic bones define the inferior boundary of the abdominal wall.

- [[Ilium]]: The large, fan-shaped "wing" of the [[Hip Bone]]. The **iliac fossa** (its concave inner surface) and the **iliac crest** (its superior margin) provide major attachment points for abdominal muscles.
- [[Pelvis]]: The upper portion of the pelvis located above the **linea terminalis** is considered part of the abdominal cavity.
- [[Sacrum]]: A triangular bone formed by **five fused sacral [[Vertebra|Vertebrae]]**. It articulates with the lumbar spine at the [[Lumbosacral Joint]] and with the ilia at the **[[Sacro-Iliac Joint]]

### Superior Boundaries ([[03 Thorax|Thorax]])

The superior margin of the abdomen is defined by the **inferior thoracic aperture**, which is formed by several skeletal elements:

- [[Ribs]] 10-12: Known as **floating ribs**, these provide posterior protection for the superior poles of the kidneys.
- **Costal margin:** Formed by the cartilaginous ends of ribs 7-10, it separates the abdominal wall from the thoracic wall.
- **[[Xiphoid Process]]:** The smallest, inferior-most part of the [[Sternum]]; it serves as a superior midline anchor for the [[Linea Alba]] and abdominal muscles.
