## Definition

The **lumbar vertebrae** are located between the [[Thoracic Vertebrae]] and [[Sacrum]]. They form the lower part of the [[Back]]



The **lumbar vertebrae** are located between the [thoracic vertebrae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thoracic_vertebrae "Thoracic vertebrae") and [sacrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacrum "Sacrum"). They form the lower part of the [back](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_back "Human back") in humans, and the region of the spine between the rib cage and pelvis in quadrupedal mammals. In humans, there are typically five lumbar vertebrae. The term is used to describe the anatomy of humans as well as domestic [ungulates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ungulates "Ungulates") and other quadrupeds; the specific number varies by species, typically ranging from six in horses and cattle to six or seven in pigs.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumbar_vertebrae#cite_note-1) In commercial butchery, these vertebrae correspond to high-value cuts of meat, including the tenderloin and sirloin steak.

## Human anatomy

In human anatomy, the five lumbar [vertebrae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertebrae "Vertebrae") are between the [rib cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_rib_cage "Human rib cage") and the [pelvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_pelvis "Human pelvis"). They are the largest segments of the [vertebral column](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_vertebral_column "Human vertebral column") and are characterized by the absence of the [foramen transversarium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foramen_transversarium "Foramen transversarium") within the transverse process (since it is only found in the [cervical region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cervical_vertebrae "Cervical vertebrae")) and by the absence of facets on the sides of the body (as found only in the [thoracic region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thoracic_vertebrae "Thoracic vertebrae")). They are designated L1 to L5, starting at the top. The lumbar vertebrae help support the weight of the body, and permit movement.
