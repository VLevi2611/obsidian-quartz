## Definition

The 5 **lumbar vertebrae** are located between the [[Thoracic Vertebrae]] and [[Sacrum]]. They form the lower part of the [[Back]], and the region of the [[Spinal Column]] between the [[Rib Cage]] and the [[Pelvis]]. The lumbar vertebrae help support the weight of the body and allow [[Movement]].
