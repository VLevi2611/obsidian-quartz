## Definition

The **sacrum** is a triangular [[Bone]] at the base of the [[Spinal Column]], that forms by the fusing of the sacral [[Vertebra|Vertebrae]] (S1-S5) by adulthood. It situates between the 2 wings of the [[Pelvis]]. The upper part of the sacrum connects with the last [[Lumbar Vertebrae|Lumbar Vertebra]], and its lower part with the [[Coccyx]].
