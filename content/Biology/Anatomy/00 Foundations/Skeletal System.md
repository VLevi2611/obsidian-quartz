## Definition

The **skeletal system** can be divided into 2 subgroups, the axial skeleton and the appendicular skeleton. The **axial skeleton** consists of the bones of the skull (cranium), vertebral columns, ribs and sternum. The **appendicular skeleton** consists of the bones of the [[01 Upper Limb|Upper]] and [[02 Lower Limb|Lower Limbs]].
The skeletal system consists of [[Cartilage]] and [[Bone]].
