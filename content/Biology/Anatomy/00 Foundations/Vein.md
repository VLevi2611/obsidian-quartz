## Definition

A **vein** is a [[Blood Vessels|Blood Vessel]] in the circulatory system that carries [[Blood]] towards the [[Heart]]. Most veins carry deoxygenated blood from the [[Tissue|Tissues]] back to the heart. There are 3 sizes of veins: large, medium and small. Smaller veins are called **venules**, and the smallest of which are called post [[Capillary]] venules. Veins have less [[Muscle#Smooth Muscle|Smooth Muscle]] and connective tissue and wider internal diameters than [[Artery|Arteries]].
