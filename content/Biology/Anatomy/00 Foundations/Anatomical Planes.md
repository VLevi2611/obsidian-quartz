## Definition

Three major groups of **planes** pass through the body in the [[Anatomical Position]].
- **Coronal planes** are oriented vertically and divide the body into [[Anatomical Directions|Anterior and Posterior]] parts.
- **Sagittal planes** also oriented vertically but are at right angles to the coronal planes and divide the body into right and left parts. The plane that passes through the center of the body dividing it into equal right and left halves is termed the **median sagittal plane**.
- **Axial planes** divide the body into superior and inferior parts.
