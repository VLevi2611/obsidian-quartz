## Definition

A **tendon** is a tough band of [[Density|Dense]] fibrous connective [[Tissue]] that connects [[Muscle]] to [[Bone]]. It sends the [[Force|Forces]] of muscle contraction to the [[Skeletal System]], while withstanding [[Tension]]. There are about 4000 tendons in the human body.
