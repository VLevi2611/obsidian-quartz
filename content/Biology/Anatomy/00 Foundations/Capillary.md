## Definition

A **capillary** is a small [[Blood Vessels|Blood Vessel]], and is part of the **microcirculation system**. They are the smallest blood vessel and are composed only of the **tunica intima** (the innermost layer of an [[Artery]] or [[Vein]]), consisting of a thin wall of simple squamous **endothelial cells**.
