## Definition

The **anatomical directions** are used to describe location of structures relative to each other.

## Main Directions

- **Anterior (Ventral)** $\rightarrow$ front
- **Posterior (Dorsal)** $\rightarrow$ back
- **Superior (Cranial)** $\rightarrow$ above
- **Inferior (Caudal)** $\rightarrow$ below
- **Medial** $\rightarrow$ toward midline
- **Lateral** $\rightarrow$ away from midline

## Relative Terms

- **Proximal** $\rightarrow$ closer to trunk
- **Distal** $\rightarrow$ farther from trunk
- **Superficial** $\rightarrow$ closer to surface
- **Deep** $\rightarrow$ farther from surface

## Examples

- The hand is **distal** to the elbow  
- The sternum is **anterior** to the heart  
- The lungs are **lateral** to the heart
