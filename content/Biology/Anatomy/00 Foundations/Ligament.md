## Definition

A **ligament** is a short band of tough, flexible [[Tissue|Connective Tissue]] that bind 2 [[Bone|Bones]] or [[Cartilage|Cartilages]] together and hold [[Joint|Joints]] together. Their function is to provide reinforcement to the joint capsules and stability by tying articulating bones together.
