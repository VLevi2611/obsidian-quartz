## Definition

The **sternum** is a long flat [[Bone]], located in the central part of the [[Chest]]. It connects to the [[Ribs]] via [[Cartilage]] and forms the front of the [[Rib Cage]]. It helps protecting the [[Lung|Lungs]], [[Heart]] and major [[Blood Vessels]]. Its three regions are the [[Manubrium]], the [[Sternum Body]] and the [[Xiphoid Process]].
