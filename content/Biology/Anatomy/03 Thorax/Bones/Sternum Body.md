## Definition

The **sternum body** is the longest part of the [[Sternum]]. The [[Pectoralis Major]] attaches to it on either side. The upper border is oval and articulates with the [[Manubrium]], at the [[Sternal Angle]]. The lower border is narrow, and articulates with the [[Xiphoid Process]].
