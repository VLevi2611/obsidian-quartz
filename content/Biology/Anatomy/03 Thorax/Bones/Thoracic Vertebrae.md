## Definition

The **thoracic vertebrae** are the 12 individual [[Vertebra|Vertebrae]] (T1–T12) that make up the middle section of the [[Spinal Column]]. Situated between the [[Neck]] ([[Cervical Vertebrae]]) and the lower back ([[Lumbar Vertebrae]]), they are uniquely designed to connect with the [[Ribs]] to form the protective [[Rib Cage]].
