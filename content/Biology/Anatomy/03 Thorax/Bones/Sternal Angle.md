## Definition

The **sternal angle** is the area formed between the [[Manubrium]] and the [[Sternum Body]] in the [[Sternum]], at their junction at the [[Manubriosternal Joint]]. It identifies the 2nd [[Ribs|Rib]] and aligns the T4-T5 [[Intervertebral Discs|Intervertebral Disc]] posteriorly.
