## Definition

The **rib cage** is an endoskeletal enclosure in the [[03 Thorax|Thorax]] that comprises the [[Ribs]], [[Spinal Column]] and [[Sternum]], which protects vital [[Organ|Organs]] of the [[Thoracic Cavity]]. A human rib cage consists of 12 pairs of ribs, the sternum and the 12 [[Vertebrae]] articulating with the ribs.
