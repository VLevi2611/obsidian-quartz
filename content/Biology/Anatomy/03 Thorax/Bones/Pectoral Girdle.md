## Definition

The **pectoral girdle** is the bony ring that attaches the [[01 Upper Limb|Upper Limbs]] to the [[Skeletal System|Axial Skeleton]]. It consists of 2 [[Bone|Bones]] on each side: the [[Clavicle]] and the [[Scapula]]. It provides a critical anchor point for [[Muscle|Muscles]] and allow for a wide range of [[Movement]]. The pectoral girdle facilitates movement through several [[Joint|Joints]]: [[Sternoclavicular Joint]], [[Acromioclavicular Joint]], [[Shoulder Joint]].
