## Definition

The **spinal column** is a core part of the [[Skeletal System]]. It is a segmented column of 33 [[Vertebra|Vertebrae]] that surrounds and protects the [[Spinal Cord]]. The upper 24 vertebrae are separated by [[Intervertebral Discs]]. The other 9 are fused in adults, 5 in the [[Sacrum]] and 4 in the [[Coccyx]].
