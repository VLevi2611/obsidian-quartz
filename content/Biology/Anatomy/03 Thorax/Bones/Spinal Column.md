## Definition

The **spinal column** is a core part of the [[Skeletal System]]. It is a segmented column of 33 [[Vertebrae]] that surrounds and protects the [[Spinal Cord]]. These vertebrae are separated by [[Intervertebral Discs]].




Individual vertebrae are named according to their corresponding region including the [neck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neck "Neck"), [thorax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorax "Thorax"), [abdomen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdomen "Abdomen"), [pelvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pelvis "Pelvis") or [tail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tail "Tail"). In [clinical medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinical_medicine "Clinical medicine"), features on vertebrae such as the [spinous process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinous_process "Spinous process") can be used as [surface landmarks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_anatomy "Surface anatomy") to guide [medical procedures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_procedure "Medical procedure") such as [lumbar punctures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumbar_puncture "Lumbar puncture") and [spinal anesthesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinal_anesthesia "Spinal anesthesia"). There are also many different [spinal diseases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinal_disease "Spinal disease") in humans that can affect both the bony vertebrae and the intervertebral discs, with [kyphosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyphosis "Kyphosis"), [scoliosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scoliosis "Scoliosis"), [ankylosing spondylitis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankylosing_spondylitis "Ankylosing spondylitis"), and [degenerative discs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degenerative_disc_disease "Degenerative disc disease") being recognizable examples. [Spina bifida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spina_bifida "Spina bifida") is the most common [birth defect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_defect "Birth defect") that affects the spinal column.
