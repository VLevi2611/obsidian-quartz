## Definition

**Ribs** are the long curved [[Bone|Bones]] which form the [[Rib Cage]] and protect internal [[Organ|Organs]]. Humans have 24 ribs, in 12 pairs, all attached at the back of the [[Vertebrae]]. Ribs connect to vertebrae at the [[Costovertebral Joints]]. A typical rib includes a **head** (posterior end), **neck**, **tubercle** (for articulation with vertebral transverse processes), and a curved **shaft** containing a **costal groove** for nerves and vessels.
