## Definition

**Ribs** are the long curved [[Bone|Bi]]


In [vertebrate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertebrate "Vertebrate") [anatomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatomy "Anatomy"), **ribs** ([Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_language "Latin language"): _costae_) are the long curved [bones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bone "Bone") which form the [rib cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rib_cage "Rib cage"), part of the [axial skeleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axial_skeleton "Axial skeleton").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rib#cite_note-1) In most [tetrapods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrapod "Tetrapod"), ribs surround the [thoracic cavity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thoracic_cavity "Thoracic cavity"), enabling the lungs to expand and thus facilitate [breathing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breathing "Breathing") by expanding the thoracic cavity. They serve to protect the [lungs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lung "Lung"), [heart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart "Heart"), and other [vital organs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vital_organ "Vital organ") of the thorax. In some animals, especially [snakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snake "Snake"), ribs may provide support and protection for the entire body.
