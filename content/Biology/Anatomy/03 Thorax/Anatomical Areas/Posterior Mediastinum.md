## Definition

The **posterior mediastinum** is the space in the thorax bounded anteriorly by the [[Pericardium]], posteriorly by the T5-T12 [[Vertebra|Vertebrae]] and laterally by the **parietal pleura**. It serves as a vital transition zone for major [[Blood Vessels]] and [[Nerve|Nerves]]
