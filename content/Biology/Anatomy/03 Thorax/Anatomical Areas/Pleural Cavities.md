## Definition

The **pleural cavities** are 2 fluid-filled, potential space between the 2 [[Membrane|Membranes]] (**visceral and parietal pleura**) that surround the [[Lung|Lungs]]. They contain a thin layer of **serous fluid** that lubricates the lungs and create [[Surface Tension]], allowing them to expand smoothly with the [[Chest]] wall during breathing.
