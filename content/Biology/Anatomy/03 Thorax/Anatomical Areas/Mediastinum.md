## Definition

The **mediastinum** is the central compartment of the [[Thoracic Cavity]]. Surrounded by loose [[Tissue|Connective Tissue]], it contains vital organs and structures within the thorax, mainly the [[Heart]] and its [[Blood Vessels]], the [[Esophagus]], [[Trachea]] and [[Thymus]].
