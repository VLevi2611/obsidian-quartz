## Definition

The **superior mediastinum** is the upper compartment of the [[Thoracic Cavity]]. It acts as a vital conduit for structures passing between the [[Neck]] and [[Chest]], housing the [[Trachea]], [[Esophagus]] [[Thymus]] and major [[Nerve|Nerves]] and [[Blood Vessels]].
