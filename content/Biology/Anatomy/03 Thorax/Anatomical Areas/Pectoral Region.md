## Definition

The **pectoral region** is the anterior [[Chest]] area connecting the [[Trunk]] to the [[01 Upper Limb|Upper Limbs]]. It houses the [[Breast|Breasts]] and 4 main [[Muscle|Muscles]]- [[Pectoralis Major]], [[Pectoralis Minor]], [[Subclavius]] and [[Serratus Anterior]]. They are responsible for [[Shoulder]] stability, [[Arm]] [[Movement]] and [[Respiratory System|Respiration]].
