## Definition

The **interchondral joints** are small [[Synovial Joint|Synovial]] [[Joint|Joints]] between the adjacent tops of the costal [[Cartilage|Cartilage]] of [[Ribs]] 6-10. They link these ribs together to form the costal margin, providing structural stability while allowing the flexibility and expansion of the [[Rib Cage]] for breathing.
