## Definition

An **intervertebral disc** lies between adjacent [[Vertebra|Vertebrae]] in the [[Spinal Column]]. Each disc forms a **fibrocartilaginous [[Joint]]**, to allow slight [[Movement]] of the vertebrae, to act as a [[Ligament]] to hold vertebrae together, and to function as a shock absorber for the spine. Discs are named by the vertebral body above and below. For example, the disc between the fifth and sixth [[Cervical Vertebrae]] is designated "C5-6".
