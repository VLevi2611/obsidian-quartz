## Definition

The **thoracic zygapophyseal joints** are paired [[Synovial Joint|Synovial]] [[Joint|Joints]] connecting the articular processes of adjacent [[Vertebra|Vertebrae]] in the [[Back]]. They provide structural stability and guide the [[Spinal Column|Spine]] [[Movement]].
