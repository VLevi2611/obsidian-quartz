## Definition

The **costotransverse joints** are [[Joint|Joints]] that connect the [[Ribs|Rib Tubercle]] to the adjacent transverse process of the [[Spinal Column]]. Found on the first 10 ribs, these [[Synovial Joint|Synovial Joints]] facilitate gliding [[Movement|Movements]] for breathing.
