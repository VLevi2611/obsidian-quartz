The **internal intercostal muscles** (**intercostales interni**) are a group of [skeletal muscles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skeletal_muscle "Skeletal muscle") located between the [ribs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rib "Rib"). They are eleven in number on either side. They commence anteriorly at the [sternum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_sternum "Human sternum"), in the [intercostal spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intercostal_space "Intercostal space")between the [cartilages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartilage "Cartilage") of the [true ribs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_ribs "True ribs"), and at the anterior extremities of the cartilages of the [false ribs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False_ribs "False ribs"), and extend backward as far as the angles of the ribs, hence they are continued to the [vertebral column](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertebral_column "Vertebral column") by thin [aponeuroses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aponeuroses "Aponeuroses"), the posterior intercostal membranes. They pull the sternum and ribs upward and inward.
