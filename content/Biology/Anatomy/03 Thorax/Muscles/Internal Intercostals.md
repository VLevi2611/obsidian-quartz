## Definition

The **internal intercostals** are a group of 11 pairs of [[Muscle|Muscles]] located between the [[Ribs]]. They originate anteriorly at the [[Sternum]] between the [[Cartilage|Cartilages]] of the true ribs, and at the anterior extremities of the cartilages of the false ribs, and extend backward as far as the angles of the ribs. They are continued to the [[Spinal Column]] and pull the sternum and ribs upward and inward.
