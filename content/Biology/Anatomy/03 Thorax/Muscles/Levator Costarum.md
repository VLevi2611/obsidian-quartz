## Definition

The **levator costarum** are 12 pairs of [[Muscle|Muscles]] which arise from the [[Thoracic Vertebrae]]. They pass obliquely downward and laterally, like the fibers of the [[External Intercostals]], and each is inserted into the outer surface of the [[Ribs|Rib]], below the [[Vertebra]] from which it takes origin, between the tubercle and the angle. They have a role in expiration.
