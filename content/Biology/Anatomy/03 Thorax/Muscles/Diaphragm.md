## Definition

The **diaphragm** is a sheet of internal skeletal [[Muscle]] that extends across the bottom of the [[Pleural Cavities]]. It is the most important muscle in the [[Respiratory System]]. As the diaphragm contracts, the [[Volume]] of the thoracic cavity increases, creating a negative [[Pressure]] there, which draws air into the [[Lung|Lungs]].
