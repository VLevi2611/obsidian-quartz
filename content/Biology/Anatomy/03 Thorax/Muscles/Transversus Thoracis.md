## Definition

The **transversus thoracis** is a [[Muscle]] that les internal to the [[Rib Cage]]. It is in the same layer as the [[Subcostales]] and the [[Innermost Intercostals]]. It acts as a weak depressor of the [[Ribs]] during expiration.
