## Definition

The **subcostales** are [[Muscle|Muscles]] that are usually well-developed only in the lower part of the [[03 Thorax|Thorax]]. Each originates from the inner surface of one [[Ribs|Rib]] and inserts into the inner surface of the second or third rib below, near its angle. They depress the ribs to assist in expiration.
