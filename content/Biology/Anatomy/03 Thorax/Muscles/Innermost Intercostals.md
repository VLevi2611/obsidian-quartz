## Definition

The **innermost intercostal** are the deepest [[Muscle]] layer of the intercostal muscles, located between the [[Ribs]]. They span the middle portions of the intercostal spaces, running parallel to the [[Internal Intercostals]] to help depress the ribs during forced expiration and stabilize the [[Rib Cage]].
