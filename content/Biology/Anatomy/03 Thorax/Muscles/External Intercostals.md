## Definition

The **external intercostals** are 11 pairs of [[Muscle|Muscles]] that extends from the tubercles of the [[Ribs]], to the [[Cartilage|Cartilages]] of the ribs in front, where they end in thin [[Membrane|Membranes]], that continued forward to the [[Sternum]]. These muscles work when inhalation occurs. The [[Internal Intercostals]] relax while the external muscles contract causing the expansion of the [[Chest]] cavity and an influx of air into the [[Lung|Lungs]].
