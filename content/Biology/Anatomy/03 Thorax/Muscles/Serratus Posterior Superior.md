## Definition

The **serratus posterior superior** is a thin [[Muscle]], situated at the upper back part of the [[03 Thorax|Thorax]], deep to the [[Rhomboid Major]] and [[Rhomboid Minor]]. It elevates the 2-5 [[Ribs]] and aids in deep respiration.
