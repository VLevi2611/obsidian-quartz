The **serratus posterior superior muscle** (or **musculus serratus dorsalis cranialis**) is a thin, quadrilateral muscle. It is situated at the upper back part of the [thorax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorax "Thorax"), deep to the [rhomboid muscles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhomboid_muscles "Rhomboid muscles").
The serratus posterior superior muscle elevates the second to fifth ribs.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_] This aids deep respiration.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_]
