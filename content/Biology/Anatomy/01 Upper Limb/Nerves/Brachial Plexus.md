## Definition

The **brachial plexus** is a network of [[Nerve|Nerves]] formed by the anterior rami of spinal nerves **C5-T1**, anatomically structured into **R**oots, **T**runks, **D**evisions, **C**ords, and **B**ranches. They transition from the neck interscalene triangle into the axilla to provide full  motor and sensory innervation the the upper extremity. The network culminates in  5 **terminal branches**:
1. [[Musculocutaneous Nerve]] **C5-C7**
2. [[Axillary Nerve]]  **C5-C6**
3. [[Radial Nerve]] **C5-T1**
4. [[Median Nerve]] **C5-T1**
5. [[Ulnar Nerve]] **C8-T1**
