## Definition

The **axillary nerve** is a [[Nerve]] that originates from the [[Brachial Plexus]] at the level of the **axilla** (armpit) and carries nerve fibers from **C5,C6**. It innervates the [[Deltoid]] and [[Teres Minor]]. It crosses the [[Subscapularis]] and [[Humerus]]
