## Definition

The **musculocutaneous nerve** is a [[Nerve#Mixed Nerves|Mixed Nerve]] branch of the lateral cord of the [[Brachial Plexus]] derived from cervical spinal nerves **C5-C7**. It arises opposite to the lower border of the [[Pectoralis Minor]]. It provides motor innervation to the [[Muscle|Muscles]]
