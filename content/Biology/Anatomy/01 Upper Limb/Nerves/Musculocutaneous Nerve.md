## Definition

The **musculocutaneous nerve** is a [[Nerve#Mixed Nerves|Mixed Nerve]] branch of the lateral cord of the [[Brachial Plexus]] derived from cervical spinal nerves **C5,C7**. It arises opposite to the lower border of the [[Pectoralis Minor]]. It provides motor innervation to the [[Muscle|Muscles]] of the anterior compartment of the [[Arm]]: the [[Coracobrachialis]], [[Biceps Brachii]], and [[Brachialis]]. It provides sensory innervation to the lateral [[Forearm]].
