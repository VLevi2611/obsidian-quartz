## Definition

The **shoulder** is the area where the upper limb attaches to the trunk. Its skeletal framework includes the [[Clavicle]] and [[Scapula]], which form the [[Pectoral Girdle]], and the proximal end of the [[Humerus]]. It is suspended from the trunk predominantly by [[Muscle|Muscles]] like the [[Trapezius]] and [[Deltoid]], allowing for significant [[Movement]] of the scapula on the [[Thoracic Bones]] to extend the reach of the [[Hand]].
