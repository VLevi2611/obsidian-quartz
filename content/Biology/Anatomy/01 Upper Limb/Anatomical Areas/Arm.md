## Definition

The **arm** is the portion of the limb located between the [[Shoulder]] and the [[Elbow Joint]]. Its primary skeletal support is the shaft of the [[Humerus]]. The arm is divided by intermuscular septa into **[[Anatomical Directions|Anterior]] (flexor)** and **posterior (extensor)** compartments. [[Muscle|Muscles]] in the anterior compartment, such as the [[Biceps Brachii]], primarily flex the elbow, while the posterior compartment contains the [[Triceps Brachii]], which extends it.
