## Definition

The **hand** is the region of the limb [[Anatomical Directions|Distal]] to the [[Wrist]] and is subdivided into the [[Carpals]], [[Metacarpals]] (palm), and [[Phalanges]] (fingers). It serves as a sophisticated mechanical tool for gripping objects and a powerful sensory tool for touch, with a high [[Density]] of receptors in the finger pads. The thumb is oriented at a right angle to the fingers, allowing for **opposition**, which is a fundamental requirement for precise manipulation.
