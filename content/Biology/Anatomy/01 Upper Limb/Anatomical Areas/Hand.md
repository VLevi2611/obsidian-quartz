## Definition

The **hand** is the region of the limb [[Anatomical Directions]] to the [[Wrist]] and is subdivided into the [[carp]], **metacarpus (palm)**, and **digits**. It serves as a sophisticated **mechanical tool** for gripping objects and a powerful **sensory tool** for touch, with a high density of receptors in the finger pads. The thumb is oriented at a right angle to the fingers, allowing for **opposition**, which is a fundamental requirement for precise manipulation.
