## Definition

The **wrist** acts as the transition zone between the [[Forearm]] and the [[Hand]]. It is formed by the [[Joint]] between the [[Anatomical Directions|Distal]] end of the [[Radius]], an articular disc, and a proximal row of **carpal bones**. The wrist contains the **carpal tunnel**, a critical gateway through which the median nerve and long flexor tendons pass into the palm. This joint allows the hand to be flexed, extended, abducted, and adducted relative to the forearm.
