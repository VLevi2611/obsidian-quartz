## Definition

The **wrist** acts as the transition zone between the [[Forearm]] and the [[Hand]]. It is formed by the [[Joint]] between the [[Anatomical Directions|Distal]] end of the [[Radius]], and a proximal row of [[Carpals]]. The wrist contains the [[Carpal Tunnel]], a critical gateway through which the [[Median Nerve]] and long flexor [[Tendon|Tendons]] pass into the palm. This joint allows the hand to be [[Movement|Flexed]], extended, abducted, and adducted relative to the forearm.
