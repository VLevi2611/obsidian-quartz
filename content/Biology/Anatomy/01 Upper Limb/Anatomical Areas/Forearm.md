## Definition

The **forearm** extends between the [[Elbow Joint]] and the [[Wrist]]. It contains two parallel [[Bone|Bones]]: the [[Radius]] ([[Anatomical Directions|Lateral]]) and the [[Ulna]] (medial). This region is unique because the radius can flip over the ulna, a [[Movement]] known as **pronation**, which turns the palm posteriorly; **supination** returns the hand to the anatomical position. These movements are essential for rotating the hand without moving the shoulder or arm.
