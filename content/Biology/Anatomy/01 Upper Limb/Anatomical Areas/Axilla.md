## Definition

The **axilla** (armpit) is the anatomical region directly under the [[Shoulder Joint]] where the [[Arm]] connects to the torso. It acts as an essential passageway for [[Nerve|Nerves]], [[Blood Vessels]] and [[Lymph|Lymph Nodes]] traveling between the neck, chest and upper limbs.
