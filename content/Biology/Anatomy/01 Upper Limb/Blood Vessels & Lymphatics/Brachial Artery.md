## Definition

The **brachial artery** is a major [[Artery]] supplying oxygen-rich [[Blood]] to the upper [[Arm]], [[Elbow Joint|Elbow]], [[Forearm]] and [[Hand]]. It begins as a continuation of the [[Axillary Artery]], and runs down the inner [[Biceps Brachii|Bicep]]
