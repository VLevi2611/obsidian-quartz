## Definition

The **radial artery** is a major [[Artery]]. It supplies oxygenated [[Blood]] to the [[Forearm]], [[Wrist]] and [[Hand]]. It is the primary site for taking a pulse. It originates from the [[Brachial Artery]] below the [[Elbow Joint]]. It runs down the lateral side of the forearm in front of the distal end of the [[Radius]] and enters the hand to form the [[Deep Palmar Arch]].
