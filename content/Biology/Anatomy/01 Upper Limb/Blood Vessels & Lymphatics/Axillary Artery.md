## Definition

The **axillary artery** is a major [[Blood Vessels|Blood Vessel]] that supplies oxygen-rich [[Blood]] to the [[Shoulder]], armpit, and upper limb. It is a direct continuation of the [[Subclavian Artery]], beginning at the first rib, going behind the [[Pectoralis Minor]], and transitioning into the [[Brachial Artery]] at the lower border of the [[Teres Major]].
