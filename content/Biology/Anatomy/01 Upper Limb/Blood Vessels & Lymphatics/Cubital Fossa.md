## Definition

The **cubital fossa** is a triangular depression located on the anterior side of the [[Elbow Joint]]. It serves as the primary transition zone between the upper [[Arm]] and the [[Forearm]], housing major [[Blood Vessels]] and [[Nerve|Nerves]] and serving as the standard site for routine medical procedures like drawing [[Blood]]. The superior border of the cubital fossa is connected to the [[Humerus]], the lateral border is on the medial edge
