## Definition

The **ulnar artery** is the [[Artery]], with oxygenated [[Blood]], in the medial aspects of the [[Forearm]]. It arises from the [[Brachial Artery]] and terminates in the [[Superficial Palmar Arch]], which joins with the superficial branch of the [[Radial Artery]]. It begins below the [[Elbow Joint]] in the [[Cubital Fossa]], and running down to the [[Wrist]].
