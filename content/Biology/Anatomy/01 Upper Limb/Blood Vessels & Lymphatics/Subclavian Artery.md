## Definition

The **subclavian arteries** are paired major [[Artery|Arteries]] of the upper thorax, below the [[Clavicle]]. They receive [[Blood]] from the aortic arch. The left subclavian artery supplies blood to the left [[Arm]] and the right one supplies one to the right arm, with some branches supplying the head and thorax.
