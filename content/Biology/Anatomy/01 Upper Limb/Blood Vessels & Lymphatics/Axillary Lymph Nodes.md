## Definition

**Axillary lymph nodes** are a critical part of the [[Immune System]]. There are 20-40 nodes located in each [[Axilla]], arranged to filter [[Lymph]] fluid draining from the breasts, chest wall, upper abdomen and [[Arm|Arms]].
