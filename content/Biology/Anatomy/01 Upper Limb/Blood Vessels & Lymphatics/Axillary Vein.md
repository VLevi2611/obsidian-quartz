## Definition

The **axillary vein** is a large, deep [[Vein]] located in the armpit that carries deoxygenated [[Blood]] from the upper limbs, axilla, and lateral chest wall back to the [[Heart]]. It begins at the lower border of the [[Teres Major]] as a continuation of the [[Basilic Vein]], travels medially through the axilla, sitting alongside and medial to the [[Axillary Artery]]. It terminates at the outer border of the first rib, transitions into the [[Subclavian Vein]].
