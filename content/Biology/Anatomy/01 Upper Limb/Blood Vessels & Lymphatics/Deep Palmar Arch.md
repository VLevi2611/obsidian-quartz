## Definition

The **deep palmar arch** is a [[Artery|Arterial]] network in the [[Hand]], formed by the [[Radial Artery]] connecting with the deep palmar branch of the [[Ulnar Artery]]. It sits deep within the palm's [[Muscle|Muscles]] and supplies oxygenated [[Blood]] to the hand
