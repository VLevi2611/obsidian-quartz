## Definition

A **lymph** is a clear, [[Water|Watery]] fluid that flows through the [[Lymphatic System]]. It originates from [[Blood]] plasma that leaks out of [[Capillary|Capillaries]] to nourish [[Tissue|Tissues]], subsequently collecting waste, excess fluid and pathogens. It acts as both a delivery vehicle and a bodily sanitation network.
