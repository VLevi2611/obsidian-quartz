## Definition

The **subclavian veins** are paired deep [[Vein|Veins]] running under the [[Clavicle]]. They drain deoxygenated [[Blood]] from the upper extremities, head and neck back to the [[Heart]]. They begin as a continuation of the [[Axillary Vein]], course medially beneath the clavicle and run anterior and inferior to the [[Subclavian Artery]].
