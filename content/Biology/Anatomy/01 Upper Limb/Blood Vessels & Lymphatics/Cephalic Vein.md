## Definition

The **cephalic vein** is a major, superficial [[Vein]] that runs from the [[Hand]] along the [[Forearm]] and [[Elbow Joint|Elbow]] to the [[Shoulder]]. It connects to the [[Basilic Vein]] via the [[Median Cubital Vein]] and empties into the [[Axillary Vein]]. it is used for venipuncture, drawing [[Blood]] and inserting IV.
