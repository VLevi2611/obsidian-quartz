## Definition

The **Wrist Joint** is a complex, biaxial [[Synovial Joint]] where the [[Forearm]] meats the [[Hand]]. It connects the distal [[Radius]] and the articular disc to 3 proximal [[Carpals]]. It facilitates [[Movement|Flexion]], extension, abduction and adduction.
