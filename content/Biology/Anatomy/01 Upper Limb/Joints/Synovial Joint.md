## Definition

**Synovial joints** are connections between [[Bone|Bones]] where the elements involved are separated by a narrow articular cavity. In addition to containing an articular cavity, these [[Joint|Joints]] have a number of characteristic features.
1. First, a layer of [[Cartilage]], usually hyaline cartilage, covers the articulating surfaces of the skeletal elements. In other words, bony surfaces do not normally contact one another directly. As a consequence, when these joints are viewed in normal radiographs, a wide gap seems to separate the adjacent bones because the cartilage that covers the articulating surfaces is more transparent to X-rays than bone.
