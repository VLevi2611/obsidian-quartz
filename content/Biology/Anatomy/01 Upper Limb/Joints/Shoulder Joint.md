## Definition

The **Glenohumeral Joint** is a [[Synovial Joint|Synovial Ball and socket Joint]]. It involves an articulation between the [[Glenoid Fossa]] of the [[Scapula]] and the head of the [[Humerus]]. Due to the very loose joint capsule, it gives a limited interface of the humerus and scapula and is the most mobile joint of the human body.
