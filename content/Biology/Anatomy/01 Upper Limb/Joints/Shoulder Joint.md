## Definition

The **glenohumeral joint** is a [[Synovial Joint|Synovial Ball and socket Joint]]. It involves an articulation between the [[Glenoid Fossa]] of the [[Scapula]] and the head of the [[Humerus]]. Due to the very loose joint capsule, it gives a limited interface of the humerus and scapula and is the most mobile joint of the human body. The shoulder Joint is [[Muscle]] dependent, as it lacks strong [[Ligament|Ligaments]]. The primary stabilizers include the [[Biceps Brachii]], [[Pectoralis Major]], [[Latissimus Dorsi]], [[Teres Major]] and [[Deltoid]] muscles.

![[Shoulder Joint.png]]

The [[Nerve|Nerves]] supplying the shoulder joint all arise in the [[Brachial Plexus]]. They are the [[Suprascapular Nerve]], the [[Axilla Nerve]] and the [[Pectoral Nerve]].
