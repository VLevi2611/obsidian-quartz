## Definition

The **metacarpophalangeal (MCP) joints**—commonly known as the knuckles—are the [[Joint|Joints]] where the hand bones ([[Metacarpals]]) meet the finger bones ([[Phalanges]]). These joints act as condyloid [[Synovial Joint]], permitting a wide range of [[Movement]] including flexion, extension, abduction, adduction, and circumduction.
