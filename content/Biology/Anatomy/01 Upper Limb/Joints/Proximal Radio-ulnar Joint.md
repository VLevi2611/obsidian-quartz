## Definition

The **Proximal Radio-ulnar Joint** is a [[Elbow Joint|Elbow]] pivot [[Synovial Joint]] between the [[Radius]] and the [[Ulna]]. A number of [[Nerve|Nerves]] run close to it, including: [[Median Nerve]], [[Musculocutaneous Nerve]], [[Radial Nerve]].
