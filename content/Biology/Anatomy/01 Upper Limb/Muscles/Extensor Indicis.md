## Definition

The **extensor indicis** is a narrow, elongated skeletal [[Muscle]] in the [[Forearm]], placed medial to, and parallel with the [[Extensor Pollicis Longus and Brevis]]. It originates in the [[Ulna]] and inserts onto the 2nd [[Phalanges|Phalanx]].
