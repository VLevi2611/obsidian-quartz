## Definition

The **rhomboid major** is a skeletal [[Muscle]] of the back that connects the [[Scapula]] with the vertebrae of the spinal column. It acts together with the [[Rhomboid Minor]] to keep the scapula pressed against the thoracic wall and to retract the scapula toward the vertebral column.
