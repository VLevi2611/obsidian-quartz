## Definition

The **rhomboid major** is a skeletal [[Muscle]] of the back that connects the [[Scapula]] with the [[Vertebra|Vertebrae]] of the [[Spinal Column]]. It acts together with the [[Rhomboid Minor]] to keep the scapula pressed against the [[Thoracic Bones]] and to retract the scapula toward the vertebral column.
