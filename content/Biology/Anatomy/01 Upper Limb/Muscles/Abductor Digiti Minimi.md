## Definition

The **abductor digiti minimi** is a skeletal [[Muscle]] situated on the [[Ulna|Ulnar]] border of the palm of the [[Hand]]. Its main function is to pull the little finger away from the other fingers ([[Movement|Abduction]]). It originates in the [[Bones of the Hand|Pisiform Bone]] and inserts onto the base of the proximal [[Phalanges|Phalanx]] of the 5th digit.
