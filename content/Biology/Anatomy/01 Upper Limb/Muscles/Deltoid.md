## Definition

The **deltoid** is the [[Muscle]] forming the rounded contour of the [[Shoulder]]. It is made up of 3 distinct sets of muscle fibers:
1. anterior deltoid - from the [[Clavicle]] to the [[Pectoralis Major]]
2. posterior deltoid
3. intermediate deltoid.
