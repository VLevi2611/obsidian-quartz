## Definition

The **triceps brachii** is a large [[Muscle]] on the back of the upper [[Arm]] between the [[Scapula]] and the [[Ulna]]. It consists of 3 parts: the medial, lateral and long head. All three heads cross the [[Elbow Joint]], and the long head also crosses the [[Shoulder Joint]]. The triceps muscle contracts when the  elbow is straightened and expands when the elbow is bent
