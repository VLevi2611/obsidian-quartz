## Definition

The **infraspinatus** is a thick triangular [[Muscle]] that is one of the 4 muscles of the rotator cuff. The main function of the infraspinatus is to extremely rotate the [[Humerus]] and stabilize the [[Shoulder Joint]]. It attaches to the [[Scapula]] to the [[Humerus]].
