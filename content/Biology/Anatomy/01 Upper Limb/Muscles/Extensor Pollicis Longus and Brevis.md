## Definition

The **extensor pollicis longus and brevis** are 2 [[Muscle|Muscles]] in the [[Forearm]] that control the [[Movement|Extension]] and movement of the thumb. They originate in the [[Ulna]] and inserts onto the distal [[Phalanges|Phalanx]] of the thumb.
