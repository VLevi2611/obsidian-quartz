## Definition

The **adductor pollicis** is a [[Muscle]] in the [[Hand]] that functions to [[Movement|Adduct]] the thumb. It has 2 heads: the transverse and oblique. It originates from the 2nd and third [[Metacarpals]] and adjacent [[Bones of the Hand|capitate Bones]]
