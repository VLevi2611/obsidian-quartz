## Definition

The **Pectoralis Major** is the large, fan shaped [[Muscle]] that makes the human chest. The Pectoralis Major arise from parts of the [[Clavicle]] and [[Sternum]]. The Pectoralis Major's primary function are [[Movement|Flexion]], adduction and internal rotation of the [[Humerus]]
