## Definition

The **opponens digiti minimi** is a triangular [[Muscle]] in the [[Hand]]. It is placed immediately beneath the [[Palmaris Brevis]], [[Abductor Digiti Minimi]] and [[Flexor Digiti Minimi Brevis]]. It controls the little finger. It originates from the [[Bones of the Hand|]] inserts onto the medial border of the 5th [[Metacarpals|Metacarpal]]
