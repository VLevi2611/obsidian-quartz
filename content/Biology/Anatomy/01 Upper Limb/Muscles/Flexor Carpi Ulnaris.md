## Definition

The **flexor carpi ulnaris** is a [[Muscle]] of the [[Forearm]] that [[Movement|Flexes]] and adducts at the [[Wrist Joint]]. The flexor carpi ulnaris has 2 heads; a [[Humerus|Humeral]] head and [[Ulna|Ulnar]] head.
