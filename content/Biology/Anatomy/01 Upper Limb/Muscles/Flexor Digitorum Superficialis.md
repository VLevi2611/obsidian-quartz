## Definition

The **flexor digitorum superficialis** is an extrinsic flexor [[Muscle]] at the [[Interphalangeal Joints]]. The muscle has 2 heads, that originate from the [[Humerus]] and parts of the [[Radius]] and [[Ulna]] and inserts on the base of the [[Phalanges]].
