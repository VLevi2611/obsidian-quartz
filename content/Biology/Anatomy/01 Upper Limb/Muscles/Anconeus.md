## Definition

The **anconeus** is a [[Muscle]] placed behind and below the [[Elbow Joint]], and seems to be continuous by its upper margin with the lateral part of the [[Triceps Brachii]]. It originates by a [[Tendon]] from the [[Humerus]] and inserts distally on the upper fourth posterior surface of the [[Ulna]]. Its role is to assist in [[Movement|Extension]] of the elbow, and supports the elbow in full extension.
