## Definition

The **abductor pollicis brevis** is a [[Muscle]] in the [[Hand]] that functions as an [[Movement|Abductor]] of the [[Metacarpals|Thumb]]. It originates from the [[Bones of the Hand|Trapezium]] and inserts onto the base of the proximal [[Phalanges|Phalanx]] of the thumb.
