## Definition

The **teres minor** is a narrow, elongated [[Muscle]] of the rotator cuff. The muscle originates from the lateral border and adjacent posterior surface of the [[Scapula]] and inserts at the [[Humerus]]. The primary function of the teres minor is to modulate the action of the [[Deltoid]], preventing the humeral head from sliding upward as the [[Arm]] is [[Movement|Abducted]]
