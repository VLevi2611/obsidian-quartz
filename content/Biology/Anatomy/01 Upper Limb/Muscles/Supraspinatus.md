## Definition

The **supraspinatus** is a relatively small [[Muscle]] of the upper back that runs from the [[Scapula]] to the [[Humerus]]. It is one of the four rotator cuff muscles and also [[Movement|Abducts]] the [[Arm]] at the [[Shoulder]]. The spine of the scapula separates the supraspinatus from the [[Infraspinatus]].
