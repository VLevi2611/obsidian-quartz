## Definition

The **subclavius** is a small triangular [[Muscle]] in the [[Chest]], placed between the [[Clavicle]] and the first rib. Its function is to suppresses the lateral clavicle, acts to stabilize the clavicle while the [[Shoulder]] [[Movement|Moves]] the [[Arm]]. It also raises the first rib while lowering the clavicle during breathing. It protects the underlying [[Brachial Plexus]] and subclavian [[Blood Vessels]] from a broken clavicle- the most frequently broken [[Long Bone]].
