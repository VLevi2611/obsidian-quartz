## Definition

The **extensor digitorum** is a [[Muscle]] of the [[Forearm]], originating from t he [[Humerus]]. It extends the medial four [[Phalanges]] of the [[Hand]], then the [[Wrist]] and finally the [[Elbow Joint]]. It tends to separate the fingers as it extends them.
