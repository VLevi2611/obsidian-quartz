## Definition

The **latissimus dorsi (lats)** is the largest [[Muscle]] in the human back. It is a flat, fan-shaped muscle that stretches to the sides, behind the [[Arm]] and is partly covered by the [[Trapezius]]. 
It is responsible for [[Movement|Extension]], adduction, transverse extension, flexion from an extended position and internal rotation of the [[Shoulder Joint]].
