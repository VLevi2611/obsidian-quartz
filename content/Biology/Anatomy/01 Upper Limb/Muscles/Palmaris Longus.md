## Definition

The **palmaris longus** is a [[Muscle]] visible as a small [[Tendon]] located between the [[Flexor Carpi Radialis]] and the [[Flexor Carpi Ulnaris]]. It has rates of absence in the general population ranging from 10-20%. It originates from the [[Humerus]].
