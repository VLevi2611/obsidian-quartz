## Definition

The **pronator teres** is a [[Muscle]] of the [[Forearm]] that, along with the [[Pronator Quadratus]], serves to [[Movement|Pronate]] the forearm. The pronator teres has 2 heads- [[Humerus|Humeral]] and [[Ulna|Ulnar]]. The muscle passes obliquely across the forearm, and ends in a flat [[Tendon]]m which is inserted onto the [[Radius]].
