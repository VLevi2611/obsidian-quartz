## Definition

The **opponens pollicis** is a small triangular [[Muscle]] in the [[Hand]], which functions to oppose the [[Metacarpals|Thumb]]. It lies deep to the [[Abductor Pollicis Brevis]] and lateral to the [[Flexor Pollicis Brevis]]. It originates from the [[Bones of the Hand|Trapezium]] and inserts onto the [[Metacarpals|Metacarpal]] of the thumb. It allows the tip of the thumb to touch the tips of the other [[Phalanges|Fingers]].
