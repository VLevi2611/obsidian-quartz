## Definition

The **palmar interossei** are 4 [[Muscle|Muscles]], one on the thumb that is occasionally missing, and three small central muscles in the [[Hand]] that lei between the [[Metacarpals]] and are attached to the index, ring and little [[Phalanges]]. They are smaller than the [[Dorsal Interossei]]. They [[Movement|Adduct]] the fingers towards the middle finger.
