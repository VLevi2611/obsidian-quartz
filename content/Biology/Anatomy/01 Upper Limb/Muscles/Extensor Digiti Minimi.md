## Definition

The **extensor digiti minimi** is a slender [[Muscle]] of the [[Forearm]], placed on the [[Ulna|Ulnar]] side the of [[Extensor Digitorum]], with which it is generally connected. It originates from the [[Humerus]] and inserts onto the middle [[Phalanges|Phalanx]].
