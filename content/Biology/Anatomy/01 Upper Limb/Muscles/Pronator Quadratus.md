## Definition

The **pronator quadratus** is a square shaped [[Muscle]] on the distal [[Forearm]] that acts to [[Movement|Pronate]] the [[Hand]]. It originates from the medial [[Ulna]] and inserts onto the anterior [[Radius]]. When a pronator quadratus contracts, it pulls the lateral side of the radius towards the ulna, thus pronating the hand.
