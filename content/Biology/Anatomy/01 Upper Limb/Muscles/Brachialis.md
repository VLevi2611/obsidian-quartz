## Definition

The **brachialis** is a [[Muscle]] in the upper [[Arm]] that [[Movement|Flexes]] the [[Elbow Joint]]. It lies beneath the [[Biceps Brachii]].. It originates from the anterior aspect of the distal [[Humerus]] and inserts onto the [[Ulna]].
