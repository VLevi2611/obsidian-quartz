## Definition

The **flexor pollicis brevis** is a [[Muscle]] in the [[Hand]] that [[Movement|Flexes]] the [[Metacarpals|Thumb]]. It has both a superficial part and a deep part. It originates from the [[Bones of the Hand|Trapezium]] and inserts onto the proximal [[Phalanges]].
