## Definition

The **coracobrachialis** is a [[Muscle]] in the upper medial part of the [[Arm]]. It originates from the [[Scapula]], and inserts onto the middle of the medial aspect of the [[Humerus]]. It acts to [[Movement|Adduct]] and flex the arm.
