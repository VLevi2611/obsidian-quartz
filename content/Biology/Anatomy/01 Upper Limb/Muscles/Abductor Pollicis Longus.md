## Definition

The **abductor pollicis longus** is a [[Muscle]] of the [[Forearm]]. Its major function is to [[Movement|Abduct]] the thumb at the [[Wrist]]. It originates from the [[Ulna]] and [[Radius]] and inserts onto the base of the first [[Metacarpals|Metacarpal]].
