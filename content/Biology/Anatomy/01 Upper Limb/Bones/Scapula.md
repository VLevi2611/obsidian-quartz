## Definition

The **scapula**, also known as the **shoulder blade**, is the [[Bone]] that connects the [[Humerus]] (upper arm bone) with the [[Clavicle]] (collar bone). Like their connected bones, the scapulae are paired, with each scapula on either side of the body being roughly a mirror image of the other.
