## Definition

The **carpal bones** are the 8 small [[Bone|Bones]] that make up the wrist, that connect the hand to the forearm. The main role of the carpal bones is to articulate with the [[Radius]] and [[Ulna]] to form the [[Wrist Joint]], to provide attachment for [[Thenar]] and [[Hypothenar]] [[Muscle|Muscles]], and to form part of the rigid [[Carpal Tunnel]] which allows the [[Median Nerve]] and [[Tendon|Tendons]] of the anterior forearm muscles to be transmitted to the hand and fingers.
