## Definition

The **lumbar plexus** is a web of [[Nerve|Nerves]] in the lower back, deep within the [[Psoas Major]]. It is primarily formed by the anterior rami of the first 4 **lumbar spinal nerves (L1-L4)**, with frequent contributions from the last **thoracic nerve (T12)**. It provides motor and sensory innervation to the [[Pelvis]] and lower extremities.
