## Definition

The **lumbar plexus** is a web of [[Nerve|Nerves]] in the lower back, deep within the [[Psoas Major]]. It is primarily formed by the anterior rami of the first 4 **lumbar spinal nerves (L1-L4)**, with frequent contributions from the last **thoracic nerve (T12)**. It provides motor and sensory innervation to the [[Pelvis]] and lower extremities and allows [[Movement]] of the lower limb.

### Major Nerves

- [[Femoral Nerve]] (L2-L4): The largest branch; controls [[Muscle|Muscles]] that extend the [[Knee]] and provide sensation to the from of the [[Thigh]] and inner [[Leg]].
- [[Obturator Nerve]] (L2-L4): Innervates the adductor muscles of the hip and supplies sensory fibers to the medial thigh
