## Definition

The **foot** is the terminal area of the [[Leg]]. It is a strong and complex mechanical structure containing 26 [[Bone|Bones]] ([[Tibia]], [[Fibula]], [[Tarsals]], [[Metatarsals]], [[Phalanges]]), 33 [[Joint|Joints]] ([[Ankle Joint]], [[Subtalar Joint]], [[Interphalangeal Joints]]), and more than 100 [[Muscle|Muscles]], [[Tendon|Tendons]] and [[Ligament|Ligaments]].
