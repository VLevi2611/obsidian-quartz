## Definition

The **popliteal fossa** is a diamond-shaped depression in the back of the [[Knee]]. It serves as a vital transitioning zone for major [[Blood Vessels]], [[Nerve|Nerves]] and [[Lymph|Lymphs]] passing between the [[Thigh]] and the [[Leg]].
