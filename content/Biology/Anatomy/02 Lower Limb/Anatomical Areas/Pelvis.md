## Definition

The **pelvis** is the lower part of a trunk between the [[Abdomen]] and the [[Thigh]]. The pelvic region of the trunk includes the **pelvic cavity**, the **pelvic floor** and the **perineum**, below the pelvic floor. The pelvic skeleton is formed by the [[Sacrum]] and the [[Coccyx]] in the back, and anteriorly by a pair of [[Hip Bone|Hip Bones]]. There are some differences between a male and female pelvis.
