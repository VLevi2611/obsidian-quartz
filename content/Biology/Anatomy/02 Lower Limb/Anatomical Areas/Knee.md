## Definition

The **knee** is an area that joins the [[Thigh]] with the [[Leg]] and consists of 2 [[Joint|Joints]]: One between the [[Femur]] and [[Tibia]] ([[Tibiofibular Joints]]), and one between the femur and the [[Patella]] ([[Patellofemoral Joint]]).
