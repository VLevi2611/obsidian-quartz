## Definition

The **femoral triangle** (groin), is a wedge-shaped space in the upper medial [[Thigh]]. It serves as an anatomical passageway for major [[Blood Vessels]], [[Nerve|Nerves]], and [[Lymph|Lymphs]] moving between the [[Abdomen]] and the lower limb.
