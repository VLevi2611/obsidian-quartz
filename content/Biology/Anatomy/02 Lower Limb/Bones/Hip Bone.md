## Definition

The **hip bone** is a large flat [[Bone]]. It is composed of 3 parts: The [[Ilium]], [[Ischium]] and the [[Pubis]] in the [[Pelvis]]. At birth, these bones are separated but by the end of puberty, they are fused together. Each hip bone is connected to the [[Femur]] through the [[Hip Joint]].
