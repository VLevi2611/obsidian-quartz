## Definition

The **tarsals** are a cluster of 7 articulating [[Bone|Bones]] in each [[Foot]], located between the [[Tibia]], [[Fibula]] and the [[Metatarsals]]. Together, they form the [[Ankle]] and the posterior part of the foot, providing structural support and facilitating [[Movement]].
- **Calcaneus** 
- **Talus**
- **Cuboid**
- **Navicular**
- **Cuneiforms**
