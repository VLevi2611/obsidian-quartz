## Definition

The **soleus** is a broad, flat [[Muscle]] located in the back of the [[Leg]], lying beneath the [[Gastrocnemius]], and forming the calf together. It originates on the [[Tibia]] and [[Fibula]] and merges with the **Achilles [[Tendon]]** to inserts at the [[Metatarsals|Heel]]. It acts as a [[Ankle]] [[Movement|Flexor]].
