## Definition

The **adductor magnus** is the largest [[Muscle]] in the inner [[Thigh]] (bigger than the [[Adductor Longus]] and [[Adductor Brevis]]). It acts as a hybrid muscle. functioning primarily to [[Movement||Adduct]] the thigh and extend the [[Hip Joint]], stabilizing the [[Pelvis]]. It originates at the [[Pubis]] and inserts into the [[Femur]].
