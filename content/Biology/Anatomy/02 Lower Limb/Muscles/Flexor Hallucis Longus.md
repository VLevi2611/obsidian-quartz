## Definition

The **flexor hallucis longus** is a [[Muscle]] and [[Tendon]] in the back of the [[Leg]] and the sole of the [[Foot]]. Its primary functions are [[Movement|Flexing]] the big [[Phalanges|Toe]] and providing the final powerful "push-off" step during walking, running or jumping.
