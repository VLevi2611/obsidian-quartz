## Definition

The **extensor digitorum brevis** is a short intrinsic [[Muscle]] located on the top of the [[Foot]]. It assists in the [[Movement|Extension]] of the [[Metatarsophalangeal Joints]] and [[Interphalangeal Joints]]. It originates on the superior surface of the [[Tarsals|Calcaneus]] and inserts into the long extensor [[Tendon|Tendons]] of the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th toes.
