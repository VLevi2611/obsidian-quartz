## Definition

The **plantaris** is a small [[Muscle]] in the posterior compartment of the [[Leg]] (calf). Originates above the lateral head of the [[Gastrocnemius]], on the [[Femur]] and inserts on the [[Tarsals|Calcaneus]]. It assists in [[Movement|Flexing]] the [[Knee]] and pointing the toes.
