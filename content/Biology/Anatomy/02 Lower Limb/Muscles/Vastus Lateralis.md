## Definition

The **vastus lateralis** is the largest and most powerful [[Muscle]] in the quadriceps group, located on the outer part of the [[Thigh]]. It originates from the top of the [[Femur]] and inserts into the [[Patella]]. It functions to [[Movement|Extend]] the [[Knee]].
