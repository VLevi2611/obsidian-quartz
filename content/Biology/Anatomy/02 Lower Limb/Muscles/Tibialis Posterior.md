## Definition

The **tibialis posterior** is the deepest [[Muscle]] in the posterior compartment of the [[Leg]]. It runs behind the [[Tibia]] and its [[Tendon]] wraps around the inside of the [[Ankle]] and attaches to the [[Tarsals| Navicular]] and medial cuneiform. It acts as a stabilizer of the leg and contracts to produce [[Movement|Inversion]] of the [[Foot]] and assist in the plantarflexion of the foot at the ankle.
