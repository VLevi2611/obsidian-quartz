## Definition

The **semimembranosus** is the most medial and deep of the hamstring [[Muscle||Muscles]] ([[Semitendinosus]], [[Biceps Femoris]]) located in the back of the [[Thigh]]. It is a primary driver of [[Hip Joint]] [[Movement|Extension]] and [[Knee Joint]] flexion. It originates from the [[Ischium]] and inserts on the posterior surface of the [[Tibia]].
