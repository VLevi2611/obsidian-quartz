## Definition

The **tibialis anterior** is a long [[Muscle]] in the anterior compartment of the [[Leg]], situated on the lateral side of the [[Tibia]]. It is responsible for [[Movement|Dorsiflexion]] the [[Ankle]] and inverting the [[Foot]]. It inserts into the first [[Metatarsals|Metatarsal]].
