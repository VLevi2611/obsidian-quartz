## Definition

The **iliopsoas** is a [[Muscle]] in the [[Thigh]], merging the **psoas major** from the spine and the **iliacus** from the [[Pelvis]]. They merge at the [[Femur]] and act as the strongest [[Movement|Flexor]] of the [[Hip Joint]].
