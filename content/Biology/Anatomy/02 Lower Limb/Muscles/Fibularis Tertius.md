## Definition

The **fibularis tertius** is a [[Muscle]] in the anterior compartment of the [[Leg]]. It is distinct that, it acts to [[Movement|Dorsiflex]] the [[Foot]] and tilt the sole outward, rather than functioning as a lateral muscle. It originates from the distal [[Fibula]] and inserts into the base of the 5th [[Metatarsals|Metatarsal]]
