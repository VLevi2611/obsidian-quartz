## Definition

The **extensor hallucis longus** is a thin skeletal [[Muscle]] in the anterior compartment of the [[Leg]]. It originates on the [[Fibula]] and inserts into the distal [[Phalanges|Phalanx]] of the big toe. It [[Movement|Extends]] the bit toe (hallux) at both the [[Metatarsophalangeal Joints]] and [[Interphalangeal Joints]]. It also assists in dorsiflexion of the [[Foot]] and foot inversion.
