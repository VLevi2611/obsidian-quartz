## Overview 

The [[Muscle|Muscles]] of the lower limb enable posture, balance and locomotion. They are organized by [[Anatomical Areas of the Lower Limb|Region]] into the gluteal muscles, thigh leg and foot.

### The Gluteal Region

These muscles connect the lower limb to the [[Pelvis]], primarily driving hip [[Movement|Extension]], abduction and rotation.
