## Definition

The **adductor longus** is a large triangular [[Muscle]] located in the medial compartment of the [[Thigh]]. It is the most superficial muscle of the hip adductor group. It originates via a strong [[Tendon]] from the [[Pubis]] and inserts into the [[Femur]]. Its primary action is [[Movement|||Adduction]] of the [[Hip Joint]], thigh flexion, hip rotation and [[Pelvis]] stabilization
