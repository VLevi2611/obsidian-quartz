## Definition

The **vastus medialis** is one of the 4 quadriceps [[Muscle|Muscles]] located on the front and inner side of the [[Thigh]]. It is responsible for [[Movement|Extension]] of the [[Knee]] and stabilizing the [[Patella]]. It originates on the medial side of the [[Femur]] and inserts in the quadriceps tendon.
