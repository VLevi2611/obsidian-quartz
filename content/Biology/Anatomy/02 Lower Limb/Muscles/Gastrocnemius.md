## Definition

The **gastrocnemius** is a large, superficial [[Muscle]] located on the back of the [[Leg]]. Along with the [[Soleus]], it forms the major bulk of the calf. It has 2 distinct heads- the lateral and medial heads that originate just above the [[Knee Joint]] on the [[Femur]]. It merges with the soleus to form the **Achilles [[Tendon]]**
