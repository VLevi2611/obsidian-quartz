## Definition

The **semitendinosus** is one of three hamstring [[Muscle|Muscles]] located in the posterior compartment of the [[Thigh]]. It originates on the [[Pelvis]], runs down the back of the [[Leg]] and transition into a long, cord-like [[Tendon]], and inserts into the upper medial part of the [[Tibia]]. It enables [[Movement||Extension]] of the [[Hip Joint]], [[Knee Joint]] flexion and inward leg rotation..
