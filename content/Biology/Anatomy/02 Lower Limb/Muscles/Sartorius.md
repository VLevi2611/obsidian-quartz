## Definition

The **sartorius** is a long, thin, superficial [[Muscle]] that runs down the length of the [[Thigh]] in the anterior compartment. It is the longest muscle in the human body, spanning both the [[Hip Joint]] and [[Knee Joint]]. It originates on the outer [[Hip Bone]] and inserts into the [[Tibia]]. It [[Movement|Flexes]], abducts and externally rotate the hip. Flexes and internally rotates the [[Leg]] at the [[Knee]].
