## Definition

The **vastus intermedius** is a deep [[Muscle]] located in the anterior compartment of the [[Thigh]]. Sitting beneath the [[Rectus Femoris]] and between the [[Vastus Medialis]] and [[Vastus Lateralis]]. It originates from the [[Femur]] and inserts into the [[Patella]]. It acts as an [[Movement|Extensor]] of the [[Knee]].
