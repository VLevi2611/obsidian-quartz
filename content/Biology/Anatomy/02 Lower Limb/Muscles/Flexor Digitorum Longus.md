## Definition

The **flexor digitorum longus** is a long, thin [[Muscle]] in the deep posterior compartment of the [[Leg]].. Its Primary functions are [[Movement|Flexing]] the lateral four toes and assisting with plantar flexion of the [[Ankle Joint]]. It originates in the [[Tibia]] and inserts into the bases of the distal [[Phalanges]].
