## Definition

The **rectus femoris** is a large superficial [[Muscle]] in the anterior compartment of the [[Thigh]] and is one of the 4 muscles making up the quadriceps group. ([[Vastus Lateralis]], [[Vastus Medialis]], [[Vastus Intermedius]]). It crosses both the [[Hip Joint]] and [[Knee Joint]]. It [[Movement|Flexes]] the hip and extends the knee.
