## Definition

The **gracilis** is the most superficial [[Muscle]] on the medial side of the [[Thigh]]. It originates from the [[Ischium]] and inserts into the [[Tibia]]. It functions as an [[Movement||Adductor]] of the [[Hip Joint]], medially and laterally rotates it and flexes the hip, and also aids in flexion of the [[Knee Joint]].
