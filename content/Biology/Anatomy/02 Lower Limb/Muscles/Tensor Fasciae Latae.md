## Definition

The **tensor fasciae latae** is a small [[Muscle]] located on the lateral hip. It works with the [[Gluteus Maximus]], [[Gluteus Medius]] and [[Gluteus Minimus]]  to stabilize the [[Pelvis]] during walking and running, while driving [[Movement|Movements]] like hip abduction, internal rotation and flexion. It originates from the [[Hip Bone]] and inserts into the [[Tibia]].
