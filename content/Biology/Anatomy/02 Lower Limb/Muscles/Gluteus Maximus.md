## Definition

The **gluteus maximus** is the largest and heaviest [[Muscle]] in the human body. It is the primary [[Movement|Extensor]] of the [[Hip Bone]]. It is the most superficial muscle in the gluteal region, situated at the back of the [[Hip Joint]]. It originates from the [[Pelvis]] and inserts into the [[Femur]].
