## Definition

The **abductor hallucis** is an intrinsic [[Muscle]] located superficially along the medial border of the sole of the [[Foot]]. It arises from the [[Tarsals|Calcaneus]] and inserts into the base of the proximal [[Phalanges|Phalanx]] of the great toe (hallux). It [[Movement|Abducts]] and assists in flexing the big toe at the [[Metatarsophalangeal Joints]].
