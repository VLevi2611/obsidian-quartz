## Definition

The **flexor digitorum brevis** is an intrinsic [[Muscle]] located in the superficial layer of the sole of the [[Foot]]. It originates from the [[Tarsals|||Calcaneus]] and splits into 4 [[Tendon|Tendons]] that attach to the middle [[Phalanges]] of the lateral 4 digits. It [[Movement|Flexes]] the toes at the proximal [[Interphalangeal Joints]] and assists in maintaining the arch of the foot.
