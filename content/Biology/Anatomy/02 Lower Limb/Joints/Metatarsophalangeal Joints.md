## Definition

The **metatarsophalangeal joints** are the hinge-like [[Joint|Joints]] in the [[Foot]] between the [[Metatarsals]] and the [[Phalanges]]. Functioning like the [[Metacarpophalangeal Joints]] of the hand, they are critical for weight bearing, balance and providing the flexibility required to push off the ground during walking or running.
