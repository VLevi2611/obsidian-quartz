## Overview 

The **head and neck** are anatomically complex regions that serve as the command center for the body and the primary entrance for the [[Respiratory System]] and [[Digestive System]]. The head consists of the [[Skull]], which protects the [[Brain]], and the **face**, which includes the eyes, nose, mouth, forehead, cheeks, and chin. The neck attaches the head to the **trunk**, supporting and positioning the head to connect cervical viscera with the posterior openings of the nasal and oral cavities.

---

### Major Compartments of the Head

The head is organized into a series of compartments formed by bone and soft tissues:

- [[Cranial Cavity]]: The largest compartment, containing the brain and its protective [[Membrane|Membranes]], the **meninges**.
- [[Orbits]]: Two cone-shaped chambers that house the eyeballs and associated [[Muscle|Muscles]].
- [[Nasal Cavities]]: The upper parts of the respiratory tract, situated between the orbits. They open anteriorly at the **nares** (nostrils) and posteriorly into the pharynx via the **choanae**.
- [[Oral Cavity]]: The entry point for the digestive system, separated from the nasal cavities by the hard and soft palates.
- [[Ears]]: Containing the apparatus for hearing and balance, mostly housed within the [[Bone|Bones]] forming the floor of the cranial cavity.

---

### Major Compartments of the Neck

The neck is enclosed by an outer musculofascial collar and is subdivided into four major longitudinal compartments:

- [[Vertebral Compartment]]: Contains the [[Cervical Vertebrae]] and the postural muscles that support the head and neck.
- [[Visceral Compartment]]: Contains important glands (thyroid, parathyroid, and thymus) and parts of the respiratory and digestive tracts (pharynx, larynx, esophagus, and trachea).
- [[Vascular Compartments]]: One on each side, containing the major [[Blood Vessels]] (carotid arteries, jugular veins) and the **vagus nerve**.

---

### Topographical Areas and Transitions

Specific areas facilitate clinical localization and the transition of structures between compartments.

- **Triangles of the Neck:** The [[Sternocleidomastoid]] and [[Trapezius]] muscles divide each side of the neck into an **Anterior Triangle** (medial) and a **Posterior Triangle** (lateral).
- [[Infratemporal Fossa]]: An area deep to the [[Mandible]] that serves as a conduit for the **mandibular nerve V3** passing between the cranial and oral cavities.
- [[Pterygopalatine Fossa]]: A small space posterior to the upper jaw that communicates with the cranial cavity, orbit, nasal cavity, and oral cavity.
- [[Superior Thoracic Aperture]]: The narrow opening at the base of the neck that allows continuity with the **thorax**.
- [[Axillary Inlet]]: The "gateway" to the upper limb located on each side of the base of the neck.

### **Anatomy Dictionary: Head and Neck Areas**

#### **Regional and Directional Terms**

- **Cephalic:** Relating to the **head**.
- **Cervical:** Relating to the **neck**.
- **Mental:** Relating to the **chin**.
- **Buccal:** Relating to the **cheek**.
- **Otic:** Relating to the **ear**.
- **Orbital / Ocular:** Relating to the **eye**.
- **Rostral:** Used in the head to mean **toward the nose**.

#### **Organ and Space Terms**

- **Pharynx:** The **throat**; a common chamber for the digestive and respiratory tracts.
- **Larynx:** The **voice box**; the upper part of the lower airway.
- **Meninges:** The three **connective tissue coverings** (dura, arachnoid, and pia mater) surrounding the brain.
- **Paranasal Sinuses:** Air-filled **extensions** of the nasal cavities into surrounding bones.
- **Gyrus:** An **elevation** or ridge on the surface of the cerebral hemispheres.
- **Sulcus:** A **depression** or groove between the ridges of the brain.

#### **Transition and Boundary Terms**

- **Nares:** The **anterior openings** to the nasal cavities.
- **Choanae:** The **posterior openings** where the nasal cavities meet the pharynx.
- **Oral Fissure:** The **mouth** opening.
- **Oropharyngeal Isthmus:** The **posterior opening** of the oral cavity into the pharynx.
- **Calvaria:** The domed **roof of the skull** that protects the brain.
- **Skull Base:** The **floor** of the cranial cavity.
