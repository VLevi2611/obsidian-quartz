## Definition
Acceleration $\mathbf{a}$ is the rate of [[Velocity]] change.
It is a [[Vector]] with the same direction as the [[Force]].
$$\mathbf{a}=\frac{d\mathbf{v}}{dt}=\frac{d\mathbf{x}}{d^2t}$$ 
## Units
$$(\frac{m}{s^2})$$
