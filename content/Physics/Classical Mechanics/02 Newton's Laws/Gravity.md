## Definition

Gravitation Force is a type of [[Force]] that effects a field that is generated by an object with [[Mass]]. Each planet has its [[Gravitational Constant]] $\mathbf{g}$. 
Earth's [[Gravitational Constant]] is $\mathbf{g} = 9.8$

## Units

Newtons $$(N)$$
