## Definition

Linear momentum $\mathbf{p}$ is a [[Vector]] representing an object's "quantity of motion" in a straight line.
Linear momentum is conserved in [[Isolated System]]. It relates the body's [[Mass]] and [[Velocity]].
$$\mathbf{p}=m\mathbf{v}$$
