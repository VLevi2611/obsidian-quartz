## Definition

Impulse $\mathbf{J}$ is the change of [[04 Momentum]] of an object. Impulse is conserved in an [[Isolated System]].
$$\mathbf{J} = \mathbf{p}_f-\mathbf{p}_i$$
$$\mathbf{J}=\int_{t_i}^{t_f} \mathbf{F}dt$$

| Symbol         | Meaning             | Units    |
| :--------------: | :-------------------: | :--------: |
