## Definition

The **domain** of a [[Function]] is the [[Set]] of the inputs. Given a function $f: X \rightarrow Y$, $X$ is the domain and $Y$ is the [[Codomain]].

for the function $f(x) = \sqrt{x}$, the domain is all the positive [[Real Number|Real Numbers]], $\mathbb{R}_{>=0}$.
