## Definition

A **range** is a [[Set]] of the actual outputs of a [[Function]] from a [[Codomain]].

Lets say $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ defined by $f(x) = x^2$
The range of $\{-2, 3\}$ under $f$ is $f(\{-2,3 \}) = \{4,9\}$ and the image of $f$ is $\mathbb{R}^+$.
