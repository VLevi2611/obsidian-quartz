## Concept Notes

- [[Sequences Convergence and Limits]]
    Formal $\epsilon$-$N$ limit definition ($\forall \epsilon > 0, \exists N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $\forall n > N, \vert{}a_n - L\vert{} < \epsilon$), limit uniqueness, boundedness of convergent sequences, limit arithmetic, and Squeeze Theorem.    
- [[bo]]
    Subsequence inheritance, guaranteed existence of a convergent subsequence for every bounded sequence in $\mathbb{R}$, bisection method, and nested interval property.    
- Cauchy Criterion
	Cauchy sequence condition ($\forall \epsilon > 0, \exists N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $\forall n, m > N, \vert{}a_n - a_m\vert{} < \epsilon$), limit-free convergence verification, and completeness of $\mathbb{R}$.
