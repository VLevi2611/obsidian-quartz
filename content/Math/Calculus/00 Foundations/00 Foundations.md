## 📑 Core Topics & Concept Notes

### 1. `[[Sets-and-Functions]]`

- **Mathematical Logic & Symbols:** Quantifiers ($\forall, \exists$), implications ($\implies$), and logical equivalences ($\iff$).
    
      
    
- **Set Theory & Operations:** Universal sets, union ($\cup$), intersection ($\cap$), set difference ($\setminus$), and complements ($A^c$).
    
      
    
- **Proof Techniques:** Mathematical induction and Newton’s Binomial Theorem.
    
      
    
- **Function Fundamentals:** Formal mapping $f: X \to Y$, domain, codomain, range, and representations (formula, graph, ordered pairs).
    
      
    
- **Functional Properties:** Injectivity (one-to-one), surjectivity (onto), and bijectivity.
    
      
    
- **Operations on Functions:** Composition ($f \circ g$), invertibility ($f^{-1}$), and domain-range relationships.
    
      
    
- **Primary Function Families:** Power, exponential, logarithmic, trigonometric, and inverse trigonometric functions.
    
      
    

### 2. `[[The-Real-Numbers]]`

- **Hierarchy of Number Systems:** $\mathbb{N} \subset \mathbb{Z} \subset \mathbb{Q} \subset \mathbb{R} \subset \mathbb{C}$.
    
      
    
- **Rational Numbers ($\mathbb{Q}$):** Equivalence classes of pairs $(p, q)$, arithmetic closure, and density.
    
      
    
- **Incompleteness of $\mathbb{Q}$:** Metric gaps and the formal proof by contradiction of the irrationality of $\sqrt{2}$.
    
      
    
- **Ordered Field Axioms:**
    
      
    - _Field Axioms:_ Associativity, commutativity, distributivity, identities, and inverses for $+$ and $\cdot$.
        
          
        
    - _Order Axioms:_ Trichotomy, transitivity, and compatibility with addition/multiplication.
        
          
        
- **Absolute Value & Metrics:** Piecewise definition, non-negativity, multiplicativity, and symmetry.
    
      
    
- **Fundamental Analytical Inequalities:** Triangle inequality, Reverse triangle inequality, and Bernoulli's inequality.
    
      
    

### 3. `[[Bounds-and-Completeness]]`

- **Bounded Sets:** Upper bounds, lower bounds, bounded above, bounded below, and bounded sets.
    
      
    
- **Extrema:** Maximum ($\max$) and Minimum ($\min$) of a set.
    
      
    
- **Supremum & Infimum:** Least Upper Bound ($\sup$) and Greatest Lower Bound ($\inf$).
    
      
    
- **Characterization Theorems:** The $\epsilon$-characterization of supremum and infimum.
    
      
    
- **The Completeness Axiom:** Every non-empty set of real numbers that is bounded above has a supremum in $\mathbb{R}$.
    
      
    
- **Key Analytical Results:**
    
      
    - The Archimedean Property of $\mathbb{R}$.
        
          
        
    - Density of Rational Numbers ($\mathbb{Q}$) in Real Numbers ($\mathbb{R}$).
        
          
        
    - Density of Irrational Numbers ($\mathbb{R} \setminus \mathbb{Q}$) in $\mathbb{R}$.
