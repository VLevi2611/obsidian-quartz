## Definition

The **dimension** is the size of the space, defined by the number of [[Vector|Vectors]] contained in any [[Basis]] for that space.

The standard basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$ is:
$$
\left\{ 
\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 
\end{pmatrix}, 
\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 
\end{pmatrix}, 
\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 1 
\end{pmatrix} 
\right\}
$$

The dimension is:
$$
\dim _\mathbb{R} (\mathbb{R}^3)=3
$$
