## Definition

A **basis** is a sequence of [[Vector|Vectors]] that have [[Linear Independence]] and [[Span]] the space.
The basis vectors provide a unique way to represent every vector in the space.

The standard basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$ is:
$$
\left\{ 
\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 
\end{pmatrix}, 
\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 
\end{pmatrix}, 
\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 1 
\end{pmatrix} 
\right\}
$$
