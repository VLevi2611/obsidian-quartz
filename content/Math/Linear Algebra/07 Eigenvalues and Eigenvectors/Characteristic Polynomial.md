## Definition

A **characteristic polynomial** of a [[Matrix]] $\mathbf{A}$ is a [[Polynomial]], 
which is [[Invariant]] and has [[Eigenvalue]]s $\lambda_1,...,\lambda_n$ as roots. It has the [[Determinant]] and the [[Trace]] of the matrix among its coefficients.
To get the characteristic polynomial, we need to solve the equation: 
$$
\det(\mathbf{A-I}\lambda_i) = 0
$$
