## Definition

A diagonal matrix is a type of [[Matrix]] where the non diagonal components are 0 and the diagonal component are the [[Eigenvalue|Eigenvalues]].

## Example

The matrix $\mathbf{A}$:
$$
\mathbf{A}=\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 2 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
$$
