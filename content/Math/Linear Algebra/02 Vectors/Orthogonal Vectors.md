## Definition

Two [[Vector|Vectors]] are called **orthogonal** if their [[Dot Product]] is 0.
[[Geometric Interpretation|Geometrically]], that means that they are perpendicular.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec x &= (1,2), \vec y = (-2,1) \\
\vec x \cdot \vec y &= 0 \\
\vec x &\perp \vec y
\end{aligned}
$$
