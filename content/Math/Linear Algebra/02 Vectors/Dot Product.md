## Definition

A **dot product** is an operation between two vectors. 
[[Multiplication]] between each corresponding component, then summing the results.
The result of scalar multiplication is a [[Scalar]].
The dot product can also be calculated by multiplying the [[Length (Norm)]] of the 2 [[Vector|Vectors]] and the $\cos$ of the angle between them in radians. 
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec x \cdot \vec y &= \sum x_i y_i \\
\vec x \cdot \vec y &=|\vec x||\vec y| \cos (\theta)
\end{aligned}
$$
For example
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec x &= (1,2), \vec y = (5,3) \\
\vec x \cdot \vec y &= (1*5+2*3) \\
\vec x \cdot \vec y &= 11
\end{aligned}
$$
