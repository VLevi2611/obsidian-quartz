## Definition

A **transition state** is the highest [[Energy]], short lived [[Electron Configuration|Configuration]] of [[Atom|Atom]] along a [[Chemical Reaction]], representing the peak of [[Activation Energy]] barrier where [[Chemical Bond|Bonds]] are in the process of breaking and forming. In this state, the [[Reagent|Reagents]] are not yet [[Product|Products]].
