## Definition

Biochemists describe [[Enzyme]] activity with the "lock and key" model. The [[Chemical Reaction|Reacting]] [[Substance]], the **substrate (S)**, attaches itself to the enzyme (E) at a particular point called an [[Active Site]] to form the **enzyme-substrate complex** (ES). The complex decomposes to form [[Product|Products]] and regenerate the enzyme.
