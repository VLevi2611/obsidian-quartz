## Definition

A **reaction profile** is a graphical way of looking at [[Activation Energy]]. [[Energy|Energies]] are plotted on the vertical axis against the [[Chemical Reaction|Reaction]] progress. The reaction starts with [[Reagent|Reagents]] on the left, progresses through a [[Transition State]] and ends with [[Product|Products]] on the right.
