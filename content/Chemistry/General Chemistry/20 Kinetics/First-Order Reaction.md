## Definition

An overall **first-order reaction** has a [[Rate Equation]] in which the sum of the exponents, $m, n, ... = 1$.
In this [[Chemical Reaction|Reaction]], is only 1 [[Reagent]] decomposes into [[Product|Products]]. Lets encounter the decomposition of $\ce{H2O2}$ into [[Water]] and oxygen.
$$\ce{H2O0 (aq)} \rightarrow \ce{H2O (l) + \frac{1}{2} \ce{O2(g)}}$$
The [[Rate of Reaction]] depends on the [[Concentration]] of $\ce{H2O2}$ raised to the 1st power, that is,
$$\text{rate of reaction} = k [\ce{H2O2}]$$
