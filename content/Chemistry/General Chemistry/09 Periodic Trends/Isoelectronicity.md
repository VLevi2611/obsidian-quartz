## Definition

**Isoelectronic** species are [[Atom|Atoms]], [[Ion|Ions]] or [[Molecule|Molecules]] that share the same number of [[Electron|Electrons]] and the same [[Ground State]] [[Electron Configuration]]. They differ in [[Atomic Number]] and [[Charge]]. Generally, across an isoelectronic species, increasing charge decreases [[Ionic Radius]].
