## Definition

The **standard Gibbs energy of formation $\Delta _f G^{\circ}$,** is the [[Gibbs Energy Change]] for a [[Chemical Reaction]] when 1 [[Mole]] of a [[Substance]] is formed from its constituent [[Element|Elements]] in their most stable form under [[STP]]. It indicates a reaction thermodynamic stability and [[Spontaneous Process|Spontaneity]].
$$
\Delta _r G^{\circ} = -RT \ln K
$$

| Symbol |         Meaning          |      Units       |
| :----: | :----------------------: | :--------------: |
|  $R$   |     [[Gas Constant]]     | $\frac{J}{molK}$ |
|  $T$   |     [[Temperature]]      |       $K$        |
|  $K$   | [[Equilibrium Constant]] |        -         |
