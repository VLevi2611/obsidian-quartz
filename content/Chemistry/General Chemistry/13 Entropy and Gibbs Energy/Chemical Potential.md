## Definition

**Chemical potential $\mu$,** is a thermodynamic quantity representing the [[Energy]] change of a [[System]] when particles ([[Atom|Atoms]], [[Molecule|Molecules]], or [[Electron|Electrons]]) are added or removed, specifically defined as the partial molar [[Gibbs Energy]]. It dictates the direction if [[Spontaneous Process|Spontaneous]] [[Mass]] transfer, flowing from high to low [[Potential]].
