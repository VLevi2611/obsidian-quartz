## Definition

The **standard Gibbs energy of reaction $\Delta _r G^{\circ}$,** is the [[Gibbs Energy Change]] for a [[Chemical Reaction]] occurring under standard conditions (1 bar [[Pressure]], specific [[Temperature]]). It determines spontaneity and [[Equilibrium]] position: $\Delta _r G^{\circ} < 0$ indicates a [[Spontaneous Process]], while $\Delta _r G^{\circ} > 0$ indicates a [[Nonspontaneous Process]].
$$
\Delta _r G^{\circ} = -RT \ln K
$$
