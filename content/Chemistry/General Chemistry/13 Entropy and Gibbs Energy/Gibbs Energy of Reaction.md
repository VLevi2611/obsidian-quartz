## Definition

The **Gibbs energy of reaction $\Delta _r G$,** measures the [[Spontaneous Process|Spontaneity]] of a chemical process, determining the maximum non-expansion [[Work]] a [[System]] can do at constant [[Pressure]] and [[Temperature]].
$$
\Delta _r G = \Delta _r G^{\circ} + RT \ln Q
$$
