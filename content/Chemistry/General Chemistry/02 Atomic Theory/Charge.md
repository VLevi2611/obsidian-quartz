## Definition

**Electric charge** is a fundamental [[Property]] of [[Matter]], resulting from an inequality between [[Proton|Protons]] (positive) and [[Electron|Electrons]] (negative) within [[Atom|Atoms]] or [[Molecule|Molecules]]. It dictates electromagnetic interactions, where like charges **repel** and opposite charges **attract**. [[Ion|Ions]] are charged species: [[Cation|Cations]] are positively charged (fewer electrons) and [[Anion|Anions]] are negatively charged (excess electrons).
