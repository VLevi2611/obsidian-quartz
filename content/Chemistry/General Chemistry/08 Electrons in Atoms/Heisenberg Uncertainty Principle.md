## Definition

The **Heisenberg uncertainty principle** implies that for a very precise measurement of position, $x$, many values of [[04 Momentum|Momentum]], $p$, are possible. One way to rationalize this result is to conceive of a highly localized particle as a superposition of many [[Matter]] [[Wave|Waves]] of different de Broglie [[Wavelength|Wavelengths]]. The superposition of many waves of different wavelengths produces an interference pattern, which tends to localize the resultant wave, and the particle it describes, to a region of space.
