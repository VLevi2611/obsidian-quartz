## Definition

A **body centered cubic (bcc)** structure is a type of [[Crystal]] lattice arrangement where [[Atom|Atom]] are located at all 8 corners of a [[Unit Cells|Cube]] plus a single atom in the center. It is characterized by a 68% packing [[Density]] and a coordination number of 8, meaning each [[Central Atom]] touches 8 neighbors.
