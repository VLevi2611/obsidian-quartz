## Definition

**lattice energy** is the [[Energy]] released when [[Gas|Gaseous]] [[Ion|Ions]] form one [[Mole]] of a [[Ionic Solids|Ionic Solid]] [[Crystal]] ([[Exothermic Reaction]]), or the energy required to separate one mole of solid crystal into gas
