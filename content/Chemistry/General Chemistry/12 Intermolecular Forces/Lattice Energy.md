## Definition

**lattice energy** is the [[Energy]] released when [[Gas|Gaseous]] [[Ion|Ions]] form one [[Mole]] of a [[Ionic Solids|Ionic Solid]] [[Crystal]] ([[Exothermic Reaction]]), or the energy required to separate one mole of solid crystal into gaseous ions ([[Endothermic Reaction]]). It measures the strength of [[Ionic Bond|Ionic Bonds]] and depends heavily on high ion [[Charge|Charges]] and small [[Ionic Radius|Ionic Radii]]. Higher lattice energy signifies a more stable, stronger [[Solid]].
