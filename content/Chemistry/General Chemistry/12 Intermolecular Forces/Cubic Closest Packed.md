## Definition

**Cubic closest packed (CCP)** is an efficient [[Crystal]] [[Unit Cells|Structure]], equivalent to [[Face Centered Cubic]], where [[Atom|Atoms]] occupy 74% of total space with a coordination number of 12. It follows an ABCABC stacking sequence of close packed planes, maximizing [[Density]] and is common in [[Metallic Solids]].
