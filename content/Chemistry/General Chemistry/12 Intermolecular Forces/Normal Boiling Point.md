## Definition

The **normal boiling point** is the [[Temperature]] at which a [[Liquid|Liquid's]] [[Vapor Pressure]] equals standard sea-level atmospheric [[Pressure]] (1 atm). At this pressure, [[Water]] [[Boiling|Boils]] at $100 ^{\circ} C$, while other liquids have unique [[Boiling Point|Boiling Point]]
