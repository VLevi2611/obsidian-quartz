## Definition

**Network covalent solids** are materials [[Composition|Composed]] of vast, continuous, 3D or 2D networks of [[Atom|Atoms]] held together by strong [[Covalent Bond|Covalent Bonds]]. These [[Solid|Solids]] function as single giant [[Molecule]], characterized by exceptional hardness, very high [[Melting Point]] and low [[Conductivity]].
