## Definition

[[Chemical Bond|Bonding]] in [[Metal|Metals]] can be explained as a network of positive [[Ion|Ions]] immersed in a sea of [[Electron|Electrons]]. That is, the [[Valence Electron|Valence Electrons]] of the metal [[Atom|Atoms]] are highly delocalized. For this reason, metals are very good [[Conductivity|Conductors]] of [[Electricity]].
