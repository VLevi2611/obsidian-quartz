## Definition

[[Chemical Bond|Bonding]] in [[Metal|Metals]] can be explained as a network of positive [[Ion|Ions]] immersed in a sea of [[Electron|Electrons]]. That is, the [[Valence Electron|Valence Electrons]] of the metal [[Atom|Atoms]] are highly delocalized. For this reason, metals are very good [[Conductivity|Conductors]] of [[Electricity]]. [[Metallic Bond|Metallic Bonding Forces]] are strong in comparison with those arising from intermolecular forces, and this, **metallic solids** have considerably higher [[Melting Point]] than [[Molecular Solids]].
