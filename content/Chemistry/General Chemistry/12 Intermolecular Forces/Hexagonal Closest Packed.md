## Definition

**Hexagonal closest packed (HPC)** is a highly efficient [[Crystal]] [[Unit Cells|Structure]] for [[Atom]] or spheres, characterized by an ABABAB... stacking sequence of layers, achieving a maximum [[Density]] of 74%. It features a coordination number of 12- meaning each atom touches 12 others, and is common in [[Metal|Metals]].
