## Definition

When a [[Liquid]] is [[Heat|Heated]] in a container open to the  atmosphere, there is a particular [[Temperature]] at which [[Evaporation]] occurs throughout the liquid rather than at the surface. [[Vapor]] bubbles form within the bulk of the liquid, rise to the surface and escape. The [[Pressure]] exerted by escaping [[Molecule|Molecules]] equals that exerted by molecules of the atmosphere and **boiling** occurs.
During boiling, [[Energy]] absorbed as heat is used only to convert molecules of liquid into vapor. The temperature remains constant until all the liquid has boiled.
