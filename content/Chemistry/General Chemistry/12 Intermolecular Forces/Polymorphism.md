## Definition

**Polymorphism** is the ability of a [[Solid]] ([[Element]] or [[Compound]]) to exist in more than one [[Crystal]] structure while maintaining the same [[Composition]]. These different forms arise from varied [[Molecular Geometry|Molecular Packing]], resulting in distinct [[Physical Properties]] like [[Solubility]], [[Melting Point]] and stability.
