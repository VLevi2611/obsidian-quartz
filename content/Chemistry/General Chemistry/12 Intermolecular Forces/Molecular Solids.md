## Definition

**Molecular solids** are made up of discrete [[Molecule|Molecules]] that interact via intermolecular forces. At very low [[Temperature|Tempreratures]], the [[Noble Gas|Noble Gases]] form [[Solid|Solids]] that are held together by [[London Forces]], and they are also considered molecular solids.
