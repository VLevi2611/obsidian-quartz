## Definition

 The conversion of a [[Gas]] or [[Vapor]] to a [[Liquid]] is called **condensation**. That is the reverse of [[Evaporation]]. Thus,
 $$\Delta _{cond}H = -\Delta _{vap}H$$
Condensation is an [[Exothermic Reaction]]. This explains why burns produced by a given [[Mass]] of steam are much more severe than burns produced by the same mass of hot [[Water]].
