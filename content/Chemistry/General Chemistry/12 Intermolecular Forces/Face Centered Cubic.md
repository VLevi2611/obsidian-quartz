## Definition

**Face centered cubic (FCC)** is a highly efficient [[Crystal]] structure featuring [[Atom|Atom]] at all 8 corners and the center of all 6 faces of a cubic [[Unit Cells|Unit Cell]]. It contains 4 atoms per unit cell, has a 74% atomic packing factor, a coordination number of 12, and is common in ductile [[Metal|Metals]].
