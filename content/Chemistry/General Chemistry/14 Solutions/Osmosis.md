## Definition

**Osmosis** is the [[Spontaneous Process|Spontaneous]] movement of [[Solvent]] [[Molecule|Molecules]] (usually [[Water]]) through a semipermeable [[Membrane]] from a region of low [[Solute]] [[Concentration]] to high solute concentration. It works to equalize concentrations on both sides.
