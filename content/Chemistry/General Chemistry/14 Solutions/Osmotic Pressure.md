## Definition

**Osmotic pressure $\Pi$,** is the [[Minimum]] [[Pressure]] required to stop the flow of pure [[Solvent]] across a semipermeable [[Membrane]] into a [[Solution]]. It is a [[Colligative Properties]], meaning it depends on the [[Concentration]] of [[Solute]] particles, not their identity. It drives [[Water]] movement in biological [[System|Systems]] and is used in industrial filtration.
