## Definition

An **azeotrope** is a [[Mixture]] of 2 or more [[Liquid|Liquids]] that maintains a constant [[Composition]] and [[Boiling Point]] throughout [[Fractional Distillation|Distillation]], as the [[Vapor]] [[Product|Produced]] has the same composition as the liquid. Because they boil as if they were a single pure component, azeotropes cannot be separated by simple distillation.
