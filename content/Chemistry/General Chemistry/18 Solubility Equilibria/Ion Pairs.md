## Definition

**Ion pairs** are distinct, electrostatically bound [[Complex Ion|Complexes]] of a [[Cation]] and an [[Anion]] that behave as a single entity in [[Solution]] without forming [[Covalent Bond|Covalent Bonds]]. They form when [[Ion|Ions]] do not completely [[Dissociation|Dissociate]], often in [[Solvent|Solvents]] with low dielectric constants, and act as an intermediate state between [[Ionic Compound|Ionic]] and neutral species.
