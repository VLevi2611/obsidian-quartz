## Definition

The **solubility product constant**, $K_{sp}$ , is the [[Equilibrium Constant]] for the dissolution of a [[Solid]] [[Ionic Compound]] into its [[Ion|Ions]] in a [[Saturated Solution]]. It equals the product of the [[Molarity|Molar]] [[Concentration]] of the ions, each raised to the power of its [[Stoichiometric Coefficients|Stoichiometric Coefficient]], indicating a [[Compound]] [[Solubility]].

The solubility of a sightly soluble ionic compound is lowered in the presence of a second [[Solute]] that furnishes a common ion.
