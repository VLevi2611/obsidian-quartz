## Definition

The **formation constant $K_f$**, of a [[Complex Ion]] is the [[Equilibrium Constant]] describing the formation of a complex ion from a [[Central Atom|Central]] [[Ion]] and its attached groups ([[Molecule|Molecules]] or ions). One feature that distinguishes $K_f$ from other equilibrium constants is that $K_f$ values are usually large numbers.
