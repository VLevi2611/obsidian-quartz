## Definition

The [[Groups|Group 2]] [[Element|Elements]] beryllium $\ce{Be}$, magnesium $\ce{Mg}$, calcium $\ce{Ca}$, strontium $\ce{Sr}$, barium $\ce{Ba}$, and radium $\ce{Ra}$. They are shiny, silvery-white, and reactive [[Metal|Metals]] with 2 [[Valence Electron|Valence Electrons]], typically forming +2 [[Cation|Cations]]. They are not found in pure form in nature, occurring instead as [[Compound|Compounds]] in ores and minerals.
