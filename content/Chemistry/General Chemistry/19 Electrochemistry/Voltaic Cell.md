## Definition

**Voltaic cells** are electrochemical devices that convert [[Spontaneous Process|Spontaneous]] [[Chemical Energy]] from [[Oxidation Reduction]] reactions into [[Electrical Energy]]. They consist of 2 separate [[Half-Cell|Half-Cells]] with [[Electrode|Electrodes]] ([[Anode]] and [[Cathode]]) connected by an external circuit and a [[Salt Bridge]], forcing [[Electron]] flow from the negative anode to the positive cathode.
