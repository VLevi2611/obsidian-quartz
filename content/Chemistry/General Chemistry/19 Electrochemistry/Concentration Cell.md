## Definition

A **concentration cell** consists of 2 [[Half-Cell|Half-Cells]] with identical [[Electrode|Electrodes]] but different [[Ion]] [[Concentration|Concentrations]]. Because the electrodes are identical, the [[Standard Electrode Potential|Standard Electrode Potentials]] are numerically equal and subtracting one from the other leads to the value. However, because the ion concentrations differ, there is a [[Voltage]] between the 2 half-cells. The [[Spontaneous Process]] in a concentration cell always occur such that the concentrated [[Solution]] becomes more [[Dilute Solution|Dilute]], and the dilute solution becomes more concentrated. The final result is as if the solutions were simply mixed. In a concentration cell, however, the natural tendency for [[Entropy]] to increase in a mixing process is used as a means of producing [[Electricity]].
