## Definition

The **cell voltage, $E_{cell}$** is the electric [[Potential]] difference between the 2 [[Electrode|Electrodes]] ([[Anode]] and [[Cathode]]) of an [[Electrochemical Cell]], [[Voltage]] representing the [[Force]] that drives [[Electron|Electrons]] through an external circuit. Measured in volts $(V)$, it acts as a measure of [[Energy]] per [[Charge]], often determined by subtracting the anodic potential from the cathodic potential.
