## Definition

**Fuel cells** are [[Voltaic Cell|Voltaic Cells]] that materials ([[Reagent|Reagents]], [[Product|Products]] and [[Electrolytes]]) pass through them, which is simply a converter of [[Chemical Energy]] to [[Electrical Energy]]. These types of [[Battery|Batteries]] can be run indefinitely as long as they are supplied by electrolytes.
