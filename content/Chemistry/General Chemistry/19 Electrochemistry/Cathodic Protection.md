## Definition

**Cathodic protection** is an electrochemical technique to prevent corrosion by making a [[Metal]] surface the [[Cathode]] of a [[Electrochemical Cell|Cell]], forcing it to stop [[Oxidation]]. It protects submerged or buried metallic structures by using sacrificial [[Anode|Anodes]] to feed [[Electron|Electrons]] to the protected structure, ensuring it remains inert.
