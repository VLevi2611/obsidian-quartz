## Definition

Lewis developed a set of symbols for his [[Lewis Theory|Theory]]. A **Lewis symbol** consists of a chemical symbol to represent the [[Nucleus]] and core [[Electron|Electrons]] (inner [[Principal Electronic Shell|Shell]]), together with dots placed around the symbol to represent the [[Valence Electron]]. The Lewis symbol for silicon is
$$
\begin{array}{c}
\cdot \\
\cdot \; \mathrm{Si} \; \cdot \\
\cdot
\end{array}
$$
