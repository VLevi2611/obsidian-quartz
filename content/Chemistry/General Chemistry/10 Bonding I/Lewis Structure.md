## Definition

A **Lewis structure** is a combination of [[Lewis Symbol|Lewis Symbols]] that represents either the transfer or the sharing of [[Electron|Electrons]] in a [[Chemical Bond]].

___
## A Strategy for Writing Lewis Structures

1. Determine the total number of [[Valence Electron|Valence Electrons]] that must appear in the structure.
2. Identify the [[Central Atom|Central Atom(s)]] and [[Terminal Atom|Terminal Atoms]].
3. Write a plausible [[Skeletal Structure]]. Join the [[Atom|Atoms]] by [[Single Covalent Bond|Single Covalent Bonds]].
4. For each bond in the skeletal structure, subtract 2 from the total number of valence electrons.
5. With the valence electrons remaining, first complete the [[Octet|Octets]] of the terminal atoms. Then complete the octets of the central atoms. If there are just enough valence electrons to complete octets for all the atoms, the structure is complete.
6. If one or more central atoms are left with an incomplete octet, move [[Lone Pair]] electrons from one or more terminal atoms to form [[Double Covalent Bond]] or [[Triple Covalent Bond]] to central atoms.
