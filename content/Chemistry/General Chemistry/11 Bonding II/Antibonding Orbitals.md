## Definition

An **antibonding orbital** is a tape of [[Molecular Orbital Theory|Molecular Orbital]] that, when occupied by [[Electron|Electrons]], decreases the stability of a [[Molecule]] and oppose the [[Chemical Bond]] formation. Formed by destructive interference of [[Atomic Orbital|Atomic Orbitals]], these high [[Energy]] orbitals have a [[Node]] between [[Nucleus|Nuclei]], causing increased repulsion.
