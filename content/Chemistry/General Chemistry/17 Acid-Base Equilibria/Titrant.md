## Definition

A **titrant** is a [[Solution]] of precisely known [[Concentration]], added slowly from a burette to a sample ([[Analyte]]) during [[Titration]] to determine the analyte's concentration. It acts as a [[Reagent]] that react quantitatively with the analyte until the [[Chemical Reaction|Reaction's]] [[End Point]] is reached.
