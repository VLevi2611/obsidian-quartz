## Definition

The **reaction quotient, $Q$,** is a measure of the relative amounts of [[Product|Products]] and [[Reagent|Reagents]] present in a [[Chemical Reaction]] at any given time, calculated using the same formula as the [[Equilibrium Constant]], but with instantaneous [[Concentration|Concentrations]]. Comparing $Q$ to $K$ determines the direction a reaction will shift to reach [[Equilibrium]]:
- $Q < K$ proceeds forward forming more products
- $Q > K$ proceeds backward forming more reagents
- $Q = K$ at equilibrium
