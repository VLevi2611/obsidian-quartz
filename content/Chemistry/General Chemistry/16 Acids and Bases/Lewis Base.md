## Definition

A **Lewis base** is a species (an [[Atom]], [[Ion]], or [[Molecule]]) that is an [[Electron]] pair donor.
A [[Chemical Reaction]] between a [[Lewis Acid]] and Lewis base results in the formation of a [[Covalent Bond]] between them.
