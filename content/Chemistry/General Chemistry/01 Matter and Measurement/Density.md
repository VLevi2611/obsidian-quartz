## Definition

**Density, $d$,** is the ratio of [[Mass]] to [[Volume]].

## #formula 

$$d = \frac{m}{V}$$

## Variables
| Symbol |   Meaning   |      Units       |
| :----: | :---------: | :--------------: |
|  $d$   | [[Density]] | $\frac{kg}{m^3}$ |
|  $m$   |  [[Mass]]   |       $kg$       |
|  $V$   | [[Volume]]  |      $m^3$       |
